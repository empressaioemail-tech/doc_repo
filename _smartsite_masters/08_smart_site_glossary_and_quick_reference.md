# Smart Site glossary and quick reference

## Core vocabulary

**Smart Site.** Both the unit and the product, one name on purpose. The unit: any addressable place, a lot, a building, a corner, a stretch of road, a park, with all the information that governs it assembled into one current, cited, usable structure. The product: the application that puts smart sites in your hands, the map, the layers, the parcel answers, the chat, the X-ray. There is no separate brand for the app versus the thing; the product is the unit made accessible. Formerly named Property Explorer; the old name is retired from all market-facing material.

**Smart Site X-ray.** The deep-read report on one smart site: what you can build (envelope drawn), flood and drainage, terrain, codes, utilities, each layer sourced and dated. Say "X-ray," never "dossier," "brief," or "report pack."

**Smart City.** A whole community's worth of smart sites connected into one operating picture. Also the frame behind the SmartCity OS product line sold to cities; that line has its own positioning set and its own rules. Do not pitch cities from Smart Site collateral.

**Buildable envelope.** The computed polygon of what can actually be built on a lot after setbacks are applied, drawn edge by edge, verified before it is shown. A computation, not a lookup.

**Citation / source chip.** The tap-to-open reference attached to an answer showing where a fact came from (the code section, the record, the map panel), how current it is, and the confidence carried.

**Honest decline / "not verified."** When the system does not have verified data for a question, it says so with a reason instead of producing a plausible guess. This is designed behavior and a selling point, not a gap to apologize for.

**Two doors.** The same truth served two ways: a human opens the app; a software agent calls the programmatic interface. Same facts, same citations, either door.

## The spine (memorize this)

One place. All the layers. Always current. Cited. Yours.

## The one-liner

A smart site is everything you need to know about a place, in one place.

## The line that lands

"I'll share your smart site with you."

## Coverage posture

Speak of coverage as nationwide United States. The layer is built jurisdiction by jurisdiction under mechanical certification gates, and the go-to-market assumption is national coverage at launch. Do not enumerate specific counties or cities as "all we have" in market-facing material; if a buyer asks about a specific jurisdiction, the honest answer is that coverage is rolling out nationally and their jurisdiction can be confirmed on request.

## Claims you may make freely

- Everything that governs a place, assembled in one map, current and cited.
- Every answer carries its source, its confidence, and its date.
- The system computes what you can build, and draws it, verified.
- Where data is missing or conditional, the system says so instead of guessing.
- Staleness is detected, not served: repealed codes and superseded parcels are caught.
- Your private data stays yours; public data benefits everyone.
- People and AI agents get the same verified truth through two doors.

## Claims you may never make

- Any cycle-time, savings, percentage, or ROI figure.
- That the system approves, permits, or certifies anything.
- That any specific named competitor is worse (no named competitor attacks).
- Any capability that is planned but not live, presented as live.
- Verbatim licensed code text reproduced in marketing material.

## Words that never lead in market copy

Digital twin. RWA. Tokenization. On-chain. Blockchain. Atom. Node. Graph. Substrate. (These live at internal and technical-diligence altitude only; the technical and substance white papers exist for readers who want that depth.)

## Name hygiene

- The product is **Smart Site**; the report is the **Smart Site X-ray**.
- "Property Explorer" is retired; historical references only.
- Do not name internal systems, engines, or vendors in customer material.
