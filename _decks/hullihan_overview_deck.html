The one-sentence version

What it is

It is a system that gathers facts about things in the world from authoritative sources, attaches to every fact where it came from and how sure it is, connects the things into a network, and earns its confidence over time by checking its past calls against what actually happened.

In your language: it works like an intelligence-analysis operation. It collects from sources, rates each source, produces cited assessments with explicit confidence, keeps chain of custody, and updates as new intelligence arrives. The plain-English version for customers is "a Carfax for any property or asset that shows its sources."

The rule enforced everywhere: no fact ever travels bare. Every unit of knowledge carries six things, and nothing is released without them.

The structure

Nodes, edges, atoms, and why shared facts live once

Nodes are the things. Edges are the relationships. Atoms are the facts a node holds. A shared fact (a building code) is its own node that many things point to, written once, not copied onto each.

Amend the code once on its node, and every parcel sees it through its edge. Node types and edge types are both classified (parcel, code, security, issuer; governed_by, issued_by, would_affect).

The differentiator

Confidence is earned, not asserted

The system does not invent its confidence. It earns it by tracking whether its past claims came true.

Isolation: private outcomes never pool into the shared ledger. Honesty: a number stays labeled "asserted" until it has actually been earned.

Trust

A verifiable, point-in-time track record

Predictions are sealed to a public timestamp layer (no token, no payments) so the record is provable, not just claimed.

flowchart LR PRED["A prediction is made

This is the technical effect that turns "trust our track record" into "verify it independently," and it prevents the system from grading itself with hindsight.

Lineage attribution: if an answer was reasoned from ICC's code, the usage still attributes back to ICC. Metering is built; automatic per-source payout is partly built.

The pattern underneath

One shape, four ledgers

Every loop in the system is the same move: do something with a stated confidence, record what happened, then act on the record.

  • Confidence ledger → re-earns how sure a fact is.
  • Resolution ledger → learns whether predicted events happen.
  • Effect-linkage ledger → learns whether events actually move the subject.
  • Usage ledger → settles payment to the data providers.
Facts, predictions, the product itself, and payments all run on the same feedback shape. That uniformity is part of what makes the system one coherent invention rather than a pile of features.
For patent strategy

The candidate cluster we believe is novel

  • The self-describing atom (claim + provenance + earned confidence + citation + time + access/license), and the rule that nothing leaves bare.
  • Earned-confidence calibration from real outcomes, with isolation and an honesty fallback.
  • Point-in-time correctness (bitemporal, no hindsight) and a verifiable, anchored track record.
  • The temporal-context engine: a curated-source forward layer and shared capture/materiality/calibration that explains the past and anticipates the future.
  • Stable-identity resolution (entities survive renames, splits, merges) and lineage-aware rights that travel with derived results.
  • One method, two embodiments (real estate and financial markets), evidence the invention is general, not tied to one field.

What we need from counsel

Three asks

  • A target provisional filing date, because anything patentable must be reduced to practice before any public launch (a launch can become prior art against our own filing).
  • Confirm the structure: the method is owned by Hauska Inc., each application (real estate, the financial embodiment) is a licensed embodiment; two embodiments strengthen the filing.
  • Guidance on protectable marks (coined or suggestive, not descriptive), including whether "atom" as we use it, or a coined name for the calibration system, is worth claiming.
