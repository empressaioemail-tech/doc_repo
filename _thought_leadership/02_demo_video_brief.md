# The video: "Watch an AI agent buy the truth"

## The shape

**Two minutes. Screen-real, text-forward, captions-first** (built for sound-off feeds, Raoul-style pacing). One continuous demonstration, no talking heads in v1: a real AI agent answers a real-world question by purchasing verified facts through our gate, on camera. **Split screen**: left, the agent's conversation and reasoning; right, "the meter" - a dressed view of the gate showing what machines actually see (request in, auth, metering tick, atoms out with provenance, royalty accrual to the source). The viewer watches both sides of a machine-to-machine transaction about physical reality.

Three cuts from one shoot: **2:00 hero** (the manifesto's companion), **0:30 social** (beats 1, 3-peak, 5), **6:00 institutional** (adds the Bulls economy as act two: the same architecture with humans in it - fan claims account, athlete records a gate-timed sprint, ledger splits at settlement).

## Assembly list (what must exist before recording)

1. **The meter view**: a presentable single-page overlay of the gate's live telemetry (request log, metering tick, provenance payload, obligation accrual). The command center's gate inspector and metering summary are the ingredients; this is a dressing pass, not a build.
2. **The scripted scenario**: one Texas parcel chosen for a clean story (zoned, flooded corner, known owner) plus one deliberate unknown for the honesty beat. Agent prompt scripted but genuinely executed - nothing staged, per the folder principle.
3. **A demo key** with visible metering and the ICC-pattern accrual path active for the royalty beat.
4. Screen capture at feed-friendly resolution, captions burned in; Bulls design-system typography NOT used here - this is Empressa-neutral (the design language of the simple diagram: near-black, one accent).
5. **v2 gate**: the end-to-end machine-payment loop (x402 challenge → USDC settlement → verified receipt) assembled and tested; the video's strongest possible beat, added only when real. Do not fake it in v1.

## Honesty check (standing)

Every on-screen claim traces to live state at record time: "11 million properties" to the parcel-node count then-current; "metered/billed" to the live gate; "source gets paid" to the live obligation meter. The video is re-cut, not re-claimed, if numbers move.
