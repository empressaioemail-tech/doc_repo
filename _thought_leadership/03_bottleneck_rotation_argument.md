# The bottleneck rotation, and why it lands on us

## The rotation so far

1. **Compute** was the bottleneck between the world and machine intelligence. Capital flooded it. (The Nvidia era.)
2. **Energy** is the bottleneck now - intelligence per unit of energy - and capital is rotating to power exactly as the theory predicts.
3. Each solved bottleneck does not end the sequence; it lights the next one. Solve compute and energy, and intelligence becomes abundant and cheap. Rails already make payment instant. So the economy arrives at: **agents that can think at machine speed and pay at machine speed, acting on a world they cannot verify.**

## The next bottleneck

Abundant intelligence plus instant settlement plus unverified reality equals fraud, hallucination, and mispricing at machine speed. The constraint on the agentic economy stops being "can the agent think" or "can the agent pay" and becomes **"what can the agent safely act on."** Trustworthy, machine-readable, continuously-attested reality - the referent layer, ground truth, the underlying - is what stands between the world and where it is trying to go.

The bottleneck mechanics are textbook: demand for verified referents scales with every transaction the solved layers enable (each one needs a referent), while the supply of verified reality is nearly fixed - because almost nobody is building it. Demand multiplying against static supply is the definition of the next rotation.

## Why this bottleneck is different - and why early matters more here

The power bottleneck is capital-intensive: money builds power plants, so the capital flood solves it on capital's timescale. **The verification bottleneck cannot be solved with money alone.** Provenance accumulates; calibration is earned against outcomes; coverage is crawled, verified, and re-verified over years; trust compounds with track record. A competitor with ten times the capital cannot buy a decade of evidence chains. Time is the scarce input - which means the moat belongs to whoever is standing at the bottleneck when the rotation arrives, and it cannot be flash-flooded away afterward.

We have been building at this bottleneck for years, three implementations deep, before the rotation had a name.

## The one-breath version

Capital chases bottlenecks. Compute was first; power is now; and when intelligence is abundant and payment is instant, the last thing standing between agents and the real economy is knowing what anything actually is. That bottleneck is verification - and unlike power plants, ground truth can't be bought, only built over time. We started early on purpose.
