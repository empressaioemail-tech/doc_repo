# Red Sands Global (Garrett) — prospect digest

Meeting 2026-07-08 (intro via Herbert's network; two transcripts taken in-chat, dissected same session). Filed 2026-08-10 at ideation close. **The raw transcripts are NOT yet in this repo; the operator holds them. Filing them under `assets/transcripts/` is an open item.** Everything below sourced from those transcripts; facts about TradeX and Red Sands are Garrett's statements unless marked independently verified (none are yet).

## Who

Garrett runs a physical fuel intermediary business through Red Sands Global (services span oil and gas plus a carbon arm). Main supplier: TradeX Fuel Exchange Inc. (his description: the only publicly traded online gas/fuel exchange; every deal registered with the SEC; buying five refineries and five tank farms). Deal flow: back-to-back fuel deals to his vetted buyers (Singapore buyer mentioned), commissions split per deal via paymaster attorneys (IOLTA accounts). Adjacent connection: partner's son ("Ron Williams + son") building a Claude-based document/AI project into which they dump deal documents (ICPOs, TSAs, CSRs, shipping docs, real and fake tank-farm packages).

## The operating condition of his business: fraud

Of 58 tank farms he physically visited, 12 passed his test. Fake terminal traced to operators in Moscow; a fake company (CWL, Houston) with a nonexistent address; tier-2 farms (renting tanks from tier-1 operators) are the fraud zone. His verification method: TCEQ environmental certificate (kill test), RRC, Secretary of State, D&B, the Port of Rotterdam list plus the TCC enhanced-due-diligence group, physical site visits, calling SGS to verify report numbers. He maintains an 80-item tank-farm approval checklist. He pays for what others will not (SGS reports, ships, tank reservations; $160k for a three-day tank reservation; insures deals on his own tank-farm choice), which is his edge.

## What he asked for (unprompted)

1. **The registry, as a paid service:** "build this list and make it a pay service... I pay for it every month" (registered buyers, sellers, tank farms, shippers, paymasters).
2. **Deal workflow:** "if you build a trade desk that allowed us to take the fuel from TradeX and then take it to my vetted buyers... nothing stopping us from closing multiple deals a week." Bottleneck is workflow, not supply or buyers: one close a quarter historically vs the 2-2.5/week TradeX can feed.
3. **Document generation:** LOIs, ICPOs, procedures per methodology (TradeX / Exxon refinery-direct / reseller: his three categories).

His commission-split document ($1.50/bbl to connectors, $1 to the TradeX contact, $4.50 to himself, handed to a paymaster) was recognized live as an override interest: same object as a division order (feeds ADR-025).
