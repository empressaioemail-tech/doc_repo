# Vertosoft — government GTM channel and refined offer

> **What this is.** First in-repo record of Vertosoft. Source: the 2026-06-07 meeting (Nick + Val + Vertosoft rep), transcript on file. We are moving forward; distribution agreement pending. Captures who they are, the deal shape, and the refined product offer + operator decisions.

## What Vertosoft is

A public-sector technology distributor and reseller. Their value to us is twofold: contract-vehicle access (the cooperative-purchasing on-ramps municipalities buy through without a full RFP) and a channel sales motion into government. They are also a strategic AWS partner.

- **Contract vehicles:** broad Texas coverage (Omnia, Sourcewell, GSA, TIPS) plus national cooperatives (NASPO, Omnia, Sourcewell, TIPS) and state-specific contracts (Virginia, Massachusetts, California). Thousands of downstream partners to cover any gap or special designation.
- **Business model:** markup only. They take a cost from us, mark it up a few points, sell to the government. No charge to get added to contract vehicles; they carry the cost of getting us on vehicles, manage the contracts, keep prices updated, pay fees, and do the reporting. They make money on the per-transaction markup.
- **Sells use-case-first:** the gov buyer cares about the problem solved, not the tech. They will help translate the Bastrop deployment into a problem statement and use case, and train their sales team to position it.

## Deal shape (from the meeting)

- **Distribution agreement** = core agreement + an optional professional-services addendum (only if we do time-and-materials/hourly billing) + two AWS addendums (AWS payer-account management + the AWS accelerator/marketplace-certification program).
- **Onboarding after signing:** partner onboarding forms (finance, marketing, sales, contracts), a contracts-team call to prioritize which vehicles to pursue first, then a sales kickoff so their team understands and can position the solution.
- **Our contact to them:** the gentleman being onboarded with city-management / plan-review background acts as the sales-rep contact.
- **Prophecy AI** connection noted: Bastrop uses Prophecy (part of our dashboard via integration), and Prophecy is also in the Vertosoft orbit — that adjacency is how Vertosoft surfaced.

## Refined offer — SUPERSEDED 2026-08-07, see current offer below

> The section as written 2026-06-07 described a four-surface line: dashboard, plan review (Codex), parcel/property intelligence, and the ambient capture extension. That framing predates the SmartCity category masters (ratified 2026-08-01, `_smartcity_masters/`) and must not be given to Vertosoft. Codex is retired as a name. Parcel intelligence is not a separate SKU; it is a city's access to its smart sites. Ambient capture is never sold. Kept for the historical record only.

## Current offer (reconciled to the SmartCity masters, 2026-08-07)

Vertosoft resells **SmartCity OS**, defined by the category masters in `_smartcity_masters/`: three categories on one foundation. **Dashboards** (the entry sale: a complete, deployable system, live in Bastrop), **Plan Review** (submittals pre-reviewed against the city's own adopted code; demo owed), and **Asset Management** (the city's physical reality as a durable, access-controlled record; delivered as a build). **Smart Files** is the customer-facing face of the foundation; the foundation itself is never named externally. Sold system-first: Dashboards is never described as phase one, and the program conversation (Plan Review, Asset Management) happens after deployment or when the city asks what comes next.

Sold as an annual per-city subscription tiered by city size, plus onboarding professional services; the government list price remains the open operator decision that gates the launch. The sales narrative is the Bastrop use case ([`06_cities_value_narrative`](../../06_cities_value_narrative.md)) told inside the three-beat pitch of `_smartcity_masters/35_smartcity_positioning_framework.md`: pain first, the three categories as verbs, then their city concretely. This remains a Layer 3 integrated-product sale to cities, distinct from the Layer 1/2 agent-builder substrate model.

**What Vertosoft's reps receive.** The one-pager set specified in `_smartcity_masters/onepager_briefs/` plus the never-say list, because a channel repeats what it is given. Reps get only register-approved claims: no cycle-time or savings figures, no competitor names, no claim the system approves or permits, no licensed code text in any demo before the ICC SaaS agreement is signed (the city's own adopted code is the safe demo material).

**Unplaced SKU question.** The county-records addition floated 2026-07-27 (`_inbox/2026-07-27_bastrop_infra_twinning_revival_and_vertosoft_three_products.md`) is not placed within the four-category structure. It needs a placement ruling before it is pitched to Vertosoft as a separate product; until then it is not part of the offer.

## Operator decisions to lock (status as of 2026-08-07)

1. **Government pricing model and list price. STILL OPEN, and now the launch gate.** Vertosoft needs a clean list price to mark up. Per-city annual subscription tiered by population/size, plus onboarding PS. The internal unit economics (under $200 compute + 1 hour human review per jurisdiction) are the cost floor; the gov list price is the external number. Every pricing-bearing artifact in the masters and the one-pager set waits on this.
2. **AWS vs GCP. Recommendation set 2026-08-07: sign the core agreement and vehicles now, defer the two AWS addendums.** Vertosoft's storefront is an AWS Marketplace private marketplace (`_inbox/2026-07-28_vertosoft_govcloud_competitive_scan.md`); our verified stack is GCP Cloud Run + Neon. The contract-vehicle and reseller value is cloud-agnostic and is the real fit for the city buyer, who purchases through cooperatives rather than cloud-commit drawdown. Two questions carried into the redline: what share of Vertosoft's municipal (not state-agency) volume actually transacts through the AWS storefront versus the vehicles, and whether a GCP-hosted SaaS can list under current AWS Marketplace policy. Revisit migration only if storefront volume for city-sized buyers proves real.
3. **Contract-vehicle priority.** Which vehicles first (TX-focused: TIPS, Omnia, Sourcewell, BuyBoard?) given the Central Texas -> Dallas -> Houston rollout. The competitive scan flags Texas DIR as the highest-leverage rail on their storefront.
4. **SKU scope. RESOLVED by the masters (2026-08-01).** One product line: SmartCity OS with Dashboards as the entry system, Plan Review and Asset Management as what a city grows into, Smart Files as the foundation's face. No Codex SKU (name retired), no parcel-intelligence SKU (demoted to smart-site access), no ambient-capture SKU (never sold). The county-records addition awaits a placement ruling before it can be carried.
5. **Sales-rep assignment** confirmed (the city-management onboardee as the Vertosoft contact).

## Thesis check (corrected 2026-08-07)

Clean GTM channel, no conflict. Vertosoft is procurement and channel, not a data aggregator. It is the Layer 3 product-to-cities motion, additive to the substrate model. The discipline note stands: sell the use case, keep the substrate plumbing out of the gov pitch, per Vertosoft's own advice and the two-altitude rule.
