# ATX Bulls program overview

## Counterparty and facts

Cody (owner, ATX Bulls; surname to be recorded). Arena football team in Arena Football One, Austin TX. Inaugural season 2027; opening night announced for March 20, 2027. Team announced early August 2026. Preseason training begins late August 2026.

Verified from atxbulls.com 2026-08-13: VIP email list live, ticket reservation interest capture live, merch Drop 001 live (blackout apparel line), 2027 uniform reveal published. Site is a promotional landing page; no ownership, stadium, or tryout detail published.

Reported by Cody in the 2026-08-13 meeting (unverified by us): roughly 2,000 inbound contacts in under two weeks since announcement, arriving through social media and the VIP signup page.

First engagement already in motion: athlete twinning. Cody received the Smart Athlete A1 season tracking protocol (PDF delivered 2026-08-13) and has agreed in principle to recorded agility testing and verified athlete twins. See `02_athlete_twin_program.md`.

## The five workstreams

1. **Fan monetization, now.** Convert the current inbound wave into revenue before the hype decays. Token-shaped instruments are one option among several; the fastest proven instruments (deposits, founding memberships, merch) come first. `01_fan_monetization_now.md`.
2. **Athlete twin program.** A1 protocol across the roster for the 2026-2027 preseason and season. The verified-athlete substrate also feeds fan products (verified player cards, combine content). `02_athlete_twin_program.md`.
3. **Team tokenization.** A true financial instrument on the team entity. This is a securities offering in every realistic form and moves at counsel speed, not marketing speed. `03_team_tokenization.md`.
4. **Stadium: twin and tokenization.** Digital twin from land acquisition through construction through operations, and a tokenized cash-flow instrument on the completed asset. The twin doubles as the feasibility and disclosure layer for the raise. `04_stadium_twin_and_tokenization.md`.
5. **End-to-end technology control.** We run the stack from lead capture through twinning through passes and payments through tokenization-platform integration. What runs on which piece of our stack, and where we integrate instead of build. `05_technology_control_plan.md`.

## Our role boundary (standing posture, restated for this engagement)

We are the technology and referent layer. Cody's entities are the issuer of anything security-shaped. We build no token layer and we custody no investor funds; we provide the twin, the verified data, the disclosure surface, the fan capture and membership rails, and the integration to a regulated tokenization platform when that stage arrives. Securities structuring, exemption selection, and offering documents are counsel work routed through Cody's attorneys. This division is not a limitation; it is the position with the durable moat and none of the liability we are not licensed to carry.

## Sequencing logic

Money flows earliest from workstream 1 and it funds patience for 3 and 4. Workstream 2 starts on the preseason clock (late August 2026) regardless of everything else. Workstream 3 has no timeline pressure and should not be allowed to invent one; it becomes real when the team has a season of financials and counsel has picked an exemption path. Workstream 4 begins as a twin immediately when siting work starts (that part needs no permission from anyone) and becomes a tokenization only after the prerequisite gate in `04` is fully green.

The unifying asset across all five is the fan and athlete graph we start capturing in workstream 1: every later product sells into the audience workstream 1 registers.
