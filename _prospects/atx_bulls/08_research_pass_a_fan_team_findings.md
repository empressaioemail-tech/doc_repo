# Pass A findings: fan monetization and team tokenization

Researched 2026-08-13. Tags: FACT / REPORTED / INFERENCE as in Pass B.

## The regulatory backdrop (frames everything)

1. **March 17, 2026: joint SEC-CFTC interpretive guidance** on crypto assets, with a five-category taxonomy. Digital collectibles and digital tools (utility tokens) are generally NOT securities when purchasers would not expect profit from the managerial efforts of others; tokenized equity or debt is always a security regardless of wrapper. **The guidance explicitly names fan tokens (citing Socios) as examples of non-security collectibles and tools** (FACT, Ballard Spahr / WilmerHale / Morgan Lewis alerts; Chiliz statement). Critical caveat: marketing, roadmaps, and communications that create profit expectations can flip a non-security asset back into an investment contract. The analysis is conduct-driven, not form-driven.
2. **The CLARITY Act has NOT passed** as of 2026-08-13; Senate floor process resumes after September 14, 2026 (FACT, CoinDesk). The friendly posture rests on interpretive guidance and enforcement priorities, not statute. A marketing misstep or a future administration can re-securitize a utility token.
3. Enforcement climate: crypto enforcement actions fell to 13 in FY2025 from 33 in FY2024 (REPORTED); the SEC NFT enforcement wave was wound down with investigations closed without charges (OpenSea Feb 2025, Yuga March 2025) (FACT). Howey remains binding law.
4. **Timing fact for this engagement**: US-issued fan tokens only became viable in roughly March 2026; Socios launched its first US fan tokens in 2026 through a Playfly Sports college-athletics deal (LSU, Maryland, Michigan State, Penn State, Texas A&M) (FACT/REPORTED). No US pro team has a mature fan-token P&L yet. The Bulls would be entering in the first viable year, ahead of every US pro-team incumbent.

## Fan-token landscape health

Socios/Chiliz: about 70 partner organizations; claims over $700M cumulative revenue delivered to sports partners (REPORTED); first sports platform authorized under EU MiCA (Sept 2025). But token prices are weak even as the business model expands: CHZ fell roughly 47 percent in a 30-day window June 2026 and World Cup fan tokens sold off through the group stage (REPORTED). INFERENCE: the durable revenue is team-side engagement and licensing revenue, not token appreciation. This supports the doc 01 design (consumptive pass, chainless v1, perks-led).

## The cautionary tale directly on point: BIG3

BIG3 sold "Team Ownership NFTs" in 2022 (25 Fire at $25K plus 975 Gold at $5K per team) marketed with the word ownership and a promised 40 percent of net proceeds of any future team sale, while the terms of sale expressly conferred no equity, only an IP license and perks. In 2025-2026 buyers filed a class action alleging benefits stripped and four franchises sold for roughly $40M without distributing proceeds (FACT for the offering terms; REPORTED for the suit's allegations). Buyers included Krause House DAO, Snoop Dogg, Gary Vaynerchuk. **This is the exact failure mode the doc 01 approved-claims line exists to prevent: ownership-flavored marketing without equity is private-plaintiff bait independent of SEC posture.**

## Membership and pre-season-one monetization precedents (with numbers)

1. **Seat deposits are the standard instrument.** Golden State Valkyries (WNBA expansion, first season 2025): $25 non-refundable deposits for waitlist priority, passed 15,000 then 20,000+ (FACT). Minor-league scale: Spartanburg MiLB took $500 suite reservations ahead of its inaugural season (FACT).
2. **WAGMI United / Crawley Town**: 3M+ pounds in hours from about 9,000 NFTs at about 355 pounds each (2022), conferring voting, merch, experiences. The closest founding-member-NFT precedent, and its aftermath (crypto de-emphasis, community fade) argues for perks-led rather than crypto-led framing (FACT/REPORTED).
3. **Oakland Ballers (independent Pioneer League, launched 2024): the on-point precedent for a startup team monetizing launch hype with community equity.** Reg CF raise: $1M in 48 hours, $3M+ total from 2,500+ investors at $170 per share on a roughly $25-26M valuation, with voting on select matters (FACT, DealMaker). Sister precedents: Chattanooga FC $872,750 from 3,256 investors (2019); Detroit City FC up to $1.2M for about 10 percent (2020-21); Oakland Roots $3,648,437 from 6,300+ investors closing Feb 2025 (FACT).
4. Green Bay Packers: unique grandfathered nonprofit structure, not replicable; the 2021-22 sale ($65.8M, 176,160 buyers) is donation-with-a-certificate mechanics run on DealMaker (FACT).
5. No completed US tokenized team equity offering found through 2026; Watford (UK, Republic) is the nearest live tokenized-equity precedent and it undersubscribed badly (about 20 percent of target). US team raises to date are conventional non-tokenized Reg CF shares.

## Securities paths as practiced (deltas from the doc 03 sketch)

1. **Reg CF**: $5M cap per rolling 12 months, FINRA portal, Form C, one-year resale restriction, annual Form C-AR. Portal fees roughly 5-10 percent plus legal and CPA costs in the tens of thousands. **Tokenized Reg CF shares are permitted** (transfer agent handles tokenization; post-restriction ATS trading possible). **Revenue-share notes are natively supported** (Wefunder standard template; typical shape a fixed percent of revenues to a capped multiple) (FACT).
2. **Reg A+ Tier 2**: $75M per 12 months, SEC qualification, two years audited financials, ongoing reporting, retail-open with limits; practitioner cost norms low-to-mid six figures and months of process (FACT/REPORTED).
3. **Reg D 506(c)**: accredited-only, general solicitation, no cap; verification burden materially eased by the March 12, 2025 SEC no-action letter (minimum-investment self-certification at $200K individual / $1M entity) (FACT).
4. Platforms active 2026: tZERO (CF through A+ tokenized offerings plus ATS), Securitize (vertically integrated; NYSE MOU), Texture Capital (broker-dealer plus ATS; handles revenue share and royalties; 2026 partnership with sports-club tokenizer T-RIZE), and the retail rails Wefunder, StartEngine, Republic, DealMaker (DealMaker ran both the Packers sale and the Ballers raise) (FACT).

## The sequencing revision this forces

The doc 03 working assumption was that a retail-facing raise needs an operating history (season one) first. **The Ballers precedent contradicts that**: a well-run community equity round timed to the launch-hype window, pre-season-one, raised $3M+ from the exact audience heat the Bulls have right now. The revised view: a small community round (Reg CF class) is viable in the hype window if and only if the league consents and counsel is comfortable with pre-revenue disclosure; the larger, tokenized, or Reg A+ shaped raise still wants operating history. Doc 03 updated accordingly.

## League constraint: honest gap

No published Arena Football One ownership, transfer, or fractional-ownership rules could be found. AF1 formed Sept 6, 2024 (eight teams leaving the AFL, with Jeff Fisher and Jerry Kurz). INFERENCE: the Bulls' franchise agreement is the controlling document and only Cody can surface it. **Gating diligence item for workstream 3: read the franchise agreement's consent and transfer provisions before anything is designed, let alone marketed.**

2. Whether a founding-member product with resale capability drifts toward investment-contract territory; BIG3 class action as the live private-plaintiff precedent.

4. Seat-deposit escrow and refundability treatment under Texas consumer law (adjacent to, not within, securities law).
