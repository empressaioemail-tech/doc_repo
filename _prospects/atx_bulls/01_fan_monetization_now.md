# Fan monetization, now

## The asset

Roughly 2,000 self-identified fans in under two weeks, seven months before the first kickoff (March 20, 2027). This is pre-revenue demand with no product to buy except merch. The wave decays if unconverted; the first job is registration and capture (every contact into one fan graph we control), the second is conversion.

## The instrument ladder, fastest and safest first

**Rung 1: capture and identity.** One signup flow we run (see `05_technology_control_plan.md`), replacing scattered social DMs and the bare VIP email list. Every fan becomes a record with consent and contact provenance. Costs nothing to the fan; this is the substrate for every rung below.

**Rung 2: proven money, no novelty.** Seat deposits (refundable or credit-toward-tickets), founding season-ticket priority, merch (Drop 001 already exists), tryout registration fees if tryouts are public-entry. These are boring, legal, immediate, and every minor-league launch does them. Nothing token-shaped is required to take this money.

**Rung 3: the Founding Member pass (digital membership).** A paid digital membership with named perks: priority seat selection, founding-member recognition (permanent, numbered), exclusive content from the athlete twin program (verified combine numbers, testing-day video), merch discounts, vote-on-trivial-matters engagement mechanics (jersey detail, anthem singer). Mechanically this is our access-pass pattern: a numbered, revocable, perk-carrying pass in the fan's account, payable by card through standard checkout. It can be presented with token-like collectibility (numbered, limited, tradeable later if ever appropriate) without being issued on a chain at all in v1.

**Rung 4 (optional, later): collectible layer.** Player moments, verified-stat cards backed by the athlete twin data (a card carrying an A1-verified 4.4 with its evidence chain is a genuinely differentiated collectible). Only after rungs 1 through 3 are running.

## The securities line, held explicitly

The Founding Member pass and anything else sold in this workstream must be consumptive: the fan is buying access, perks, recognition, and content. No profit language, no revenue share, no appreciation talk, no "get in early on the team" framing in any sales copy. The moment an instrument is marketed with profit expectation from the efforts of others it walks into Howey territory and belongs in workstream 3 under counsel. Marketing copy for this workstream gets reviewed against that line before publication. This is the same discipline as our claims registers elsewhere: an approved-claims list for fan products, maintained in this folder once copy exists.

Fan-token precedents and the current regulatory posture are being researched (dispatched 2026-08-13); findings land in `06_research_agenda.md` and may adjust the rung 3 and 4 design. Counsel review of the pass terms is required before money is taken on rung 3 regardless.

## What we run

Capture, fan graph, pass issuance and management, payments (standard card checkout; Circle-backed rails are available in our stack when useful), content delivery from the twin program, and the approved-claims discipline. Cody's side owns pricing, perks policy, and fulfillment of physical benefits.
