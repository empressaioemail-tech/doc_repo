# Team tokenization

## The honest frame

Any instrument that gives a fan or investor an economic interest in the team (equity, revenue share, profit participation) is a securities offering in every realistic US form. There is no token structure that changes this; a token here is a wrapper on a security, not an escape from one. The value of doing it as a token is distribution, cap-table mechanics, and eventually transferability, not regulatory avoidance. This workstream therefore moves at the speed of entity formation, financials, and counsel, and its marketing must stay completely separated from workstream 1 fan products (which are consumptive and must never carry profit language).

## Working sketch of the paths (to be confirmed by research and counsel)

Three exemption families exist for a raise like this: Reg CF (community round through a funding portal, capped, cheapest, retail-inclusive), Reg A+ Tier 2 (larger retail raise, SEC qualification, audited financials, ongoing reporting), and Reg D 506(c) (accredited investors only, fastest, no retail community angle). Tokenized shares or units are permitted under all three when done through the right infrastructure (transfer agent, and an ATS if secondary trading is ever offered). Precedents exist for community ownership of teams (the Packers model as spiritual ancestor; recent minor-league crowdfunding raises as practical ones) and are being compiled by the research pass dispatched 2026-08-13. League-level ownership rules for Arena Football One are unknown and must be checked with the league before anything is marketed.

The realistic sequencing gate, revised against the Pass A findings (`08_research_pass_a_fan_team_findings.md`): the Oakland Ballers precedent ($1M in 48 hours, $3M+ total via Reg CF, raised pre-season-one on launch hype) shows a small community round is viable inside the hype window, before any operating history, if the league consents and counsel accepts pre-revenue disclosure. The larger, tokenized, or Reg A+ shaped raise still wants season-one financials. A 506(c) to accredited investors can move on its own clock. Exemption selection is a counsel decision informed by how much Cody wants the community-ownership story versus speed and size.

**Franchise-agreement update (2026-08-13, `09_af1_purchase_agreement_analysis.md`): the AF1 Purchase Agreement prohibits any ownership interest in the team entity, or any entity up its chain, from being publicly traded (3.1(a)), and requires every direct or indirect individual owner to be listed and to sign a Letter of Investment Intent (5.4, 5.5). As drafted this takes ATS-tradeable tokenized equity off the table and makes a large equity crowd operationally incompatible. The candidate community instrument shifts to a revenue-share note (debt, not an ownership interest; natively supported at Reg CF), with League written consent as the prudent posture regardless. Team pro formas must exclude league revenue share until after the second completed season and reflect assessment and capital-call obligations. Four underlying League Agreements remain unread and may carry further restrictions.**

## What the community-ownership story is actually worth

The strategic reason to do this at all, rather than raise privately: 2,000 inbound fans in two weeks is evidence of a community that may want to own a piece of its team. A community raise converts fans into evangelist-owners and is itself marketing. That story only works retail (CF or A+), which is the slower, more disclosure-heavy path. This tradeoff is Cody's call once counsel prices both routes.

## Our role

The disclosure and data layer, and the fan-graph rail. Concretely: the team's operating record presented as a verifiable data room (financial documents, attendance, the athlete program's verified assets) for whichever offering path counsel selects; investor-facing continuous disclosure after the raise; integration to the chosen regulated platform (funding portal or tokenization platform); and the fan graph from workstream 1 as the distribution list a retail raise is marketed to, within solicitation rules counsel sets. We do not structure, issue, custody, or sell.

## Blocked on
