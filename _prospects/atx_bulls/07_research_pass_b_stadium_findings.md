# Pass B findings: stadium and construction tokenization

Researched 2026-08-13 via web sources. Tags: FACT (established), REPORTED (claimed by sources, single-sourced or announcement-stage), INFERENCE (our synthesis).

## Headline findings

1. **No tokenized stadium or arena deal has been completed anywhere, US or international, as of August 2026** (INFERENCE from exhaustive search). A tokenized Austin arena would be first-of-kind: no disclosure templates, no valuation comps, novelty press value, elevated regulatory attention.
2. **Construction-phase tokenization is done as debt or revenue-participation instruments in practice, not equity.** Closest comp: World Liberty Financial + DarGlobal + Securitize tokenizing loan revenue interests in the Trump International Hotel and Resort Maldives (announced Feb 2026, completion 2030, accredited-only, fixed yield plus revenue participation) (FACT, BusinessWire/PRNewswire). Equity tokens on unbuilt assets are rare because there is nothing to distribute until stabilization.
3. **Secondary liquidity is demonstrably near zero even for the flagship deal.** St. Regis Aspen (Aspen Coin, $18M raised 2018, tZERO ATS since 2020): last trade May 28, 2026 at roughly issue price, 24-hour volume zero (FACT, STOmarket trading data). Any investor materials implying a liquid exit are a misrepresentation risk.
4. **Arena football economics are tenant economics.** Teams at this tier lease existing municipal arenas; a team-owner-built arena is off-pattern for the league. Roughly 4 in 10 US stadiums over two decades used tax-exempt municipal bonds (FACT, Journalist's Resource); current minor-league examples run $250M to $350M municipal packages (Mobile AL, Macon-Bibb GA).
5. **Competitive fact for the metro: a second Austin-area indoor football team exists.** ATX Draft (Indoor Football League, owner Vivi Lin), also launching spring 2027, reported to be seriously considering leasing the H-E-B Center at Cedar Park (8,700 seats) (REPORTED, Community Impact 2026-07-14). Austin's prior indoor teams (Wranglers, Wild) folded.
6. **Team facts verified**: ATX Bulls is an Arena Football One expansion team for 2027; owner and president Cody Trapp, co-owner Shawna Ciano (FACT, AF1 announcement). The announcement contains no venue commitment. No stadium plan, land purchase, or arena development is publicly reported.

## Precedents and failures

**Cash-flow tokenization precedents.** St. Regis Aspen ($18M, Reg D, 2018; the liquidity lesson above). Lofty: last US retail blockchain-native fractional RE platform standing, roughly $1.7M rent distributed platform-wide in 2025, small scale (REPORTED). RedSwan listed a $170M hospitality portfolio and an $89.4M multifamily portfolio Nov 2025 (REPORTED). T-RIZE signed a $300M deal Dec 2024 to tokenize a 960-unit ground-up Quebec development; announcement-tier, no completion evidence found (REPORTED). Tokenized RE market context: over $10B in 2025, RWA market over $32B ex-stablecoins in 2026, GENIUS Act (stablecoins) passed July 2025 (REPORTED).

**Failures worth knowing.** RealT: voluntary liquidation July 2026 after Detroit tax and blight litigation, suspended rent distributions, court-appointed fiduciary; the failure was at the property-management layer, not the blockchain layer (REPORTED, multiple consistent sources). Prodigy Network: $690M crowdfunded from 6,500 investors for ground-up NYC and Chicago projects, bankruptcy March 2021, fraud suits, foreclosures (FACT). Lesson set: single-sponsor governance risk, construction overruns, and retail marketing all amplify when the asset does not exist yet, and tokenization does not outsource asset operation.

**Adjacent-but-not-venue.** Watford FC via Republic (2024-2025): about 10 percent of the club (not the stadium) as digital equity at a 175M pound valuation targeting 17.5M pounds; only about 3.6M pounds raised from 2,245 investors by April 2025, roughly 20 percent of target (FACT/REPORTED). Undersubscription is itself a data point on retail appetite. tZERO athlete-tokenization partnerships exist (team and athlete economics, not venues). A San Diego Bitcoin-backed NFL venue concept is podcast-stage only.

**Fan-funded construction precedents (the realistic scale).** Green Bay Packers 2021-2022 stock sale: about $65.8M net from 176,160 buyers at $300, but the instrument pays no dividends and does not appreciate; donation-with-a-certificate, run on DealMaker (FACT). Buffalo Bills: county municipal bonds marketed to fans (REPORTED). Tifosy (UK, FCA-regulated): true stadium-construction fan finance as mini-bonds, Stevenage FC 600K pounds in six weeks for a stand, Frosinone 1.5M euro, Pescara 3M euro (FACT). Scale observation: fan raises deliver hundreds of thousands to low millions per campaign. A slice, never the building.

## Platform landscape 2026

Securitize: institutional leader (BlackRock BUIDL manager), SEC-registered transfer agent plus broker-dealer plus ATS, NYSE MOU and Computershare partnership 2026; will do single-asset construction deals with a strong brand attached (the Maldives deal). tZERO: broker-dealer plus ATS plus digital custody, secondary-trading APIs late 2025. Note: Securitize and tZERO are in active patent litigation with each other (filed circa June 2026, Delaware), a live vendor-risk item on both (REPORTED, CoinDesk). RedSwan CRE (Houston): CRE-specific, FINRA member, actively soliciting sponsors nationally, Reg D accredited-only; geographically closest and most sponsor-friendly to a Texas mid-size deal. Texture Capital: active but low-visibility. Blocksquare: EU-oriented. What a sponsor brings to a platform: formed issuer entity, exemption chosen with counsel, offering documents, diligence package; the platform supplies KYC/AML, accreditation verification, whitelist-enforced transfer restrictions, transfer-agent recordkeeping, optional ATS listing.

## Structures and exemptions as practiced

Standard structure: SPV LLC holds the asset, tokens are LLC membership units or feeder interests, ordinary securities exemptions apply. Reg D 506(c): unlimited amount, general solicitation, accredited-verified only; dominant for tokenized RE. Reg A+ Tier 2: up to $75M per 12 months, retail allowed, SEC qualification plus audited financials, roughly $50-100K+ upfront and months of process; the only path to true fans-invest-small-amounts. Reg CF: up to $5M per 12 months via FINRA portal; too small alone for an arena but usable as a community tranche.

## Venue cost reality

Benchmark $15K to $55K per seat. Credit Union of Texas Event Center, Allen TX (6,275 seats): $52.6M, roughly $78.9M in 2025 dollars. Wilson NC 4,500-seat stadium: $70M. Realistic planning band for a 5,000-seat US arena: $60M to $150M depending on spec (FACT/INFERENCE from examples).

## Consequences for the doc 04 gate (applied)

The gate in `04_stadium_twin_and_tokenization.md` survives contact with the research and gains three sharpenings: the tokenized tranche should be presumed **debt or revenue-participation shaped** until counsel says otherwise (matches every real construction precedent); the **GMP contract with a bonded contractor** is the single item that converts concept to financeable; and the **intercreditor question** (a senior lender papering against a tokenized junior tranche) is novel enough that few lenders have done it, which makes lender selection part of the critical path, not an afterthought.

## Counsel routing (adds to doc 06)

Securities counsel: exemption selection; debt-versus-equity token structure; secondary-liquidity language discipline (the Aspen zero-volume fact makes overstatement a liability); Securitize-tZERO patent litigation as vendor risk; whether any team-revenue linkage makes the arena token a more complex instrument. Real estate counsel: site control, entitlements path (city versus ETJ versus county), GMP and bonding, senior-lender intercreditor terms against a tokenized junior tranche, appraisal engagement.
