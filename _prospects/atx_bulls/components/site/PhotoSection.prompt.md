# PhotoSection
Every marketing section on the site.

```jsx
<PhotoSection image="https://atxbulls.com/family-arena-2027.png" overlay={<Stamp lines={["ARE","YOU","READY?"]} style={{position:"absolute",right:80,top:120}} />}>
  <SectionHeading … />
</PhotoSection>
```

Copy always sits on the shaded left third. Photography is night, high contrast, orange rim light — never sunny daylight stock.
