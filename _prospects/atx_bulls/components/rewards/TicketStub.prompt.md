# TicketStub
A fan’s digital ticket, used in the wallet and on game day.

```jsx
<TicketStub matchup="Bulls vs. Rattlers" date="Mar 20" time="7:00 PM" venue="Austin, Texas" section="112" row="F" seat="7" />
```

The 6px orange cap and the dashed tear line are what make it read as a stub — keep both.
