# Stadium: twin and tokenization

## The two halves, and their different clocks

**The twin starts now-ish and is unconditionally ours.** The moment siting work begins, the stadium exists as a record: candidate parcels, zoning and entitlement state, flood and utility posture, feasibility inputs, decisions with reasoning. This is originate-mode twinning per the twin substrate thesis: an asset born as a twin never needs reconstruction later, and every stage of its life (siting, design, construction, operations) deposits into one continuously verified record. Central Texas is our deepest data footprint; parcel, zoning, flood, and jurisdiction intelligence for candidate sites is a live product today, not a build.

**The tokenization happens only after the gate below is green.** Tokenizing a construction project is a securities offering on a real asset, and no credible platform, investor, or counsel will touch one before the underlying package exists.

## The prerequisite gate (the answer to Cody's question)

Nick's instinct in the 2026-08-13 meeting (land purchase, feasibility, true investment packet as bare minimum) is directionally right. The full gate, as deals actually require it, drafted here and to be confirmed against the research pass:

1. **Sponsor entity** formed, with the team's relationship to the stadium entity defined (the stadium is almost always its own SPV, not the team entity). **Hard design rule per the franchise-agreement analysis (`09_af1_purchase_agreement_analysis.md`): the stadium SPV must sit entirely outside the team's ownership chain (sibling under common control, never owning or owned by the team entity), because AF1's 3.1(a) prohibits public trading of any interest in the team entity or entities up its chain. A sibling SPV's investors are outside that wall as drafted; counsel to confirm, and the unseen Operating Agreement to be checked for venue-approval or scheduling obligations that could touch a team-affiliated stadium.**
2. **Site control.** Owned or under binding contract. Optioned land may be marketable to accredited investors; retail paths effectively require control.
3. **Entitlements posture.** Zoning confirmed or a credible rezoning path, plus the permit map for the venue type. (This is literally our product; the twin carries it.)
4. **Feasibility.** Market study (demand, events calendar beyond ten home games, competing venues), engineering feasibility, and an operating pro forma with defensible assumptions. A single-tenant arena pro forma that only works on football is weak; the events business is the underwriting.
5. **Construction package.** Budget with real estimates, ideally a GMP contract or at least a design-builder attached; construction risk is the thing investors price hardest in ground-up deals.
6. **Capital stack defined.** The tokenized slice is one tranche, usually alongside senior debt and sponsor equity. Nobody tokenizes 100 percent of a construction project; the token tranche needs to know its seniority, its distribution waterfall, and its dilution rules.
7. **Valuation support.** Appraisal or equivalent third-party basis for the raise valuation.
8. **Offering package.** PPM or offering circular, subscription docs, risk factors, use of funds, sponsor track record disclosure.
9. **Securities path selected** (counsel): 506(c) accredited, Reg A+ retail, or CF community slice, each with its own timeline and disclosure weight.
10. **Infrastructure**: KYC/AML provider, transfer agent, tokenization platform engaged, ongoing reporting plan.

Items 1 through 8 exist in any well-run development regardless of tokens. The honest summary for Cody: tokenization adds items 9 and 10 to a normal development package; it does not replace any of it.

## Why the twin is the tokenization's unfair advantage

Every prerequisite above is a document today; in our model it is a living record. The feasibility, entitlement, and construction state of the project as a continuously verified twin becomes the offering's disclosure layer: investors in the token see the actual state of the asset (entitlement milestones, construction progress, then operating cash flow) with provenance, rather than a quarterly PDF. That is the twin-behind-the-token pattern from the portfolio RWA thesis, applied to an asset we twin from birth. A tokenized venue whose disclosure surface is a live verified twin is a categorically better product than the same tokens with an email newsletter, and it is the part no tokenization platform provides.

## Financing context to hold

Minor-league venues are historically financed by municipal participation, private sponsorship, naming rights, and seat licenses more than by retail investment products. The tokenized tranche should be understood as one instrument inside that familiar stack, not the whole plan. Venue cost ranges and precedents (including any fan-funded stadium precedents) are in the research pass; findings land in `06_research_agenda.md`.

## Our role

The twin from siting day one (this is a sale of existing product); feasibility intelligence on candidate sites; the disclosure data room for the raise; construction-phase capture (progress, milestones, eventually sensors per the sensor program); operations-phase twin (the stadium as a smart site, which it would be anyway as our flagship originate-mode venue). Issuance, custody, and sale of securities: never ours.
