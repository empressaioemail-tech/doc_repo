# AF1 Purchase Agreement — what it means for the program

Source: "2027 AF1 Purchase Agreement - Austin.pdf," provided by Cody 2026-08-13. The copy reviewed is the unexecuted form (blanks for date, buyer entity, territory). Treated as the operative form; the executed version should be obtained for the record.

## Deal structure and economics (facts from the document)

1. AF1 is a Wyoming LLC ("Seller"/League). The buyer purchases a **Membership in the league LLC itself** plus exclusive Territorial Rights, then executes three deeper agreements: the (Revised) Company Agreement, a Membership Agreement, and an Operating Agreement, plus a Reserve for Cumulative Trauma Claims Agreement (referenced at 3.3).
2. Price: **$200,000** total. $100,000 due on or before August 1, 2026; the second $100,000 paid from the posted Letter of Credit after completion of the 2027 season. Buyer becomes a Member effective August 1, 2026 with voting rights for the 2027 season. A **$200,000 Letter of Credit (or cash deposit)** is due by November 1, 2026 to secure operational obligations.
3. **League Assessments** (3.1(b)): the buyer pays a share of league expenses including league office, officials, and notably **salary and benefits of coaches and players** (league-centralized payroll model), workers compensation, playoff expenses. First assessment not due before November 1, 2026. **Capital calls** possible on a three-quarter Board vote (3.1(c)).
4. **Revenue Share** (1.3): each membership carries a share of league revenue; 35 percent of the revenue share pool is distributed to member teams within 30 days of the Championship Game; pool computed as total non-equity revenue minus league budget, split equally among teams in good standing. **New teams after the 2025 season are not eligible until they have completed a full season, so the Bulls' first possible distribution follows their second season** (2028 season at the earliest). Teams leaving the league forfeit. If the league is sold, revenue share is bought out at a weighted average of the prior three years' revenue (50/30/20).
5. Default (3.3): missed payment, LOC, or execution lets the League terminate the Membership and Territorial Rights and retain all sums paid.
6. League holds a royalty-free, worldwide, sublicensable license to team IP for broadcast and marketing of AF1 (3.1(h)). Team publicity about this agreement requires League consent (7.14). Wyoming law, exclusive Wyoming jurisdiction (7.7, 7.8). No assignment of the agreement or rights without the other party's consent, withholdable in sole discretion (7.5).

## The tokenization wall: 3.1(a)

Verbatim substance: **"No ownership interest in Buyer, or any entity owning a direct or indirect interest in Buyer, will be publicly traded."**

Reading, for counsel to confirm or correct:

1. **A tokenized team equity instrument trading on an ATS is very likely "publicly traded" and therefore prohibited** as drafted. The Watford-style tradeable digital equity is off the table for the team entity without League renegotiation.
2. **A conventional community raise (Reg CF class) with restricted, non-traded units is arguably not "publicly traded"** (no trading market exists; Reg CF units are resale-restricted for one year and no ATS listing would be sought). This is a genuine counsel question, not a settled reading, and League consent in writing would be the prudent posture regardless of the technical reading.
3. The ownership-transparency provisions compound the practical burden of any equity raise: 5.4 requires a complete list of **every individual with a direct or indirect ownership interest** in Buyer, and 5.5 requires a **Letter of Investment Intent executed by every such individual**. A raise adding 2,500 small equity holders is operationally incompatible with those provisions unless the League agrees otherwise or the investors sit outside the "ownership interest" definition.

## The instrument that may thread the needle: revenue-share notes

A revenue-participation note issued by the team entity is **debt, not an ownership interest**. Noteholders are creditors; they appear on no ownership list, sign no Letters of Investment Intent, and a note is not an "ownership interest in Buyer" under 3.1(a) as drafted. Pass A established that revenue-share notes are natively supported at Reg CF (Wefunder standard template) and are unambiguously securities under every path (fine; that is what the exemption is for). INFERENCE, for counsel: the community instrument most compatible with this franchise agreement is a **revenue-share note timed to the hype window**, not equity. Open sub-questions: whether ATS trading of such notes would offend the spirit or renegotiated letter of 3.1(a); whether the deeper League Agreements restrict member-entity indebtedness or revenue pledges (unknown, documents unseen); and whether the note's revenue base should be team local revenue only, given the League Assessment and capital-call priority claims on the entity.

## What this means for the pro forma of any team instrument

League revenue share cannot appear in the Bulls' investor economics until after the second completed season, and the entity carries assessment obligations (including league-payroll share) and capital-call exposure from November 2026. Team-level revenue available to any instrument is local: tickets, local sponsorship, merch, local activations. Any offering pro forma that shows league distributions in year one is wrong on the face of the franchise agreement. The $200,000 franchise fee is also a diligence fact any investor will see; valuation narratives need to be built with it in view.

## The stadium is outside this wall

3.1(a) restricts ownership interests **in Buyer and in entities owning interests in Buyer** (upstream). A stadium SPV that neither owns an interest in the team entity nor is owned by it (a sibling entity under common control, or an unrelated entity) is not reached by this clause as drafted. **Structural consequence: keep the stadium SPV entirely outside the team's ownership chain** and the stadium tokenization proceeds under ordinary securities and real estate law, unconstrained by the League document. If the holdco that owns the team also owns the stadium SPV, the holdco itself must never be publicly traded, but the stadium SPV's own investors sit outside the chain. Counsel to confirm the sibling structure cleanly; also check the unseen Operating Agreement for any venue-related obligations (home-venue approval rights, scheduling commitments) that could touch a team-affiliated stadium.

## What we still have not seen (the wall may have more bricks)

The Purchase Agreement incorporates by reference: the **Company Agreement** (as revised), the **Membership Agreement** (which retains a "Rights Fee" concept, 3.1(d)), the **Operating Agreement**, and the **Reserve for Cumulative Trauma Claims Agreement**. Transfer restrictions, consent rights, rights of first refusal, pledge and encumbrance restrictions, and indebtedness covenants customarily live in exactly those documents. The gating-document item in doc 06 is therefore **partially resolved**: 3.1(a) answers the headline question, but the four underlying agreements must be obtained from Cody and read before any instrument is designed. Also worth obtaining: whether Cody's executed version matches this form, and the identity and structure of the Buyer entity as actually formed (5.1, 5.3).

## Program consequences applied

1. Doc 03 (team tokenization): tradeable tokenized equity is off the table as drafted; the candidate community instrument shifts to a revenue-share note; League written consent is the prudent posture for anything retail-facing; no league revenue in pro formas before season two.
2. Doc 04 (stadium): unchanged in substance, strengthened in structure; the sibling-SPV separation is now a hard design rule, not a preference.
3. Doc 01 (fan monetization): unaffected. Consumptive passes, deposits, merch, and memberships touch no ownership interest and no League clause found here, except that marketing copy must also respect 7.14 (no publicity about the League agreement itself without consent).
4. Doc 06: gating item updated from "obtain the franchise agreement" to "obtain the four underlying League Agreements and the executed Purchase Agreement."
