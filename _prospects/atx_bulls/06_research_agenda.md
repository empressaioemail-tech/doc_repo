# Research agenda and counsel questions

Two research passes dispatched 2026-08-13; **both landed same day.** Pass B findings: `07_research_pass_b_stadium_findings.md` (headline: no tokenized stadium precedent exists anywhere; construction tokens are debt-shaped in practice; secondary liquidity near zero even for the flagship deal; a second Austin indoor team, ATX Draft, is circling the H-E-B Center for spring 2027). Pass A findings: `08_research_pass_a_fan_team_findings.md` (headline: the March 17, 2026 SEC-CFTC guidance explicitly names fan tokens as non-securities when consumptive, making 2026 the first viable US year; the BIG3 class action is the live cautionary precedent for ownership-flavored marketing; the Oakland Ballers Reg CF raise, $3M+ pre-season-one, revises the doc 03 sequencing assumption; AF1 ownership rules are unfindable publicly, so the franchise agreement is the gating document). The pass scopes as dispatched:

**Pass A: fan and team tokenization landscape.** Fan-token models (Socios class) and their 2025-2026 trajectory; US securities posture on fan tokens and NFT memberships; non-token digital membership and seat-deposit precedents for launch-stage teams; precedents for crowdfunded and tokenized team ownership (Packers model, Reg CF minor-league raises with amounts, DAO attempts, 2024-2026 tokenized offerings); the Reg CF vs Reg A+ vs Reg D 506(c) tradeoffs as practiced, with costs and timelines; active tokenized-offering platforms in 2026; revenue-share instruments as an equity alternative.

**Pass B: stadium and construction tokenization.** Tokenized commercial real estate precedents with cash-flow distribution, stadium and venue examples specifically; ground-up construction-phase tokenized raises and how construction risk is handled; the 2026 RWA platform landscape for real estate sponsors and their published sponsor requirements; standard deal structures (SPV, token as LLC unit, exemption paths, secondary liquidity reality); minor-league venue financing norms and cost ranges for 3,000 to 8,000 seat indoor arenas; fan-funded stadium precedents; anything public on ATX Bulls stadium plans and the Austin venue development climate.
