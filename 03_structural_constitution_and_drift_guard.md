# Structural Constitution and Drift Guard

```
Version: 2026-06-02
Status:  Canonical invariants. Changes rarely and deliberately.
Purpose: The standard the business is checked against, and the method for checking.
```

This document is the stable anchor. It states what must not drift and how to notice when it is drifting. It is deliberately separate from the roadmap, which changes, so that the anchor stays still while the work moves. The two skills that operationalize it, premortem-check and catalog-thesis-check, run proposed moves against these invariants. decision-log records the moves that clear. This document is the standard they all check against.

## Part one: the invariants

These are the things that, if violated, mean the company has drifted from itself. Each states the invariant, what counts as a violation, and the condition under which the invariant itself could legitimately change.

**I1. The two roots are inviolable.** The substrate rests on calibration and sovereignty. Every move serves at least one and violates neither. Violation: any move that advances one root by breaking the other. Change condition: only if a third root emerges that is neither served by the four commitments nor reducible to these two.

**I2. Never trade sovereignty for retention.** Retention comes from being right, not from trapping. No lock in, no switching cost engineered as a moat, not even temporarily during the vulnerable early phase. Violation: any retention mechanism whose force comes from the cost of leaving rather than the value of staying. Change condition: none foreseen. This is the line least open to compromise.

**I3. Confidence is earned, not asserted.** Every output that carries a confidence signal must be calibratable against outcome, and the system must be built to tighten that calibration with use. Violation: shipping asserted confidence as if it were earned, or a confidence number with no path to being checked. Change condition: none. This is the core of the trust claim.

**I4. The isolated tier stays genuinely complete.** A participant who declines to contribute still gets a competent, working product. Violation: crippling the isolated tier to coerce participation, which is capture wearing an opt in costume. Change condition: none.

**I5. Value returns to contributors.** The network's value compounds back to the people who created it. The company does not aggregate and centrally capture the network's value. Violation: any structure where contributors generate compounding value that the center keeps. Change condition: none. This is the non extractive thesis.

**I6. Reward is intrinsic first.** The primary reward for participation is the contributor's own work getting better. Monetary reward exists only to compensate spillover, paid as a mutual or cooperative dividend, reduced cost or share of value, never a tradeable or speculative token. Violation: leading with financialized incentives, or introducing a speculative token. Change condition: revisit the dividend mechanism if observed contribution lags, but never the no speculative token rule.

**I7. The infrastructure stays invisible.** IPFS, the ledger, and the payment rail are plumbing, never the pitch. Buyers experience the benefits, ownership and provable attribution, never the underlying mechanism. Violation: leading a buyer conversation with the technology rather than the benefit. Change condition: none at the buyer surface.

**I8. Cities are partners, not targets, and the framing is cost recoupment.** Data sources are licensors who share in value, per partnership first sourcing. The municipal value return is framed as cost recoupment, never profit. Violation: treating a jurisdiction as an extraction target, or framing municipal participation as the city profiting from its regulatory function. Change condition: none on the partnership principle.

**I9. Displacement, not acquisition, against the extractive incumbent.** The company does not arm the model it is built to replace. Violation: powering, partnering into, or otherwise strengthening the extractive aggregator's capture model. Change condition: only from a position of clear strength after displacement is working, and only if the structure preserves all invariants above.

**I10. Sell reasoning, not data.** Every output carries reasoning chain, source citation, confidence score, and timestamp regardless of tier, with free and paid layers held in their settled order. Violation: shipping output that fails the quality gate, or inverting the tier model. Change condition: pricing specifics can move, the quality gate cannot.

## Part two: the drift detection method

Run any proposed move, new workstream, feature, commercial structure, partnership, naming, or architectural choice, through these questions before it enters company intelligence as a decided direction.

1. **Root test.** Which root does this serve, and does it break the other. A move that serves neither does not earn cycles. A move that breaks either stops here.
2. **Capture test.** Does retention here come from value or from the cost of leaving. If from the cost of leaving, it is drift toward the thing the company refuses.
3. **Earned confidence test.** Does any confidence claim in this move have a path to being checked against outcome. If not, it asserts rather than earns.
4. **Return test.** Does the value created here compound back to the contributors, or get captured centrally.
5. **Completeness test.** If this gates a benefit behind participation, is the non participating product still genuinely complete.
6. **Visibility test.** Does this surface the plumbing or financialize the incentive in front of a buyer.
7. **Spine and focus test.** Does this feed or express the substrate, and is it on an active sprint. If neither, what gets queued or killed to make room. The shop is lean.
8. **Build versus ship test.** Is this splitting scarce build attention across competing builds, or is it shipping something already built. Concentration constrains the former, not the latter.

A move that passes all eight is aligned. A move that fails the root, capture, earned confidence, or return tests is a structural conflict and does not proceed without a conscious, recorded override, because those four are load bearing. A failure on the others is resolvable but must be named, not waved through by silence.

## Part three: how this is used

Before a decision: run premortem-check, which scores the move against the structural commitments, and catalog-thesis-check for anything touching brand, naming, tier, or architecture.

After a decision clears: record it with decision-log, including its reversal criteria, so the canon compounds instead of living in chat history.

On a cadence: run project-refresh to check whether the active work has drifted from this document, and update the roadmap audit, not this constitution. This document moves only when an invariant's stated change condition is actually met.
