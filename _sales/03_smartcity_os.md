# SmartCity OS

The "large" in small/medium/large. Longest sales cycle, biggest contract, sold use-case-first to small and medium municipalities. Bastrop, Texas is the live flagship deployment.

## One-liner

One pane of glass to run your city. See everything about any property in a single click, run plan review the way the state will, and get ahead of problems instead of reacting to them. Manage the city from your phone.

## The buyer

City managers, department heads, planners, and inspectors in small-to-medium cities. These are the cities the Palantir-scale vendors skip and the point-solution vendors nickel-and-dime. The government buyer cares about the problem solved, not the technology. Never open with "digital twin" or any substrate talk; open with the workflow pain.

## The problem

A typical city runs on six departments and a dozen disconnected systems: GIS in one place, permits in another, code enforcement in a third, finances in a fourth, plus spreadsheets and email holding it together. Nobody has a single view of the city. Staff answer the same property questions over and over. Institutional knowledge walks out the door with every staff transition.

## The four surfaces (use-case-first)

1. **The dashboard: one pane of glass.** Every system pulled into one view of the city, on a map. Permits, code enforcement, development, finances, operations, emergencies. Proactive instead of reactive, usable from a phone. Proof point: Bastrop's city leadership runs the city day to day on it.
2. **Plan review (Codex).** Plan review today is slow, inconsistent, and resubmission-heavy. Codex runs a cited review against the city's own adopted code: every finding cites a real code section, the reviewer accepts, edits, or rejects, and it rolls into a structured comment letter. The reviewer's existing workflow, accelerated. The same engine also lets contractors self-check before submitting, so submittals come in cleaner.
3. **Parcel / property intelligence.** Click any parcel and see everything through it: zoning, setbacks, permits, flood and hydrology. The demo that lands: "what happens to this property if it rains four inches in 24 hours," answered with citations.
4. **Ambient capture ("it learns your city").** Staff decisions and institutional knowledge get captured as the city's own intelligence as the team works, so the platform gets sharper with use and knowledge stops evaporating with turnover. Bundled into the OS, not a separate line item.

## The infrastructure vision (roadmap, label it as the direction)

The current Bastrop deployment runs on the earlier map; the upgrade path is the full digital-twin base layer. On the twin, the city's physical infrastructure (water mains, wastewater, storm drainage, streetlights, traffic signals) becomes part of the living model. The end state is infrastructure that reports its own condition, so the city knows a water line is failing or a streetlight is going out before a citizen calls. Sell this as the direction the platform is headed and what the city grows into. Do not claim live sensors today.

## Proof and credibility

- **Bastrop is live.** A real Texas city runs on the platform today. Frame Bastrop as the pioneering first city in a growing network, a design partner shaping the product from the practitioner's seat. Never frame any city as a data source.
- **The city owns its data.** A city's private data stays its own, full stop. This matters enormously to government buyers.
- **Built on public records.** The property intelligence layer is built from the same public records any citizen could request, processed uniformly. No special data arrangements needed from the city to get started.

## The grant angle

Cities fund modernization through grant programs (resilience, data redundancy, modernization initiatives). SmartCity OS fits grant narratives well, which lowers the buyer's cost barrier and gives them a reason to act now. Ask early: "are there grant programs or budget cycles this could ride?"

## Competitive frame

Closest analog is Palantir, which serves big government and is priced like it. Point solutions (permit software, GIS viewers) each solve one silo and add another login. SmartCity OS is one AI-native platform for the whole city, sized and priced for the overlooked middle, where the city owns its data and the platform compounds on the city's own outcomes.
