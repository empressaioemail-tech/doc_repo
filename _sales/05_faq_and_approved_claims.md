# FAQ, approved claims, and rules of the road

## Approved claims (safe to say to a prospect today)

- Property Explorer is live, on the web, phone-friendly, free to browse with no account.
- Over two million Central Texas parcels are browsable on the map.
- Bastrop, Texas is a live SmartCity OS city: real city leadership runs day-to-day operations on the platform. Bastrop is our pioneering first city and design partner.
- Setback lines and buildable envelopes draw automatically on covered properties.
- Site plans download from the app; 3D terrain files download in about thirty seconds (a job that takes an architect roughly six hours by hand; this is Nick's own before-and-after as a practicing architect).
- The flood and drainage report is live as the first paid report; more report types are rolling out.
- Every answer the system gives carries a source citation, a confidence signal, and a timestamp. When data is not verified for a place, the product says so instead of guessing.
- Records in the data layer are anchored with blockchain-based event anchoring (part of our substrate SDK), making the record tamper-evident. Use this as a trust point when a prospect asks how we guarantee data integrity, not as an opener; if they want technical depth, route to Nick.
- All property intelligence is built from uniform public records (appraisal districts, county records, FEMA, state GIS). No scraped private data, no special-access arrangements.
- Zoning is wired in for a growing set of Central Texas cities, with more coming online continuously.
- The customer owns their data. A tenant's or customer's private data is never pooled into anyone else's product.

If a claim is not on this list or in the product files, check the live app or ask Nick before saying it.

## Never say (without Nick's explicit go)

- **A price.** Every price is either being finalized or packaged per engagement. "Free to browse, paid tier for deep work, and I will confirm current pricing" is always the right answer.
- **A guaranteed savings number.** The honest framing is in the custom-builds file: savings stack across many workflows and get scoped in discovery.
- **Live sensors / IoT monitoring.** Infrastructure that reports its own condition is the roadmap vision. Day-one reality is pattern intelligence on data the customer already has.
- **Coverage promises for a specific county or data layer** without checking the app first. Coverage expands weekly; check, then claim.
- **Customer names.** Bastrop is public and approved. Any other customer or prospect (including the multifamily operator) stays anonymous ("a large Austin multifamily operator") until Nick approves a named reference.
- **Delivery dates.** "Coming soon" and "weeks, not months" are fine where this pack says so; specific dates are not.
- **Internal codenames.** Engine names, internal architecture terms, and internal project names stay internal. The customer-facing names are Property Explorer, SmartCity OS, and plan review.

## Objection handling

**"I can already see this on the county appraisal district site."**
The CAD shows you one silo: the tax record. It does not draw your setbacks, calculate your buildable envelope, show flood exposure, produce a site plan, or let you ask questions. And it definitely does not do it on your phone standing in the yard. We unify the CAD, the courthouse, FEMA, and the city, then add the analysis on top.

**"Is the AI just making this up?"**
The opposite, and it is our favorite question. Every answer is cited to its source with a timestamp and a confidence signal, and where we have not verified data the product says "not verified here" instead of inventing something. We built the whole system so that a wrong number cannot silently appear.

**"Is my county covered?"**
"Let me pull it up right now." Open the app on their address. Whatever it shows is the honest answer, including any "not verified" layers, plus "coverage is expanding across Texas continuously."

**"How much does it cost?"**
Property Explorer: free to browse; the paid tier covers reports, exports, saving, and AI chat, and I will confirm current pricing for you. SmartCity and custom builds: scoped per engagement after discovery, because it depends entirely on which systems we wire in.

**"We already have GIS / permit software / property management software."**
Keep it. We connect underneath what you already run, we do not rip and replace. Your systems become tabs in one view instead of six separate logins. Over time you decide what stays.

**"What happens to our data?"**
You own it. Your private data stays yours, is never pooled into anyone else's product, and if you ever leave, it leaves with you in a usable format.

**"You're a small company. What if you disappear?"**
Two answers: your data is yours and exportable from day one (see above), and the platform is built on public records and open standards, not proprietary lock-in. Also, a real city already runs on this in production.

## Mini glossary

- **Parcel:** one legally defined piece of land, the unit everything hangs on.
- **Setback:** the required distance between a structure and the property line (front, side, rear). Set by zoning.
- **Buildable envelope:** the area left on a lot after setbacks, where you can actually build. The app draws it.
- **ADU:** accessory dwelling unit (granny flat, casita, garage apartment). A top use case: "can I add an ADU here?"
- **Digital twin:** a living digital representation of a real-world thing (a parcel, a road, a building, a city) that stays current as the real world changes.
- **Plan review:** the check building plans go through against the code before a permit is issued. We make it fast and cited, for cities and for builders pre-submission.
- **One pane of glass:** one screen showing everything, instead of many logins.
