# Property Explorer

The "small" in small/medium/large. This is the product Cammie can start sharing immediately: consumer-friendly, works on a phone, free to try, share by text link.

## One-liner

Everything about any property, in the palm of your hand, one click. It is the county appraisal district plus the courthouse plus FEMA plus the city's zoning, unified on a map, with AI reporting on top.

## Who it is for

Anyone who looks at or sells property:

- **Real estate agents.** Answer buyer questions on the spot, in the field, from a phone. Share a live property view with the client. It helps them sell and it makes them look sharp.
- **Builders and custom builders.** The strongest fit. "Can we put an ADU here, where do the setbacks fall, what is the buildable envelope" are questions they currently have to call the city about. Now it is one click, standing in the yard.
- **Architects and engineers.** Site plans and 3D terrain files download in seconds. Building site topography by hand takes about six hours per site. The terrain download does it in about thirty seconds.
- **Investors.** Quick feasibility screening: buildable envelope, zoning, flood exposure, ADU potential, before making an offer.
- **Homeowners.** Usually arrive through a share from one of the above. They browse free, which grows the network.

## What it does today (safe to demo and claim)

- **Browse free, no account needed.** The map opens on Central Texas with satellite imagery. Click any parcel and see the public-record facts: ownership-record data, lot lines, zoning where the city is wired in, land use, flood layer where verified.
- **Setbacks and buildable envelope.** On covered properties, the app draws the setback lines and the buildable envelope right on the parcel. This is the wedge visual: nobody else shows this on a click.
- **Measure and draw tools plus GPS.** Sketch an ADU, a pool, an addition, right on the property with a client watching. Works on a phone or iPad standing on the site.
- **Share.** Send the property view to a client. They land on the same screen with a free account. Every share grows the user base (this is also how Cammie's referral tracking will work).
- **AI chat about the property.** Chat with full property context loaded. Ask questions in plain English.
- **Reports.** Cited, source-attributed property reports. The flood and drainage report is live as the first paid report, with more report types rolling out. Every report carries citations, confidence, and timestamps.
- **Site plan export.** Download a site plan of the property (PDF, and CAD-friendly formats for professionals).
- **3D terrain download.** A 3D file of the site terrain within the property boundaries, ready for design tools. This is the six-hours-to-thirty-seconds story, and it is Nick's own story as an architect. Tell it.
- **Installs like an app.** Works as a phone-installable web app with GPS on the map.

## Free vs paid

- **Free:** browse, parcel click, base public information, receiving shares.
- **Paid (Pro):** the deep work. Reports, AI chat, save properties, exports (site plan, terrain).

## Coverage honesty (important)

Coverage is deepest in Bastrop and live across Central Texas, expanding across Texas. Not every layer is verified everywhere yet, and the app is honest about it: where data is not verified it says so instead of guessing. Use that as a selling point ("when it shows you a number, the number is real and cited"), and never promise a specific county or layer is covered without checking the app first. The two-minute pre-call check: pull up the prospect's own address or farm area in the app before the conversation.

## Demo flow (10 minutes)

1. Open the app on a property the prospect knows (their house, their listing, their last project). Familiar ground makes it land.
2. Click the parcel. Walk the facts panel: lot, zoning, land use, flood.
3. Show the setback lines and buildable envelope. Pause here. This is the moment.
4. Ask their real question ("could you fit an ADU back here?") and sketch it with the draw tool.
5. Download the site plan or terrain file if they are a builder or architect. Tell the six-hour story.
6. Share it to their phone. Point out their client would land on this same screen free.
