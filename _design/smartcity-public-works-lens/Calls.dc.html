Counting rule: every generated goto call-volume bucket on this pack; answered and abandoned are each drawn independently and offered is their sum, so no class here is the remainder of another. Both margins sum to 2365; the product renders them as two separate tables, and two tables that must agree and are never shown agreeing is a reconciliation nobody performs. No calendar date is printed anywhere: nothing was read; this record was generated.
