I am looking at 4207 Brandt Rd in Austin. What can I actually build on it, and does the flood zone reach the lot?
Zoning is SF-3, so a single family house with a rear unit is allowed. The buildable envelope after setbacks is about 4,150 sq ft. Zone AE covers the back of the lot, so the house pad has to sit forward of it.
