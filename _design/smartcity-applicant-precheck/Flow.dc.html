Plan precheck: the flowFIXTURE
A pre-submittal self-check on a SmartCity public page, branded with the city's logo. The applicant finds the lot, picks a project, uploads plans, reads findings, revises, and may submit with suggestions still open. The city sets the rules, opens a precheck by its code, and records its decision against the lot. Each box states whether it exists today, read from source at the SHAs in source-state.json.

Where the public page lives. A SmartCity public surface, per this design. Its address, and how it relates to Smart Site, is not ruled.
Which service runs the check. The adjudicator is in plan-review and the AI reading is in legacy-design-tools. The precheck needs both.
Where plan sets are stored. Smart Files is plan-review's store, and has no identity for an applicant who has not submitted.
Who pays, and which checks ship first. Access is drawn as a city setting with no value chosen.
How far the decision travels. A city's decision is that city's data. Showing it publicly is the city's choice.
What the boards hold to
The checklist is shown before upload, so every result has a denominator: 9 checks, 6 read from plans against the lot, 3 left to the city.
Results are sorted by who acts next. The applicant never sees Pass or Fail.
Every reading says it came from software and names its sheet.
A rule with no edition carries no citation. Only the two Bastrop sections in source are cited.
Suggestions never block. Submitting with one open is a normal path, and the city sees it.
