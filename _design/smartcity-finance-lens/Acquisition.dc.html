The cost of not doing this is on the capture. The Scenario Modeler runs three-year projections with conservative, expected and optimistic ranges and stamps them HIGH CONFIDENCE, with a 3/3 connected badge beside it. Its expense baseline is the budget column copied. A confidence badge on a simulation whose baseline was never measured is the most expensive shape in the product, because it is the screen a city manager takes into a council meeting.
