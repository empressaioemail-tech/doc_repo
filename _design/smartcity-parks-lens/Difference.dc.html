Two sentences the product keeps apart

The line src/domains.mjs draws, drawn

These two states look alike on any screen that reports them as an absence, and they ask a city for two completely different things. The left column is every other department lens on a city with no grant. The right column is Parks, on every city there will ever be.

The rule, in the product’s own words src/domains.mjs, above DOMAIN_REGISTRY

“WHAT ABSENCE FROM THIS LIST MEANS, and it is the only surviving meaning of the words not built: the surface does not exist yet. Everything in the list is built, and its emptiness on a given pack is a statement about SOURCES with a basis attached. Those are different sentences to a customer and this list is the line between them.”

There are five states in all. Four are in DOMAIN_STATUSES: ok, granted-empty, ungranted, no-fixture-source. The fifth, not-registered, deliberately has no entry there, and that absence is itself the design.
