End access: Fixture staff 07

FIXTUREADMIN

What ends, where, and how fast, when SmartCity ends one account. The products do not all stop at the same moment, and this page says which.

Where access ends 4 places
Where When access ends Why
SmartCity dashboards On the next request Every request checks whether the account is still active.
Plan review Up to 15 minutes It keeps no record of ended accounts. A sign-in already issued lasts until it ages out.
Smart Files Up to 15 minutes The same as Plan review.
The sign-in provider Not ended Ending the person's session at the provider is not built.
Who asks, and how fast no owner
No owner, no turnaround
The city has to tell SmartCity when someone leaves, and nothing yet says who hears it or how fast SmartCity acts.

The single sign-on option that was ruled out would have ended access everywhere the moment a city switched off one account, without anyone telling us. The ruling that stands makes ending access a process instead: the city tells SmartCity, and SmartCity ends it. That trade was made knowingly. It needs a named owner and a stated turnaround, or the gap is found after a staff member has already left.

Basis: the 2026-09-14 staff identity ruling names this as the real cost of the decision. No owner and no turnaround are recorded anywhere.
