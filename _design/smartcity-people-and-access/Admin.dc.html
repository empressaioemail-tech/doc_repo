Not yet true 4 things this page cannot promise
Nothing on this page has run against a real sign-in provider.The provider calls are written to its documented interface and have never been made against a real organization. Build now, verify later, by operator direction.
Two-step sign-in is switched on by hand.It has to be turned on in the provider's dashboard for each organization. Nothing in the product checks that it was.
A role limits nothing yet.Only this page checks a role. Every account can open every lens until department access ships.
Ending access does not end the provider session.The account is ended here. The person's session at the sign-in provider is not, and that call is not built.
