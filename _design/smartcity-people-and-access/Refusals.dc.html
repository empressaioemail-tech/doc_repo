When access is refused

FIXTURE

What a person is told when the product will not let them in, in the product's own words. A refusal names its reason. It is never a blank page, and it is never the wrong reason.

Refusals 7 shown of 13 the product can emit
Who What they are told Why
Someone whose access was endedrevoked
“this account has been disabled.” Right for this person. Their account existed and SmartCity ended it.
Someone SmartCity never set uprevokedWRONG
“this account has been disabled.” The same words, and false for this person: nothing was ended, because nothing was set up. The product can tell these two apart and reports them as one.
What they should be toldnot_provisionedPROPOSED
“this account has not been set up by SmartCity.” Proposed. The product does not emit this today.
A department account opening People and accessnot_admin_or_city_manager
“People and access is readable by the admin and city-manager roles only.” Only the city manager and SmartCity may read the list of accounts.
A city manager account with no city on itno_tenant_claim
“this city-manager identity carries no tenant claim to scope the roster to.” Without a city there is no list to show, so it refuses rather than showing every city.
Anyone, while the account list cannot be readrevocation_check_failed
“could not confirm this identity is still active: …” It refuses rather than letting the person in. That is the right way for this to fail.
Anyone whose sign-in has aged outtoken_too_old
“this token was issued …” A sign-in is capped at 15 minutes by default, which is what bounds access in Plan review and Smart Files after an account is ended.
