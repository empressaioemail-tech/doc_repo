- [SmartCity Records search](smartcity-records-search/) — the one work surface that is neither a mount nor a region: a finder across the cases in the eleven registered domains and the documents behind the Files mount. COVERAGE BEFORE RESULTS, because four different sentences share the shape of a short list and only one of them is "no matches" — no matches, not asked (a region whose vendor is withheld), not permitted (the product's own `read_outside_scope`), and not searchable (a document with no text layer, counted under its reason). Nothing on the boards is a number without a denominator: the corpus panel carries the product's own in-scope/searched/per-reason counts with `emptyCoverage()`'s counting rule verbatim, and the illustrative figures are badged as illustrative. The outcome vocabulary is derived from the product's four statuses, its refusal code and its two count words, so a fifth cannot be added by editing a board. This surface may not call itself "Not built" in its own voice, and `node check.mjs` (56 self-tests) refuses it outside the quoted shipped values. DRAFT 2026-09-18, awaiting ratification; drawn after the blanket approval, so that approval does not cover it. Also adjudicates G-147's second item: the applicant-facing plan review view is ALREADY COVERED by smartcity-applicant-precheck, which claims the same plan row, so the gap there is buildability and the still-uncovered Citizen lens rather than coverage. `node violate.mjs` (24 plants) found two real instrument defects on its first run, both fixed.
- [SmartCity Citizen lens](smartcity-citizen-lens/) — the only surface a resident ever sees, drawn at `smartcity-dashboards` `origin/main` `7487d7c0`: four regions and the four distances between them kept apart (there is no region registered, it is not a capability, no source is connected, and there is no subject to measure), each printing a state word, a distance and a basis or none of the three. The one word this design changes is `None on file`, which the product's own five-word badge vocabulary does not carry and which states a zero about a resident on a lens that by design does not know who is reading; the shipped disabled lookup with its basis printed underneath is the exemplar, carried verbatim rather than softened. The twelve-tile grid is not re-proposed and `check.mjs` refuses a tile by name. Nobody is named, the fixture board is badged in three places, and absent, zero and unmeasured never collapse — a region that has not been read carries no count at all, not a zero. DRAFT 2026-09-18, awaiting ratification; drawn after the blanket approval, so that approval does not cover it. `node check.mjs` (54 self-tests in both directions, 13 rules against `source-state.json`) and `node violate.mjs` (16 plants, including both refusals).
