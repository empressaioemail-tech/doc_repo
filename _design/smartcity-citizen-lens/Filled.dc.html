The board is a fixture and it says so in three places: the DEMO FIXTURE badge in the top bar, the footer, and the row marker. A fixture that is not badged is the defect this board exists to prevent, and check.mjs refuses a board carrying fixture rows without the badge.

What this board may not show
xA resident. Nobody is named on any board here, and a fixture row names a generated identifier instead.
xA metric strip. The twelve-tile grid was dropped once already; a tile is a measured figure and there is no measurement on this lens to put in one.
xA figure with no counting rule, including the number on this page. The instrument counts them and refuses the board when the count is not the number it can support.
