The distances this lens keeps apart

five cards, and only one of them carries a figure

This is the board the design exists for. The Citizen lens has the least data behind it of any shipped surface, which makes it the surface where absent, zero and unmeasured are most tempting to render as the same thing. They are not the same thing, they do not ask the same person to do the same work, and the figure that appears on this board appears exactly once.
