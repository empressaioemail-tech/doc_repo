SMARTCITY OS · PLATFORM OVERVIEW

# No More Department Silos

SmartCity OS joins what your departments already run into one record: every place, every file, every asset. One click on a parcel shows everything. One search finds anything. Every department sees its own view of the same record, tailored to their role.

## One record, three ways in

**By Place.** Click any parcel and see everything your city knows about it: the permits, the zoning, the flood layer, the documents, the decisions. Every department's records, one screen.

**By Department.** The city manager sees across everything. Development services sees the pipeline. Finance sees the money. Residents see what's theirs. Same record underneath, so the numbers agree and the map is the same map.

**By Search.** One search across everything your city holds. Revise a document once and it's current everywhere, and see the revision history.

## What it is

Every place becomes a smart site. Every document becomes a smart file. Every water main, sidewalk and vehicle becomes a smart asset. That is SmartCity OS.

## How you use it

**Dashboards. See it.** Your whole city in one place, with each department seeing what is theirs, every view drawn from the same record. A complete, deployable system on day one.

**Plan Review. Decide on it.** Submittals arrive pre-reviewed against your city's own adopted code, cited section by section. Your reviewer's judgment always governs.

**Asset Management. Own it.** Every physical asset your city tracks, water mains to sidewalks to vehicles, takes its place in the same record. Built with you, around what you already have.

## Results you can see. Answers you can check.

One view of the whole city, and numbers that agree. Staff answering a question once instead of four times. Submittals arriving pre-checked, so more review stays in-house and the queue moves. Records that hold up years later, and knowledge that stays when people retire. Every answer carries its source and its date. Open any number and see where it came from. And the record is yours: you decide who sees what, and it stays.
