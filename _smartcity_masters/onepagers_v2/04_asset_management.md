# A Record of Your City You Own and Can Prove

Every physical thing your city is responsible for, in one place. Water mains to sidewalks to vehicles: where each one is, what condition it is in, what has been done to it, and who changed it and when. From the paperwork, to its live condition, to a visual view when you need one.

Today an asset lives in a utility layer, a work-order system, a spreadsheet, or a set of as-built drawings in a filing cabinet, and none of them know anything about each other, or about the properties, roads, and flood zones around them. When the person who built that knowledge retires, it leaves with them.

## What it does

**One connected picture, not another silo.** Your assets sit alongside your properties, roads, terrain, and flood data, not in a system that knows nothing about any of it. "Which properties lose water if this main fails, and are any of them in the flood zone" is a query, not a project.

**A record you can prove.** Every asset carries where it came from, when it was established, and every change since. Nothing is silently overwritten. Years from now, the record still holds up.

**You decide who sees what.** Public assets can be public. The sewer network can be visible only to public works and the contractors they authorize. A sensitive facility can be locked to a named few. That control is built into the record itself.

**Live condition, kept.** Sensor and telemetry readings attach to the asset they measure, and every reading becomes part of that asset's permanent record. See how an asset has actually behaved over time, not just what it reads right now.
