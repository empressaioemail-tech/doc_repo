# The white-space package

The formal answer to one question: why does nothing else on the market do what this product line does, and what evidence supports saying so. Assembled 2026-08-07 from the Vertosoft launch-prep session. Everything the argument rests on is inside this folder; nothing here requires a file outside it except the product claims registers in the parent masters.

## How this package is used

**Channel enablement.** The rendering of 01 for partner reps is specified in [../onepager_briefs/06_onepager_category_whitespace.md](../onepager_briefs/06_onepager_category_whitespace.md), including the evidence band drawn from 03 to 06. Reps get the rendered page, not this folder.

**Collateral figures.** No number leaves this package for a market-facing artifact unless it is third-party, dated, and attributed, and appears in a one-pager brief exactly as approved there. The triangulated coverage numbers in 02 never leave. The figures rule in [../onepager_briefs/00_onepager_system.md](../onepager_briefs/00_onepager_system.md) governs.

**Pricing.** 07 plus the anchors summary feeds the government list-price decision, which remains the open operator gate for the Vertosoft launch.

**Evidence grades.** Every note labels each claim (published-price, measured-study, government-document, vendor-claim, anecdotal, honest-absence, or the bracketed equivalents). Honest absence is treated as a finding throughout; two absences (the retrieval-versus-judgment split in 05, the recorder before-and-after deflection study in 06) are flagged in 01 as greenfield measurements nobody owns yet.
