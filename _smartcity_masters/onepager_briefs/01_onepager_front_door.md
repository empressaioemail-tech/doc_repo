# Front door — one-pager brief (locked copy)

The first page a city manager or a channel rep sees. It differentiates the whole line in one read and is the parent every product one-pager hangs off. The idea it must land: one record, three ways in, nothing ripped out — and the products are how the city uses the record.

This copy was locked in the 2026-08-07 working session against the approved render. The final-copy document version is `../onepagers_v2/01_smartcity_os_front_door.md`; the two must stay identical. Changes route through the operator.

## Audience and job

City manager, first contact; also the page a Vertosoft rep leads with. Job: recognize the silo pain in the headline, understand the three-doors idea in one pass, see the three products as ways to use the record rather than modules to buy separately, and want the demonstration.

## Page anatomy and copy (locked)

### Zone 1 — Hero

**Chip:** PLATFORM OVERVIEW

**Headline:** No More Department Silos

**Subhead:** SmartCity OS joins what your departments already run into one record: every place, every file, every asset. One click on a parcel shows everything. One search finds anything. Every department sees its own view of the same record, tailored to their role.

### Zone 2 — One record, three ways in

Section label: ONE RECORD, THREE WAYS IN. Three cards, equal weight.

**Card: By Place.** Click any parcel and see everything your city knows about it: the permits, the zoning, the flood layer, the documents, the decisions. Every department's records, one screen.

**Card: By Department.** The city manager sees across everything. Development services sees the pipeline. Finance sees the money. Residents see what's theirs. Same record underneath, so the numbers agree and the map is the same map.

**Card: By Search.** One search across everything your city holds. Revise a document once and it's current everywhere, and see the revision history.

### Zone 3 — The equation

A statement band, set apart visually from the cards:

Every place becomes a smart site. Every document becomes a smart file. Every water main, sidewalk and vehicle becomes a smart asset. That is SmartCity OS.

Vocabulary rules: "smart site," "smart file," and "smart asset" are lowercase unit vocabulary, ratified for city pages (smart asset ratified 2026-08-07). "Water main" is two words. Never present Smart Site as a separate app or product here. The who-sees-what claim lives in Zone 5 on this page, not in the equation.

### Zone 4 — How you use it

Section label: HOW YOU USE IT. Three product cards, each carrying its verb and pointing to its own one-pager.

**Card: Dashboards — see it.** Your whole city in one place, with each department seeing what is theirs, every view drawn from the same record. A complete, deployable system on day one.

**Card: Plan Review — decide on it.** Submittals arrive pre-reviewed against your city's own adopted code, cited section by section. Your reviewer's judgment always governs.

**Card: Asset Management — own it.** Every physical asset your city tracks, water mains to sidewalks to vehicles, takes its place in the same record. Built with you, around what you already have.

Voice rule for the Asset Management card: offer-voice only, never inventory-voice (never imply assets are already under management anywhere, never a count). The "All departments. All assets. One screen." banner lives on the Asset Management one-pager, not here.

### Zone 5 — Results band

**Header (two lines):** Results You Can See / Answers You Can Check

**Body:** One view of the whole city, and numbers that agree. Staff answering a question once instead of four times. Submittals arriving pre-checked, so more review stays in-house and the queue moves. Records that hold up years later, and knowledge that stays when people retire. Every answer carries its source and its date. Open any number and see where it came from. And the record is yours: you decide who sees what, and it stays.

Discipline note: every expectation in this band is a mechanism outcome from a register; no figure, percentage, or measured result may ever be added to it. "Answers you can check" is cashed by the source-and-date sentence; do not change one without the other.

### Zone 6 — Proof

Live with the City of Bastrop, Texas. City leadership uses it day to day.

### Zone 7 — Footer

**CTA:** Schedule a demonstration.

For the Vertosoft catalog card, the listing title follows the storefront's naming grammar: SmartCity OS for Public Sector. The catalog short description draws from Zone 2's three-doors language.
