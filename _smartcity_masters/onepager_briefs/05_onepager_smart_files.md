# Smart Files — one-pager brief

Not a fourth category. Smart Files is the part of the foundation a city touches directly, and it is the most immediately legible thing in the whole offering: every person in a city hall has lost a document, found four versions of it, or updated one copy of five. This page can be the warmest in the set.

Whether Smart Files gets its own one-pager or stays a band on the overview page is a layout call. If produced, this is the copy.

## Audience and job

City manager and everyone in the building. Job: instant recognition of the file problem, and the two promises: revise once and it is current everywhere, and what it was before is still there. Plus ownership, stated plainly.

## Page anatomy and copy

### Hero

**Headline:** Search Everything Your City Holds, From One Place

**Subhead:** A document lives once and shows up everywhere it belongs. Revise it once and it is current everywhere at once, and the previous version is still there when you need it. Your data is yours.

### Pain strip

Four systems and a shared drive. Five copies of the same document, and nobody sure which one is current. The version somebody updated last year that never made it into the packet.

### What it does: three cards

**Card 1: Find anything, from one place.** One search across everything your city holds, instead of four systems and a shared drive.

**Card 2: Revise once, current everywhere.** A change lands everywhere that document appears. Nothing is silently lost; what it was before is still there.

**Card 3: It is yours.** Your data belongs to you, and you are not locked to us or to any single host.

### Assistant band (optional)

An assistant that follows you through the system and can actually answer, because everything underneath it was built to be read.

That sentence exactly, nothing more. Do not describe features of the assistant, claim a specific interface, or promise a ship date.

### One-record band

**Header:** The same foundation everything else reads from.

**Body:** Dashboards, Plan Review, and Asset Management all draw from the same record Smart Files searches. Revise a document once and it is current everywhere it is referenced.

### Footer

**CTA:** Schedule a demonstration to see Smart Files on your city's documents.

## Claims discipline for this page

Not approved, do not print: any statement that records are on distributed or content-addressed storage today, or the words IPFS, content-addressed, or distributed storage at all. Any name for the foundation, or any diagram of it. Any claim the reworked assistant sidebar is shipped. Any framing of the system learning from the city's staff. Everything on the never-say list in `00_onepager_system.md`.
