# The smart-site contribution economy

Operator vision, captured for the roadmap. Not scheduled; OPS-10 (the internal flag rail) is the near-term substrate it will stand on.

## The product vision

People "claim their smartsite" — the owner or steward of an addressable place takes an attributed relationship to its digital twin — and get PAID to contribute to the data: correcting a misread setback, confirming a boundary, updating what a worker changed on the property. The compensation rail reuses an existing token a partner company has already created and uses for digital-twin contributions (their live case: a worker who performs work on a property is compensated for updating the twin). We would reuse that token and apply it here rather than inventing a new instrument.

The pipeline shape is already real in miniature: OPS-10's flag rail (find issue → flag → note → ledger → isolated fix → resolution) is the uncompensated v1 of the exact contribution loop. Claiming adds identity and standing; the token adds the reward; the Warden and the calibration layer add verification so rewards attach to VERIFIED contributions, not raw submissions (the same earn-vs-assert discipline as commitment #2 — a contribution's value is established the way an atom's confidence is).

## The thesis (operator, captured 2026-08-04)

AI in society will be deflationary. As AI-powered tech (and its translation into robotics) compresses the cost of cognitive and physical work, the question is how people participate in the upside rather than only absorbing the displacement. The operator's thesis: REWARDS PROGRAMS are a clean mechanism for humans to participate in the upside of training and refining AI systems — a way society can meet the deflation. People contribute ground truth (corrections, confirmations, updates — the judgment calls machines get wrong, like a flag lot's rear-vs-side backing), the systems get measurably better, and the contributors share in the value created, paid through an instrument designed for exactly this. The smart-site map is a natural first surface: physical-world ground truth, verifiable contributions, an existing token rail, and a feedback loop (discrepancy patterns → engine fixes → calibration gains) whose value is measurable enough to fund the rewards.

This composes with the standing catalog thesis: the moat is compounding calibrated reasoning; human contributions are a calibration source; the contribution economy is arrow two (calibration capture) extended to the public, with payment closing the loop.

## What this is not

Not a now-build; not a commitment to any token economics before counsel and bizops review; not public flagging before the internal rail proves signal quality.
