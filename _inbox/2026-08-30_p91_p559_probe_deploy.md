# What this image measures

The boot strip gains three tokens. `net=` probes four origins by fetch, cors first then no-cors (`ok<status>` reachable with CORS, `opq` reachable without CORS, `blk` blocked, `to` timeout): the Esri World_Imagery tile server, the hauska-map-tiles GCS bucket, services7.arcgis.com, and our own `mcp.smartsite.cloud`. `gl=` reports webgl2, webgl1, or none (MapLibre needs WebGL; a sandboxed iframe may not have it, and nobody had measured it). `bridge=` sends an app-initiated `resources/read` for a registered probe resource through the host rpc and reports ok, an error code, timeout, or nohost. The board resource declares the four origins in `_meta.ui.csp.connectDomains` and `resourceDomains`, previously empty arrays, so the run measures the declared case, which is the case that matters for a live map.

Read-only: no app behavior changes; the probe paints into the strip only.
