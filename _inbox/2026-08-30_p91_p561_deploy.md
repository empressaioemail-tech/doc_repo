# What this image serves

More than one parcel on one ground.

Batch anchors. A node-depth array now reads one facets call per parcel for the first twelve ids, concurrently. Every row carries its own `anchorRead`, including the rows past the cap, and a top-level `anchorBatch` names cap, received, attempted and notAttempted, so a truncated batch declares itself instead of resembling an absence. The stub path is untouched and the node batch cap of 25 is unchanged.

The canvas. Each parcel is composed from its own anchor through the same `ringFit`, `ringPixel` and `groundPlan` the single-parcel view uses, so imagery registration is inherited rather than reimplemented and no shared origin is invented. Every drawn ring is clickable and drafts the ordinary Open turn for that parcel, per the standing ruling that a click drafts a message and the user sends it.

The honesty rules, which are the point of the card. A parcel that cannot be drawn, for want of an anchor or a ring, is NAMED beside the canvas with its reason and never silently omitted; a canvas quietly showing four of seven parcels is the defect class this operation keeps finding, and it is invisible from the outside. Below two drawable parcels there is no canvas and the panel falls back to today's behaviour. Above a 5,280 ft extent the rings paint without imagery rather than stretching a tile mosaic across a county. No aggregate is invented anywhere.
