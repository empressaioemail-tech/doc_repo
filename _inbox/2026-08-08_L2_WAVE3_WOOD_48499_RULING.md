# Wood County 48499 — ruling

**Ruling: PARK. Wood 48499 is withheld from the store as an explicit, named absence.** It is not silently dropped, and the Texas WGS84 envelope guard is not weakened, loosened, or bypassed.

## What the source actually contains (measured, not inferred)

Read-only probe of the live StratMap archive
`https://data.geographic.texas.gov/0fa04328-872e-481c-b453-126a74777593/resources/stratmap25-landparcels_48499_lp.zip`
(HTTP 200, 21,701,917 bytes) with a full per-record scan of the shapefile geometry headers.

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Total SHP records | 44,576 |
| Records outside the Texas WGS84 envelope | **1** |
| Corrupt record | recNum 43505 (zero-based index 43504) |
| Corrupt record bbox | -96.1197, **13.9197**, -96.1184, 13.9237 |
| Bbox of the other 44,575 records | -95.6686, 32.5410, -95.1439, 33.0023 |
| Declared PRJ | `GEOGCS["GCS_WGS_1984", ...]` — geographic degrees, correct |

Wood County's true extent is approximately lng -95.80 to -95.13, lat 32.60 to 32.94. The 44,575 good records sit inside that. The single bad record sits at latitude 13.92, in the Pacific Ocean off Central America.

## The corrupt record is a null placeholder, not a property

DBF attributes for record 43505:

GEO_ID      : ""

SITUS_ADDR  : ", ,"

`Prop_ID = "0"` with empty owner, empty legal description, empty situs address, and zero market value. This is a placeholder or digitizing artifact in the county's published layer, not a real parcel. No customer-facing property record is lost by withholding it.

## Why the guard is correct and stays untouched

The guard's own doc comment in `lib/cad-ingest/src/txgio/parse.ts` states its design premise:

> "This throws rather than counting a skip. A projection error is a WHOLE-COUNTY property, not a per-feature data defect; skipping would silently load zero or a handful of rows and report success."

That premise is sound for the failure it was written against (a projected CRS, a missing `.prj`, swapped axes) and it is the only thing standing between a corrupt coordinate and the store. Wood is a case the premise does not cover: a genuine per-feature source defect inside an otherwise correctly projected county, confirmed by measurement at 1 bad record in 44,576 with a valid geographic `.prj`. That is a finding about the guard's coverage, not a defect in the guard, and not a licence to relax it.

## Why park rather than carve

Three options were available: ingest with the corrupt feature declined as a named absence; ingest partially and record the exclusion; or park.

Both ingest options require a per-feature exclusion path that does not exist in the CLI today. Building one means new code in `lib/cad-ingest` on the acquisition-critical path, and this session holds **no merge and no deploy authority**. Shipping an unreviewed exclusion branch to make one county land would trade a fail-closed guarantee for a convenience, on exactly the code path where the guarantee matters most. Wood is 44,575 parcels of a roughly 9.3 million row store; the cost of waiting is small and the cost of a bad precedent is not.

Parking keeps the store honest: Wood reads as absent, not as present-and-complete, and not as present-minus-one-silently.

## What unparks Wood

Either of these, in order of preference:

1. **Publisher fix.** StratMap reissues the 48499 layer with record 43505 corrected or removed. Re-probe the archive; if the scan returns zero out-of-envelope records, Wood ingests through the unmodified guard with no code change at all.
2. **Reviewed per-feature exclusion.** A merged, reviewed change to `lib/cad-ingest` that declines an individual feature as a counted, named, artifact-recorded exclusion — never a silent skip — while leaving `assertTexasWgs84Bbox` fail-closed for whole-county projection errors. The distinguishing test must be explicit: a county whose records are *predominantly* out of envelope still throws; a county with a bounded handful of defective records records them as declined. That change needs its own review and its own test, not a wave-time patch.

Until one of those lands, Wood 48499 is absent by ruling, with this document as the reason.

## Finding

**W3-WOOD-48499 (source defect, open).** StratMap `stratmap25-landparcels_48499_lp` contains one placeholder record (`Prop_ID=0`, OBJECTID_1 30421) whose geometry is at latitude 13.92. The publisher's SHP header bbox is polluted by it (`ymin` 13.9197). Reported as a source-quality defect; no workaround applied.

**W3-GUARD-COVERAGE (design finding, open).** The envelope guard treats every out-of-envelope coordinate as a whole-county projection failure. Measurement shows per-feature source defects also exist. The guard's fail-closed behaviour is correct as a default; the gap is that it offers no reviewed path to decline a bounded, named set of defective features. Recorded for a future reviewed change, not acted on here.
