# Tier-3 municipal wedge — thesis frame

Built in a strategy-first discussion 2026-07-31. Altitude-only; deliberately no SmartCity architecture yet.

## THE FRAME (settled at altitude)

TIER 3 = a SECOND, PARALLEL WEDGE (municipal), distinct from the RE-pro wedge (PE→MCP→plan-review). Not a reframe of the RE wedge; two wedges into the same spine.

| | RE-pro wedge (Tier 1) | MUNICIPAL wedge (Tier 3) |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer | the professional (agent/architect/investor) | the CITY (Bastrop, via SmartCity) |
| Value | "the buildable answer, cited" — decision support | "your civic reality, durable + authoritative + access-controlled" — SYSTEM OF RECORD |
| Data flow | we read public records → serve pros | cities' OWN data (utilities/infra/permits) → RBAC'd back to them + authorized parties |
| Moat | compounding cited reasoning | being the SUBSTRATE OF RECORD — switching cost is existential once records live on it |
| Unlocks | MCP agent market | tokenization base layer + municipal-records durable-environment + plan-review distribution |

## THE LOAD-BEARING INSIGHTS

1. RBAC IS WHAT TURNS "MORE LAYERS" INTO "SYSTEM OF RECORD." A public parcel layer is a product. Bastrop's sewer network — visible only to Bastrop public works + contractors they authorize, each asset carrying provenance + edit history — is infrastructure a city can't easily leave, because it becomes THEIR operational truth, not a copy. This is TENANT-SOVEREIGNTY (constitution) becoming the PRODUCT, not just a compliance rule.

2. THE TOKENIZATION / "ON-CHAIN RECORDS" CONNECTION resolves to ONE role: we are the verifiable-attestation / durable-record substrate; someone else does the token/legal/chain wrapper. "On-chain" is the word people reach for because they lack a durable-authoritative-record system — we ARE that better system. A city doesn't need a blockchain to make permit records durable+authoritative; it needs our substrate (provenance/confidence/timestamp/RBAC/immutable-audit). Same for RE tokenization: the token needs verifiable asset-truth underneath = us. WE DON'T BUILD TOKENIZATION; we make ourselves the thing every tokenization/on-chain effort NEEDS underneath.
   - HOW FAR our role goes (substrate-only vs substrate+attestation-to-chain-bridge vs further) is UNDECIDED — flagged as THE core deep-discussion item; depends on PE/spine decisions.

3. BASTROP = FIRST CITY ON THE SUBSTRATE / reference twin (pioneer framing, standing decision, now applied to INFRA not just codes). Its infra layers (water/sewer/utility/info — more available) become the proof that "your civic reality, RBAC'd + durable" works.

## THE TWO OPEN QUESTIONS (both entangled with SmartCity — cannot set in isolation)

Q1. TOKEN/RECORD ROLE BOUNDARY — substrate-only vs substrate+attestation-bridge vs further. UNDECIDED. Depends on PE/spine decisions AND on the buyer relationship (which runs through SmartCity).

Q2. POSITIONING SPEARHEAD — utility/infra-twin vs municipal-records-durable-environment vs unified-civic-base-layer. NOT SET: operator flagged it "bleeds into overall SmartCity positioning." The spine's municipal story must be COHERENT WITH SmartCity's existing position to cities, not invented alongside it.

## THE FRAME'S OWN CONCLUSION
We've framed as far as honest at altitude. Both open questions stand on ground we don't have yet: SmartCity's existing municipal position + the buyer relationship that runs through it. NEXT: bring in the SmartCity picture (WHAT it is + how it's positioned to cities — the positioning/identity layer, NOT the deep onto-spine architecture yet). Then set positioning coherently-with-SmartCity + see the buyer relationship the token-role question depends on. (SmartCity-onto-spine ARCHITECTURE is a later beat, after the WHAT is grounded.)
