# What this image serves

An anchor on the wire and aerial imagery under the parcel drawing, both built inside `artifacts/smartsite-mcp` with no cortex change.

The anchor comes from `cityLimitsFact.queryPoint` on the anonymous cortex facets route, read through the existing client concurrently with the brief and bounded at 2,000 ms, and emitted as `anchor` plus `anchorRead` beside `draw` on single-id node results only. It never emits a coordinate it did not read: no default, no 0,0, no centroid guess. A sentinel, a missing point, a non-JSON body, an upstream non-OK or a timeout is a declared absence carrying a reason and no `anchor` key, and none of them can fail the panel. The attach step deletes any `anchor` the upstream carried before writing its own, so the only coordinate that can leave this server is one the anchor module read.

The ground is an underlay of Esri World Imagery tiles behind the existing SVG drawing, computed rather than delegated: no map library, no pan, no zoom, and the drawing itself unchanged. Registration is tautological rather than asserted, because the anchor pixel and the ring origin pixel are the same call. Mercator carries its `cos(latitude)` term and feet convert by the US survey factor the frame declares, so a ring matches the roof beneath it. Esri tile paths are z/y/x. Zoom stops at 19 because Esri answers an over-zoomed request with a placeholder image, which is the grey box standing in for imagery that this panel must never paint. Any `anchorRead` status other than `ok` paints today's void ground. The imagery is labelled with its source and states its vintage as unstated, because Esri publishes no per-tile capture date.

One rendering deviation, declared: with the ground on, the ring's void fill drops from 55 percent to 16 percent, because a 55 percent scrim hides the roof the ground exists to show. It is one CSS line scoped to `[data-ground="on"]`; with the ground off or absent the drawing is byte identical to p558.
