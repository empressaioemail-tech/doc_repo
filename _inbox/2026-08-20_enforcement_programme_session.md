# Session Capture

## What was asked for

An architecture for the path from raw sources to atoms, across two substrates running the same thesis: a property substrate and a markets substrate. A reference for how it should work, and a diagnostic the operator's agents could score the current implementation against, so friction could be located rather than guessed at.

## What happened instead

The reference was produced. The diagnostic was produced. Running it established that the diagnostic could not be trusted, and the session went into the ability to measure rather than the pipeline itself.

That detour was correct and it should be named as a detour. The map originally asked for is roughly half complete on both substrates and is the last item in both queues.

## The governing finding

Every defect found across both substrates is one shape: a validity check that can be satisfied by a value carrying no meaning.

Presence is not validity, and a check whose predicate is shaped like presence cannot measure a property shaped like meaning.

The methodological consequence governed the rest of the session. Measuring output applies the same predicates that admitted the defect, so the instrument and the disease share a definition of valid. Where a code reading test and an output measuring test disagree, the code reading test is correct.

## The three findings that justified the detour

No repository in the estate carried branch protection. Every continuous integration check anywhere ran, reported accurately, and blocked nothing. Every green that gated a merge was a courtesy.

A live unauthenticated endpoint served a wrong flood hazard determination, wrong in 5,714 of 5,714 adjudicated cases, including 1,995 parcels told they were outside a hazard area they were inside. Closed in production during the session.

A capture path wrote atoms storing formatted display strings where numbers belonged, with no revision state, so no correction job could ever repair a row already written. Halted, reshaped, and resumed correctly.

## What landed

**Property substrate.** Flood exposure closed in production. Branch protection Stage 1 applied across six repositories and verified by refused pushes rather than by a settings endpoint. Stage 2 reliability report delivered with the check name error caught before application, which would otherwise have jammed every pull request in the estate.

**Markets substrate.** Seven merges. A record kind split off a transform enum by type rather than by detector. A second starved detector deleted and replaced by types. A deployment gate with two party attestation. The sixth state made self reporting. The unrepairable capture halted and reshaped to store readings rather than renderings. A false green corrected within a day of appearing.

**Systems.** 135 dead branches removed with a verified restorable bundle. Two hooks scoped to the repository a command mutates, closing an over reach and a silent miss with one fix. A deletion log built so a destructive action cannot recur unattributed. Fleet inventory established with its uncovered set named.

**Trading.** Three live risk questions answered by tracing rather than assertion, and a deploy of thirteen commits authorised on the strength of it.

## The doctrine produced

Six documents in the OPS folder. 51 is the pipeline reference and diagnostic battery. 61 is the enforcement doctrine. 62 is the seat topology. 90 is the build order. 91 is the branch protection runbook. The vehicles document explains how rules reach a session.

The states a control can occupy, all catalogued from instances: dormant, starved, undeployed, scope narrow, scope broad, advisory, and one that reports the wrong verdict because its predicate tests an adjacent proposition.

## The two practices worth keeping

**Pre-registration.** Naming, before producing work, the specific ways that work is expected to be wrong, then checking each. It caught a real defect in its author's own output the first time it was used, and later caught an error in a dispatch rather than in the work.

**Instrument self audit.** Auditing your own instrument under the rules you are applying to the subject. It is the only thing that caught anything in this programme's tooling. Four defects in one deployment gate, three wrong structural answers from text searches, two false positive classes from a syntax tree rule. Every one found by the author turning the criteria inward.

## The uncomfortable result

Two defects were caught externally. Continuous integration found two things an author had already declared verified, and found them because its clone depth happened to differ from that author's environment. A background search confirmed a negative because its author had forgotten it was running.

Both real, both load bearing, neither designed. Once accidents are excluded, this programme's external detection rate is approximately zero. Self audit is not a supplement to review here; it is the primary mechanism and review catches the residue.

## What the programme actually bought

Product improvement is close to zero. One wrong answer removed from production.

Everything else is instruments and hygiene. The honest framing, produced by the markets seat and adopted: a substrate that reports its own state honestly, not one that works better.

## Corrections that landed after this capture was first written

Numbers this session repeated that were wrong, each falsified at source by the seat that inherited them.

The geometry ledger population is 254, not 253. One row per Texas county, no non county entities. The 253 belonged to a different rail and travelled by inheritance, repeated by analyst and seat alike for weeks.

The flood store disagreement is not a subtype quibble. Of 37,331 disagreements across 533,867 comparisons, 129 are the zone subtype case everyone was citing. 36,723 are one store naming a hazard zone while the other says outside it, which is in or out of a hazard area. The carried memory was aimed at a real case and wrong about its size.

Micro futures were not absent from the point value table, which holds 51 keys. The figure of 28 came from a different dictionary.

The calibration magnitude was not fiction. Point value and quantity cancel exactly in the counterfactual ratio, so both populations were clean and no historical repair is owed.

A markets row count of 513 sums two predicates rather than being one number, and its file list reached under a fifth of the predicate's tree wide scope. Corrected range 321 to 427.

An unresolved binding figure of 46 million was an artifact of a query the analyst specified badly, conflating key shape mismatch with orphaning across families where one parcel legitimately carries many rows. A real orphan population likely hides inside it and this query cannot size it. Sizing it requires a per family key grammar that does not exist, which is the actual prerequisite for the binding constraint.

There is no parcel node table. Node type is an entity type inside the atom table, so the database half of write time binding validation cannot be built as specified, because a foreign key has nothing to reference.

## The finding that outranks everything else in this capture

A universe synchronisation path mass delisted an entire trading universe from a single vendor outage, setting every symbol outside the macro roots inactive and closing its alias eras, and returned success.

A durable destructive mutation, driven by a fabricated empty, reported as available. It was not on any list, it was found during the final pass, and it is the governing defect at its most expensive: a fail open producing not a wrong number but an irreversible write.

## What is open

**Product.** Drivers on the markets side, which is one contract change and one adapter, and takes the twin from three unserved layers to two. The flood chain on the property side, which is a fail open hazard flag, a corpus stamp, and a consumer repoint.

**Enumeration.** Roughly half complete on both substrates. On property, 48 distinct verified checks with three holding and only one carrying a second derivation. On markets, a candidate count resting on an instrument audited once.

**Structural.** Write time binding validation, absent at every layer on both substrates and confirmed against the live store. Stage 2 required checks. A geometry scorer re-derivation whose producer was never committed to any repository.

**Purchasing.** Effectively nothing. The markets enumeration classified 185 rows with zero genuinely absent, so its purchasing list is empty. On property one candidate remains, federal hazard data at parcel resolution rather than tile. Everything else that looked like a purchase resolved to a control decision, a type change, or a claim about our own process with no external witness by construction.

## Operator items

Serialise repository writes or give each seat its own worktree and branch. Three concurrent write events in one day, two of which could have destroyed work. A topology and an implementation dispatch exist for this.

Expect a visible product change when the flood consumer repoint lands, and the number is now known. 4,354,603 parcels statewide carry no geometry, which is 26.5 per cent, and that is the unmeasurable population before anything is stamped. Data disappears wherever containment cannot be verified. That is the product becoming honest and it will read as a regression to anyone who does not know it is coming.

The doctrine document itself is untracked in git, in two divergent versions, one newer and shorter and one older and more complete. Every other document citing it cites something a fresh clone does not contain. Decide which version is canonical and track it.

## The analyst's errors, recorded because the record is the point

Ordered a deletion of a note from a prior seat's summary of it, without reading the note. It documented the gap correctly and deleting it would have destroyed the evidence trail for a re-derivation ordered in the same message.

Told a seat to check a function on the strength of its name, in a dispatch instructing them not to do that. The function had zero production callers.

Asserted a property of a log without reading it, and told a seat to count it before a fix destroyed a distinction the log never recorded.

Called a two instance observation the strongest cross substrate finding of the programme, wrote it into canon as established, and had it falsified by a four schema test one exchange after owning the same error.

Overstated a seat's contribution in a summary and was corrected by that seat, on the grounds that a seat letting its own contribution be overstated corrupts the calibration signal the programme exists to earn.

Inferred the ordering of two returns from the order they were pasted and reported a property of a seat's work based on it.

Gave a check name that did not exist, which would have jammed every pull request in the estate had a seat not verified it against the repository.

Every one of these is the governing defect, arriving in the analyst rather than the substrate.
