# CTX card E: the bake projects the facet set; the reader is not assumed to be the gap

Date: 2026-08-28  Status: approved

The Central Texas cutover is serving a thinner brief than the old shape did, and the walk could not see it. The conformant tier 1 bake writes the base facets and nothing else; the old bake joined the zoning stamp, derived the envelope, and carried the CAD fields; the Property Explorer renders from those keys and shows `not verified here` where they are missing. The operator asked whether the UI must change. This card answers with evidence, fixes the bake, and makes the difference between old and new a test.
