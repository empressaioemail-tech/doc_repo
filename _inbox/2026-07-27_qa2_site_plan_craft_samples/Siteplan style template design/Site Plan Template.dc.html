2c GENERATOR INSTRUCTION BLOCK
1 · SHEET
Letter portrait, 8.5×11 in, ground neutral-100. Always two pages: p1 drawing, p2 summary + provenance. Never a third page, never a bleed. Page margins 48 / 46 / 36 px at 96 dpi; inner gaps from the --space-* scale.
2 · HEADER, BOTH PAGES
Accent kicker (11px, 0.22em, uppercase) → street address in Barlow Condensed 600 uppercase 30px → one 13px line of city / parcel / county. Right of it on p1: three stat columns (LOT, BUILDABLE, ZONING) in Barlow Condensed 600 22px, buildable in accent-700; on p2: sheet number over parcel id, 11px, right-aligned. A 1px full-ink rule closes the header. No frame, no title strip, no boxed block.
3 · LAYER ORDER & WEIGHT
contour → contour labels → envelope fill → setback dash → property line → dimension tags → sheet furniture. The property line is the heaviest stroke on the sheet; nothing may match or exceed it. One accent hue only. Colour never carries meaning alone — every layer also differs in weight or dash.
4 · LABEL COLLISION CASCADE
Bearing/distance tags sit outside the ring, one per segment, rotated to the segment, centred on its midpoint, distance first. On collision, in order: (a) shorten to distance only; (b) move to a leader line into the margin; (c) drop from the drawing and print the full segment table on p2. Never stack, never duplicate a tag. Setback labels print once per unique value per side; identical adjacent values collapse to “SIDE 5' (typ.)”. Suppress any label on an edge shorter than 3× the label. Contour labels: one per contour, in the left margin, never inside the envelope.
5 · EMPTY LAYERS MUST LOOK INTENTIONAL
Every layer appears in the legend, in fixed order, populated or not. An empty layer is greyed to neutral-500 with its reason on the same line (“Street — layer empty · no road node attaches”). Never delete the row, never leave a blank swatch, never draw substitute geometry. If setbacks consume the lot, draw no envelope and state the reason in the legend and as an UNAVAILABLE row on p2.
6 · UNAVAILABLE VALUES
A solid ink UNAVAILABLE chip plus one plain sentence of reason, sentence case, max 12 words. Never “N/A”, never an empty cell, never a fabricated value. Fixture or provisional values keep their real value and take a FIXTURE LABEL chip instead.
7 · SUMMARY PAGE
Two-column key/value on a 200px label column, hairline rule per row, grouped under PARCEL, ZONING & BUILDABILITY, SITE CONDITIONS in that order, max 5 rows each; group headings in accent, 10.5px, 0.2em. Values carry their unit plus at most one grey qualifier inline; qualifiers never become their own row. Numbers tabular. Provenance follows as a three-column table (layer, source, confidence) using the system table style.
8 · FINE PRINT
Bottom of each page, 8.2px, neutral-600, one paragraph. Always: derived from public GIS records / not a boundary survey / not for legal record / tags GIS-approximate. Honest-absence and synthetic-fixture disclosures append to that same paragraph — no callout boxes on the drawing.
9 · SHEET FURNITURE
North arrow top-right inside the drawing area, filled triangle over a condensed “N” — no compass rose. Scale bar bottom-right: three segments, 0 / half / full, unit on the last label, with scale, sheet number and generated stamp beneath it. Legend bottom-left on the same baseline, two columns. Lot-area callout centred in the envelope, condensed uppercase over a 2.5-unit grey qualifier; suppress it if the envelope is narrower than 40 label-widths.
10 · NEVER
No fabricated geometry. No rounded corners, no drop shadows in print, no gradients, no emoji, no second accent hue, no italic body text, no hard-coded hex, font name or px where a token exists.
