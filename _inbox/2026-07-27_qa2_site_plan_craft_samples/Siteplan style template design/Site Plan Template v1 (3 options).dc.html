1e GENERATOR INSTRUCTION BLOCK
1 · SHEET
Letter portrait, 8.5×11 in. Always two pages: p1 drawing, p2 summary + provenance. Never a third page, never a bleed. Margins from the spacing scale, not ad-hoc numbers.
2 · HEADER BAND (TOP, BOTH PAGES)
Left: sheet kicker in accent, then the street address as the largest string in Barlow Condensed, then county / parcel / zoning on one line. Right: scale, sheet number, generated timestamp (ISO, UTC), revision. No right-side title strip.
3 · LAYER ORDER & WEIGHT
contour → envelope fill → setback dash → property line → labels → sheet furniture. The property line is always the heaviest stroke on the sheet; nothing may match or exceed it. One accent hue only — steel. Colour never carries meaning alone; every layer also differs in weight or dash.
4 · LABEL COLLISION CASCADE
Bearing/distance tags sit outside the ring, one per segment, rotated to the segment, centred on its midpoint. On collision, in order: (a) shorten to distance only; (b) move to a leader line into the margin; (c) drop from the drawing and print the full segment table on p2. Never stack, never duplicate a tag. Setback labels print once per unique value per side; identical adjacent values collapse to “SIDE 5' (typ.)”. Suppress any label on an edge shorter than 3× the label. Contour labels: one per contour, in the left margin, never inside the envelope.
5 · EMPTY LAYERS MUST LOOK INTENTIONAL
Every layer appears in the legend, in fixed order, populated or not. An empty layer is greyed to neutral-500 with its reason on the same line (“Street — layer empty · no road node attaches”). Never delete the row, never leave a blank swatch, never draw substitute geometry. If setbacks consume the lot, draw no envelope and state the reason in the legend and as an UNAVAILABLE row on p2.
6 · UNAVAILABLE VALUES
A solid ink UNAVAILABLE chip plus one plain sentence of reason, sentence case, max 12 words. Never “N/A”, never an empty cell, never a fabricated value.
7 · SUMMARY PAGE
Two-column key/value on a 200 px label column, hairline rule per row, grouped under PARCEL, ZONING & BUILDABILITY, SITE CONDITIONS in that order, max 5 rows each. Values carry their unit plus at most one grey qualifier inline; qualifiers never become their own row. Provenance follows as a three-column table: layer, source, confidence.
8 · FINE PRINT
Bottom of each page, outside the drawing frame, 8 px, neutral-600, one paragraph. Always: derived from public GIS records / not a boundary survey / not for legal record / tags GIS-approximate. Honest-absence and synthetic-fixture disclosures append to that same paragraph — no callout boxes on the drawing.
9 · SHEET FURNITURE
North arrow top-right inside the drawing area. Scale bar bottom-right: three segments, 0 / half / full, unit on the last label. Legend bottom-left on the same baseline. Lot-area callout centred in the envelope; suppress it if the envelope is narrower than 40 label-widths. Framed sheets carry the four registration marks — never drop them.
10 · NEVER
No fabricated geometry. No rounded corners, no surface fills on framed objects, no drop shadows on the sheet, no gradients, no emoji, no second accent hue, no italic body text, no compass rose with cardinal text, no hard-coded hex or px where a token exists.
