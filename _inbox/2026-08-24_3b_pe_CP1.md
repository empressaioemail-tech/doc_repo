## Executive summary

Today every purchasable CTA ends in **`window.location.assign(checkoutUrl)`** to hosted Stripe. `billingClient.ts` speaks **`successUrl` / `cancelUrl`** and treats **`checkoutUrl`** as the only success signal on the subscription path. There is **no** `/checkout` route, **no** Payment Element, **no** `@stripe/stripe-js` dependency, and **no** CSP allowlist for `js.stripe.com` in-repo. Post-return UX is a thin **`checkout-reconcile-banner`** ("Confirming your purchase…") with no 3b success card, no timeout copy, and no queued-report kick. Implementation can proceed on routing, left-column markup, appearance object, and tests that do not need a live secret; **mounting Stripe UI must wait** until cortex item 1 returns `clientSecret` + `publishableKey` on `uiMode: "custom"`.
