Layout law (do not break)

  1. The map is the product. Every tool opens over it and closes back to it. Never a split screen. Never a second permanent panel.
  2. One dock. Opening a bubble replaces the dock. Tapping the active bubble closes it.
  3. Inspect card is top-left and free. Find bar is top-center. Workbench bubbles are top-right. Map utilities are bottom-right. Logo chip is bottom-left.
  4. Inspect actions: Research, Save, Make subject. Do not add a fourth CTA on the card (Share and Use in your AI live in chrome, not on inspect).
  5. Locked tools show a value line plus View pricing & unlock. Pricing never lives in the dock.
  6. Honest absence is slate, hatched, named. Errors are red. Warnings stay amber. Do not collapse these three.
  7. Provenance is a disclosure, not a shout on the card face. Citations are blue links. Atom chips are teal.
  8. Dark only. System UI. Oxygen never loaded. Do not add a display face.
