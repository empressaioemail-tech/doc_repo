# Central Texas go-to-market strategy

## The frame

Central Texas is a targeting decision, not a coverage claim. Smart Site's coverage is spoken of as nationwide with per-place honesty inside the product, and that rule is not weakened here. What Central Texas gives us is the place where the depth is deepest, the honest-absence rate is lowest, the founder's own network is thickest, and the one genuinely regional story we can tell is strongest. We point the channels there because that is where a first user has the best chance of a good first answer, not because the product stops at the county line.

Operationally Central Texas is the six counties running the conformant serving shape: Bastrop 48021, Travis 48453, Williamson 48491, Hays 48209, Caldwell 48055, and McLennan 48309. Five of those are one metropolitan and media market. McLennan is Waco, which is a separate media market with separate groups, separate agents, and separate influencers, and it dilutes any affiliate targeting that tries to hold both. The recommendation below treats the Austin metro five as the launch target and holds Waco as the first expansion proof, because proving the playbook transfers to a second market is worth more than five percent more reach in the first one.

## What we actually serve there, stated honestly

The six counties serve the conformant tier-1 shape from walked production publishes. They are not county-complete. As of the 2026-08-30 measurement there were 232,770 parcels unstamped and Travis carried 119,389 rows with no situs row at all. Those numbers move and must be re-measured before any external use; the point that survives re-measurement is structural. A meaningful share of Central Texas parcels will return an honest absence rather than a full answer, and the product is built to say so.

This is a marketing constraint and also the marketing asset. Every competitor in this category returns a confident answer whether or not it has one. Declared absence is the differentiator that the whole enforcement doctrine exists to protect, and it is the thing a professional buyer will recognize as the mark of a real instrument. The campaign leads with it rather than apologizing for it.

The second hard constraint is the report menu. Live generate today is the X-ray and Flood and Drainage. Feasibility and Comparison are locked into the menu but are not live generate paths and may not be pitched as live. Every campaign asset, affiliate kit, and social post in this plan is built on the two that work.

## The wedge for this market

Flood and drainage is the Central Texas story and it is the one nobody else tells with citations. Central Texas has real and recurring flood salience, the Flood and Drainage report is one of the two live generate paths, and the question it answers is one a buyer feels before they feel any other question about a parcel. It is also seasonal, which gives the content calendar a natural rhythm rather than a manufactured one.

The X-ray is the general-purpose wedge and carries the constraints-and-buildability story: what governs this parcel, where the envelope is, what the setbacks are, cited to an ordinance section. Austin's infill and unit-count rules make that question unusually live in Travis County right now, and the growth corridor through Hays, Williamson, and Bastrop makes it live for land buyers. The specific regulatory hooks in each city need verification against the corpus before they enter copy; they are a research item, not an approved claim.

## Who buys

The locked ladder already resolved the audience question and it does not get re-derived here. Investor, land flipper, and agent are one audience, not three. They ask the same questions, they buy Solo, and affiliate targeting treats them as one segment. The real division is the professionals who need deliverables, who buy Studio.

| Segment | Buys | The question they arrive with | The Central Texas shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| Investor, flipper, agent | Solo $49 | Can I add a unit, does it pencil, what kills this deal | Austin infill and the Hays, Williamson, Bastrop growth corridor |
| Architect, designer, builder | Studio $129 | Give me a site base I can draw on, cited, plus who owns it | High volume of Austin infill and ADU design work |
| Firms | Team $299 | The same, for three or more people, on one bill | Small Austin design and investment shops |
| Homeowner and share recipient | Free, then the $15 unlock | What can I do with my lot, and is this flood thing real | The share loop's natural landing point |

The tier each segment is sold into was re-cut and RULED 2026-08-31: Solo answers one parcel, Studio works a list of them. Screens and boards move to Studio, the records package becomes a visible row, the comparison surface regroups by job, Studio goes to two seats, and Prospect is redefined as monitoring. Prices are untouched. Ladder at `03_ladder_recut_proposal.md`, decision at `_decisions/2026-08-31_smartsite_ladder_recut_studio_works_a_list.md`, implementation at OPS-16 P-101.

The consequence for this plan is that the investor, flipper, and agent segment is now sold into Studio rather than parked at Solo, because working a list of deals is that segment's actual job. Affiliate targeting follows the job, not the price.

The free tier and the thirty-day unlock are the on-ramp, and the unlock's expiry is surfaced as a freshness property rather than a paywall. Bastrop repealing its entire zoning code in April is the proof that a forever answer is a promise we cannot keep, and saying so out loud is better positioning than hiding it.

## The three channels

### Affiliate, through PromoteKit

Terms are locked at 20 percent, recurring, capped at twelve months. Attribution is PromoteKit against Stripe subscriptions, and payouts run through PayPal. The mechanical consequence that governs sequencing is that PromoteKit attributes against live Stripe subscriptions, so the affiliate program cannot be tested, let alone launched, before the live switch completes. Recruiting can begin before that; link distribution cannot.

Recruiting affiliates is a human sales motion and it is the one place in the Smart Site go-to-market where human sales is legitimate. The humanless ruling governs how a subscriber buys, not how a partner is signed. That motion runs in GoHighLevel as a pipeline with stages, and it is the primary reason GoHighLevel exists in this stack at launch.

The Central Texas target list is Austin real estate investor groups, Central Texas agent networks and brokerages, land and ranch newsletter and video operators, and ADU and infill builder audiences. Each of those wants a different one-line pitch, and the locked handoff is explicit that an affiliate cannot sell property intelligence. They need one outcome an audience already wants, one line, one demo, one link. That makes the affiliate kit a per-segment deliverable rather than a single document, and the kit's claims come from the masters, never from the affiliate.

Anti-gaming needs a stated rule before the first link goes out: self-referral is not payable, and an affiliate may not make a claim outside the approved register. The second half of that is the one that can actually hurt, because an affiliate promising a valuation, a permit, or a cycle-time saving creates an exposure the product's own honesty discipline exists to prevent.

### The share loop

The share loop works today and carries full fidelity regardless of the recipient's tier. It is the highest-leverage asset in the plan and the cheapest, because it delivers someone who is in a transaction right now with a person who already uses the product, landing on a live analysis instead of a landing page. In Central Texas its natural shapes are an agent sharing with a buyer, an investor sharing with a partner or a lender, and an architect sharing with a client.

What is owed is not capability, it is measurement. Without share-created and share-viewed events and a recipient-to-signup attribution join back to the sharer, the loop cannot be reported next to affiliate performance as the locked handoff requires, and it cannot be distinguished from noise. That is the one item in this plan worth holding a line on, and the reason is not perfectionism: the first sixty days of affiliate traffic is exactly the window where the two channels have to be told apart, and events cannot be backfilled.

The design question underneath is whether the sharer gets anything. The locked ruling says the sharer is doing unpaid marketing and should meet zero friction, which argues for leaving it alone. PromoteKit makes a second option available at no build cost, which is to give every subscriber an affiliate link and turn the share loop into a compensated channel automatically. That is decision 1 below and the recommendation is no at launch.

### Agent distribution, through Claude Sync

Claude Sync shipped and is confirmed working by the operator. The Smart Site MCP serves a thirteen-tool catalog: find_parcel, get_smart_site, list_my_properties, save_property, set_property_status, create_screen, add_to_screen, list_screens, run_report, request_records, check_request, export_instrument, and ask_the_map. Two carry live limitations that any demo must respect: ask_the_map reports not ready, and the near selector on find_parcel shipped non-functional and declared. A demo built on those two would be a fabrication in exactly the class this operation refuses.

The demo-safe path is find a parcel, get its smart site, run a report. That sequence works and it is the most differentiated thing we can show anyone. Nobody else in this category has a working agent connector against cited property records.

The connector is a door, not a tier, and that is now ruled (`_decisions/2026-08-31_smartsite_connector_is_a_door_not_a_tier.md`). It mirrors the app's entitlement gates rather than carrying its own, because the positioning master's "two doors, one truth" claim is broken the moment the door itself is priced. Its commercial job is acquisition and retention, not differentiation, and no campaign in this plan sells the connector as a reason to buy a particular rung.

The honest read on this channel's position in the funnel is that it is not top-of-funnel yet. Discovery requires the directory listings that P-88 covers, and P-88 has not started and is gated on a live probe of the connector. Until a listing exists, Claude Sync is a reason to buy and a reason to stay rather than a way to be found, and it should be pitched that way in the ladder and used as a content asset rather than counted as acquisition. The directory listing is the step that converts it into acquisition, and it is the highest-leverage unbuilt GTM item in the plan.

## The content system

One content system feeds every channel, scheduled from the GoHighLevel social planner, and every asset draws its language from the masters. The never-say list governs without exception: no cycle-time, savings, or ROI figures, no named-competitor attacks, no claim that the system approves, permits, or certifies anything, no valuation, no 3D, and never a lead with digital twin, tokenization, atom, node, graph, or substrate.

Five pillars, in rough order of how much work they do.

Demonstration is the first and the largest. A real Central Texas parcel, what governs it, cited to the section. This is the entire product in one post and it scales without invention because the product produces it.

Flood and drainage is the second, seasonal, regional, and built on a live generate path.

Honest absence is the third and the most contrarian. A post that shows the product declining to answer, and explains why that is the feature, will do more for a professional audience than any capability claim. It is also the thing that makes the first two credible.

The agent workflow is the fourth. Claude answering a Central Texas parcel question with citations, on the demo-safe path.

Audience-specific education is the fifth and feeds the affiliate kits: what an agent should check before listing, what an investor should check before offering, what a designer needs before drawing.

Channel selection and posting cadence get set during the GoHighLevel build, because the planner's supported channels have to be verified at setup rather than assumed.

## Sequencing

The dependency chain is short and strict, and most of the plan is parallel to it.

1. Stripe live activation completes, with the two code fixes that precede it. The retired-pair fallback in the PE client posts a hardcoded Pro tier and would charge a Studio or Team click at the wrong price, and the deployed api/pe-billing.ts function is publicly routable to the same seam. Neither is a dashboard step. Evidence and the ordered checklist are in `_inbox/2026-08-31_p97-stripe_close.json`.
2. The published terms are reconciled with the product before live money. terms.html promises an in-product cancellation flow that does not exist. Either the billing portal is exposed in the PE app or the terms are amended.
3. Funnel, share, and activation instrumentation lands. This is the gate on affiliate spend, not on the affiliate build.
4. PromoteKit is configured and tested against live Stripe, and the payout rail is proven with one real payout.
5. Affiliate links go out to the first recruited partners.

Running in parallel and blocked by none of it: the GoHighLevel buildout, content production, affiliate recruiting into the pipeline, the affiliate kits, and the P-88 directory listing work.

## What this plan does not do

It does not open a second market. It does not pitch Feasibility or Comparison as live. It does not build an affiliate platform, because PromoteKit is the ruled choice. It does not put Smart Site subscribers into a human sales pipeline, which the humanless ruling forbids. It does not enumerate covered jurisdictions in any external artifact. It does not touch the municipal or Solutions motions, which have their own model and their own collateral.

## Operator rulings, 2026-08-31

Decisions 1 to 3 are ruled and binding. Decision record `_decisions/2026-08-31_ctx_gtm_rulings.md`.

1. RULED: affiliate is opt-in with an application. Every subscriber does not automatically receive a link. Automatic links would convert the share loop into a 20 percent recurring cost on referrals the locked ruling already says arrive free, and would change the share gesture from generous to transactional. We pay 20 percent for reach we would not otherwise have. Revisit once the share loop is instrumented and its unpaid performance is measured rather than assumed.

2. RULED: McLennan is held. The launch target is the Austin metro five, Bastrop, Travis, Williamson, Hays, and Caldwell. Serving Waco continues unchanged; targeting it waits, and it becomes the first expansion proof that the playbook transfers to a second media market.

3. RULED: affiliate launch holds on share and funnel instrumentation. This is the only hold in the plan. Recruiting, kits, and content proceed meanwhile.

### Still open

4. What is the one-line outcome each affiliate segment sells? The ladder is now ruled, so the candidates below are re-pointed and ready for an operator ruling. They remain candidates: this is a positioning commitment, and a line entering a kit without approval is a claim nobody ratified.

## Affiliate segment lines — candidates, awaiting ruling on decision 4

Re-pointed 2026-08-31 against the ruled ladder. Every line states an outcome the audience already wants, per the locked rule that an affiliate cannot sell property intelligence. All four are demonstrable on the two live generate paths, X-ray and Flood and Drainage. None carries a number, a saving, or a claim of approval, permitting, or certification.

| Segment | Sold into | Candidate line | Demo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Investor, flipper | Studio | Find out what kills the deal before you make the offer | X-ray on an Austin-area lot: setbacks, envelope, flood, cited |
| Agent, brokerage | Solo, driving Free | Send your client their smart site | Share a live smart site with a client, on a phone |
| Land and ranch | Solo, some Studio | Know what the water does to that land before you drive out to see it | Flood and Drainage plus X-ray on a rural Bastrop or Caldwell tract |
| Builder, designer | Studio | Start with the setbacks already drawn, cited to the ordinance section | X-ray on an infill lot, envelope labeled approximate, then the site plan export |

Two notes that bind whichever lines are chosen. The agent line is the share gesture, which makes affiliate and share the same motion for that segment and is the one place two channels compound rather than compete; that argues for recruiting agents first. And the land line is deliberately built on water rather than on what you can build, because unincorporated Central Texas is legitimately unzoned and a build-focused line is weakest exactly where that audience operates.

## A refused line, recorded so it cannot return

`_inbox/2026-08-10_smartsite_humanless_gtm_handoff.md` offers "get the site base without paying for a survey" as an example affiliate pitch. That line is REFUSED and may not enter any kit, asset, or affiliate communication. The approved answer in `_smartsite_masters/07_smart_site_faq_bizdev.md` is the opposite: envelopes are labeled approximate, not survey grade, and Smart Site is "the fastest defensible read, not a replacement for a stamped survey or a permit." An affiliate repeating the handoff's version makes a claim that fails the moment a professional checks it, which is the exact failure mode the never-say list exists to prevent. The handoff doc predates the masters on this point and the masters win.

## Measurement owed before external use

Three classes of number in this plan are not yet measured and must not be asserted until they are. The Central Texas parcel and honest-absence counts move with every publish and require a live read at the time of use. Market size by county requires a named instrument and a stated source rather than a recalled figure. The city-level regulatory hooks that would make the Austin infill and growth-corridor copy specific must be verified against the corpus before they enter any asset.
