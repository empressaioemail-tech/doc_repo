Platform session · against 30b design system

One city record, seen through lenses

Four first-class products on one system: Dashboards, Smart Files, Plan Review, and Asset Management. Every layout below is built from the tokens and components already ratified in the design system. No new token, no new component, no second look. Content is template-city: a demo fixture, never a claim that a city is onboarded.

Internal · do not publish outside the building

These layouts carry citations to a licensed corpus under a proof-of-concept licence that does not extend to customer-facing applications. Internal circulation only. If any mock leaves the building, strip the licensed corpus titles first.

Reading these mocks

Three identities never collapse. Everything here is template-city, a demo fixture. Live Bastrop stays on its current app and is not cut over by this design. A next city is a pack on this same product, not a fork.

Section 11 · Flows

Four flows, annotated

11.1 Staff: from the city to an override

Land on Overview
Top bar answers city and environment. The first panel is What needs you today, four rows. No greeting, no hero.
Click the PR-2026-0148 row
Row opens the drawer over Overview with the case summary and a link into the owning lens. The queue is never lost.
Open in Development services
Lens switches, sidebar active item moves, pipeline queue loads with the case selected and the map moved to its parcel. No identifier typed.
Open review console
Review tab mounts the Plan Review console natively. Same shell, same header, no second chrome, no frame.
Read the unresolved rows
Matrix defaults to Unresolved only: one fails, two uncertain, one unchecked, grouped under their corpus headers. 44 passing sections stay gray and out of the way.
Open reasoning on the failing section
Basis panel shows derivation, source count, confidence with its state, and the read time. Section identifier and heading only.
Override with a reason
Override is disabled until the reason field has content. On commit: toast Determination recorded, row restates to the examiner’s call, case history gains the reason with name and timestamp.

11.2 Demo visitor: four lenses, no parcel form

Land unauthenticated on template-city
Environment badge reads Demo city in amber. Overview lens loads with the demo fixture already in view.
Move between the four lead lenses
Overview, Development services, Finance, Citizen, all from the sidebar. Every one loads populated or honestly empty. Nothing asks for a parcel identifier.
Click a parcel on the map
The identifier appears as a consequence of the click, in the region footer. It was never an input.
What the visitor never sees
Staff names, assignments, work orders, case routing, connections, people and access. The demo carries the shape of the product, not a city’s private operations.

11.3 Empty city: Finance and Citizen

Open Finance
Page header carries a state pill, not a metric strip. Four zeros would be four false claims.
Read the honest-empty
Names the four sources it reads, states that none is connected, and says explicitly that this is not a zero balance. Basis block gives the count and the contact.
Read the source register
Each source is a row with its own state. Permit fee revenue shows Partial, because fees exist on cases but the ledger has not confirmed collection.
Open Citizen
Payment card states that online payment is unavailable and gives the counter address and hours. No pay button that resolves to nothing, no Payment complete.

11.4 Applicant: the thin status view

Open My requests
One card per case, public vocabulary only. Waiting on you, never Routing DS review 2 of 4, never a reviewer name.
Open the comment letter
Findings in plain language with their citations. Local ordinance may quote in full; licensed sections carry the full canonical title and no body text.
Upload revisions
Status moves to In review. The applicant is never shown the applicability matrix or the examiner’s internal adjudication.

11.5 Plan Review attaches a file from Smart Files

In the review console, attach evidence to a finding
The picker opens Files scoped to what the acting tenant can see. No upload dialog writing into the review app.
Choose 311-water-st-A501-egress.pdf

A new version lands
v4 becomes current everywhere it is referenced. The review that cited v3 still resolves to v3, because prior versions stay resolvable.
What did not happen
No second document table. No pf_documents inside the review app. One filing system, referenced in place.

Section 14 · Compass

Compass: a sheet that is the control, grown

Compass is the assistant that follows the user across all four products. It is not a page, not a nav item, and not a rail state. It is one source control in the top bar that changes size into a sheet, and the same object again into a maximized surface.

Ticket sentence

The panel expands from the tapped element’s rect using a shared element transition, spring eased, with corner radius interpolating from tile to container, content fading in on a short delay, and the underlying surface scaling up slightly and dimming.

Two jobs, kept apart. Chrome is how Compass arrives: a shared-element transition. Atom language is how evidence opens inside it: a reserved chip that fetches on tap and grows in place. They are separate objects and neither borrows the other’s behaviour. A chip on a dashboard row opens a popover; it does not launch Compass.

14.1 Part A — the five properties

The umbrella term is a shared element transition, also called a hero transition or matched geometry transition. Apple’s names are zoom transition and matchedGeometryEffect. The small thing and the big thing are the same object changing size, not two views swapping places. Five things happen at once; skipping any one is why copies feel off.

  • 1Origin anchoring. The panel grows from the exact rect of the control that was tapped, not from the centre of the screen. The transform origin is the source rect. This is what makes the content feel like it came from somewhere.
  • 2Corner radius interpolation. Corners animate from the source radius to the sheet radius over the same duration, on a continuous corner curve, not a circular arc. Skipping this is the single most common reason a copy feels wrong.
  • 3Spring physics, not an easing curve. Damped spring with mass, stiffness and damping. Fast start, a barely perceptible settle, no perceptible stop. Duration reads as 350 to 500ms but is a function of the spring.
  • 4Content crossfade with a delay. Destination content fades in slightly after the container starts growing, clipped inside the expanding frame. The container leads, the content follows.
  • 5Background recession. The surface the panel launched from scales up a few percent and dims, so there is a z-axis between layers. Closing reverses all five.

The property that makes it feel alive rather than canned: the animation is interruptible and velocity preserving. The sheet can be grabbed mid-open and dragged back; it reverses from its current position and speed rather than finishing and restarting.

14.2 Present, maximize, dismiss

Working demo, not a still. It runs the real spring, anchors to the real source rect, interpolates radius, delays the content, and recedes the background. Reduced-motion is honoured live: if your system is set to reduce motion, present and dismiss are instant with no spring and no scale.

14.4 Motion specification

Technique: FLIP, First Last Invert Play. Measure the source rect, measure the destination rect, apply an inverting transform so the destination begins looking exactly like the source, then release it. Transform-based, not animated width and height, so it stays on the compositor. Radius is compensated by the inverse of the scale at the start so it interpolates true rather than distorting.

This is the only named exception to the 180ms cap. Drawers, dialogs, row enters, popovers and toasts stay on --sc-dur-1 through --sc-dur-3, and data still never animates. Do not reopen the cap for anything else.
