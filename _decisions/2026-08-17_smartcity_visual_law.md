## Structural commitment check

- Sell reasoning, not data: provenance chip and basis line are components, not slogans. Bare confidence numbers are a shape violation.
- Confidence is earned: three states on the chip (baseline / provenance-backed / earned). Uncertain and Unchecked are louder than Pass.
- Tenant sovereignty: environment badge keeps demo / live / next-city from collapsing. Citizen and staff share tokens, not skins.
- Dual interface: this is Empressa chrome. Hauska is out of kit.

## Reasoning

Register not card deck kills glass-card at the root (1px hairline; rest has no shadow). Sidebar is arithmetic: seven top-nav items was already the ceiling and the roster still has to hold department lenses plus Plan Review, Files, and AM. Applicability matrix inverts status because a plans examiner is paid for unresolved rows; green-for-pass would spend the loudest treatment on work that needs no one. Unchecked hatch is the plat convention for "nobody has been here yet," which is more dangerous than Fail. Code citation has no body slot so licensed ICC text cannot leak into a screenshot. Environment badge is the only amber in chrome because three identities that look identical is how gold `48021:34137` gets quoted as Bastrop onboarded. "Not built" nav keeps G-24 and extra lenses honest. Charts and comment-letter print are deferred on purpose.

## Reversal criteria

Reverse the register metaphor only if operator accepts glass/glow again. Reverse sidebar only if the shipped IA stays at four lead lenses forever. Reverse inverted matrix only if examiners reject quiet-pass in a staff sitting. Reverse "no ICC body slot" only if counsel clears verbatim display. Artifact URL dying is not reversal; the rulings in this record stand.
