## Decision

The marketed honesty claim is that the payload makes silence legible, so a model refuses where the record refuses instead of filling the hole from training priors. It is not a verified property that Smart Site "will not degrade." Collateral that says the stronger thing is overclaim until the contract covers every field the model will repeat as fact.

The contract that exists today covers brief sections (present, absent-verified, unknown, refused, unread) and overlay states. A missing atom maps to unknown, never to absent-verified. A refusal carries `agentGuidance` aimed at the model. Geometry is a server projection: named edges with lengths and bearings already computed, and a draw block that is an instruction. The model does not do coordinate math.

The contract does not cover edge metadata. Neighbor ids and adjacency currently serialize as flat fact with an implicit present. That gap is X2 on `_inbox/2026-08-30_smartsite_mcp_app_v2_WDLL.md`. Do not market the honesty differentiator until X2 is customer-done.

## Context

Operator restatement 2026-08-30, attributed to the operator's own wording in the 2026-08-28 QA battery, not to a team measurement. The phrase was a design argument: Smart Site would differentiate against competitors whose data quietly degrades into confident nonsense inside an LLM. The degradation is not transport. It is a missing field with nothing to stop the model from inventing a plausible setback.

This session's walk showed the section half working as designed: envelope refused with `agentGuidance` not to invent setback distances or a buildable polygon (same string the 2026-08-30 five-parcel capture recorded as honored). Two caveats remain live.

Caveat 1, already flagged: flood (and land use on some bakes) returns disposition present with `citationsDegraded: true` and an empty citations array. A reviewer who checks finds sections marked present that cannot be sourced. Still true on every flood section in the 2026-08-30 walks. F2 and the data-lane citation restore own it.

Caveat 2, new this walk, operator observation: every edge came back as present, with neighbor ids and adjacency stated as fact and no disposition guard. Five of six of those labels on verified shared boundaries were wrong. This seat has not independently re-measured the 5-of-6 count; it is filed as operator-attributed pending a file-based instrument (X1 reciprocity sweep plus X2 serialize rule). The mechanism does not depend on the count: a wrong neighbor with no disposition is the same defect class at n=1.

`_smartsite_masters/01_smart_site_positioning.md` "What only we can say" still states the stronger claim ("this is a feature competitors cannot claim"). That line is a consumer of this decision. Do not rewrite the approved-claims register in this pass; do not use the stronger line in new collateral until X2 lands.

## Structural commitment check

Sell reasoning, not data: a neighbor id without a disposition is data the model will repeat as reasoning.
Confidence is earned: `present` on a section or an edge that cannot be sourced is unearned.
Cost per jurisdiction: no new ingest; X1 reads the store; X2 is a serialize rule.
Dual interface: the same edge object reaches Claude and the panel; a hover tooltip that repeats a wrong neighbor is the same defect.

## Reasoning

Most property data arrives as flat prose or a report with gaps. Silence is not a signal. The five dispositions are the signal. Absent-verified means we looked and there is nothing. Unknown means we did not look or could not tell. Unread means the read has not been attempted. Refused means the bake declined, and `agentGuidance` tells the model what not to invent. That is why a missing setback does not become a fluent invented setback.

The geometry half is the same idea applied to coordinates. The server projects the ring into a local frame in feet and hands over named edges. Reconstruction that stays inside those numbers is arithmetic on an upstream result. That stays reliable because the hard part was done once.

A contract that stops Claude inventing a setback and then hands it a wrong neighbor as fact has not kept the promise. X1 (reciprocity sweep) finds disagreement. X2 is the wire rule: neighbor and adjacency cannot serialize as flat fact. A reciprocity miss, an unverified ray-hit (O3), or an unconfirmed label maps to unknown or refused with guidance, never to implicit present.

Second mechanism rejected: the 5-of-6 miss is only a data-quality problem and X1 alone closes it. Rejected because a repaired label that still ships without a disposition will fail the same way the next time the join is wrong, and the model will still have nothing in the payload telling it so.

## Reversal criteria

Revisit the marketing gate if X2 is customer-done: every `draw.edges[]` element carries an explicit disposition on neighbor and on adjacency (or the edge as a whole), a known-wrong or unverified neighbor cannot serialize as implicit present, and a Connect walk on the gold shared-boundary set shows the model refusing or hedging on those labels the way it already refuses setbacks. Revisit the five-state vocabulary only if the atom contract adds a sixth state that is independently derived, not as a silent widening of `present`. A live probe that showed flood citations restored and non-empty would close caveat 1 without touching this decision.
