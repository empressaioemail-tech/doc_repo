# Decision

We are not RWA creators. Real-world-asset companies and operators mount their RWA onto our provenance, Smart Files, and map visualization. That mount is the legitimate sell to the RWA crowd. We do not mint, issue, or operate their token.

## Context

Operator added this on 2026-08-15 after the isolation card was approved and the mount-composition vision was filed. The earlier record named tokenization as an optional future mount ("if that's a thing"). This record answers the commercial question that left open: we do not become an RWA issuer. We offer a clean adoption surface so an RWA party can attach their asset to a place that already has public-record provenance, a private file room, and a map.

No named RWA counterparty is claimed in this record. The claim is positioning, not a live deal.

## Structural commitment check

- Sell reasoning, not data: the RWA buyer is buying a place they can contest (citations, accessPolicy, CID, map). They are not buying a token we minted.
- Tenant sovereignty: an RWA operator's private room stays tenant-private in Smart Files. Public spine facts stay public. Their token rail, if they have one, stays theirs.
- Dual interface: an RWA mount is MCP plus the SmartSite (or later) map embed. A token-minter UI is not a Hauska or Empressa product in this decision.
- Catalog thesis: aligned if we stay the mount. Yellow if G-58 or any near-term card starts a token mint, a chain integration, or an "Empressa RWA" brand. Red if we sell ourselves as the issuer.

## Reasoning

The RWA market already has issuers. What it lacks is a clean way to sit a tokenized claim on a real place with public-record provenance, a private evidence room, and a map that is not a marketing screenshot. That is the composition we are building: spine (what is true about the place) plus Smart Files (what this party attached) plus SmartSite map (how it is seen). The RWA operator brings the asset representation. We do not create it.

This is the same mount pattern as a title company, a city, or a builder. The RWA party is another actor who chooses a mount set. Treating them as a customer of the mount, not as a reason to build a token factory, keeps the Hauska thesis intact and keeps G-58 from silently becoming a chain project.

## Reversal criteria

Reverse "we are not RWA creators" only if the operator later accepts an issuer role in writing and a new plan row names the mint, the chain, and the license. Do not treat inbound RWA interest as that reversal. Do not add a token mint, a chain adapter, or an RWA SKU to G-58.

## Dependencies

Companion to `_decisions/2026-08-15_capability_mount_composition.md`. Isolation WDLL and G-58 stay the files module plus one SmartSite consumer. RWA mount is a later consumer card, same as title or builder.

## Counterparties

Internal: operator, Lane A planner. External: unnamed RWA operators as a class. No named issuer, chain, or marketplace is committed here.
