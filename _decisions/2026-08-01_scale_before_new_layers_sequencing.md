# Sequencing decision — scale first, layers alongside

## THE QUESTION
After the in-flight fixes land, should we establish the other public data layers (soils, wetlands, utility-availability, etc.) BEFORE scaling to statewide, or scale first?

## THE DECISION
Scale the CERTIFIED assembly layers statewide FIRST. Do NOT establish new layers as a prerequisite. New public layers split into two kinds and are timed accordingly:
- Statewide-UNIFORM data layers (soils/SSURGO, wetlands/NWI, OZ, transmission, school districts) — single national/state sources, uniform coverage, NO per-jurisdiction certification. These ride as a CHEAP PARALLEL TRACK during the scale (each is a wire-one-source job like the OZ layer, and lands statewide instantly).
- Per-jurisdiction-ASSEMBLY new layers (utility-availability from municipal utility districts, easements from deed records, historic overlays from city ordinances) — carry the SAME certification cost as zoning/setback. DEFERRED until after the scale proves the mold holds wide.

## SCALE TARGET (ratified)
Primary: statewide parcel + zoning + buildable (the certified assembly layers) across TX jurisdictions — the "one place, all the layers, current, everywhere" footprint. This is "done enough to fan." New data layers ride alongside as cheap wire-one-source adds.

## WHY (the reasoning that makes this correct, not just safe)
1. You can only STAMP what's CERTIFIED. The entire arc (Block-13 cert, setback engine, the certified mold) proved ASSEMBLY correctness per-layer/per-jurisdiction. The fan-out engine's premise is: take a certified mold, stamp it across jurisdictions. Adding 4 uncertified new assembly layers before scaling quadruples the cert surface AND does it uncertified-at-scale — the exact drift trap already learned (scan-fix loop; "certified a broken Bastrop"; area-not-sample).
2. Two KINDS of new layer, only one is cheap. Statewide-uniform layers have NO per-city assembly problem (there's no "certify Bastrop's soil differently than Smithville's") — the OZ pattern is the template: wire the source once, works everywhere. These scale for FREE and belong DURING the scale. Assembly-derived layers carry the zoning/setback cert cost and must wait.
3. Coverage IS the wedge (GTM pivot 2026-07-04: market footprint per vertical + own the MCP market). Statewide parcel+zoning+buildable is a bigger footprint move than deep-but-narrow Bastrop-plus-six-layers. Breadth sells "one place, all layers, everywhere"; a 7th layer in one city does not.
4. Uniform layers get BETTER wide. Soils/OZ/wetlands are most valuable shown across the whole footprint. Adding them AS the footprint lands makes each instantly statewide; adding them before under-sells them.

## THE ORDERING
1. Land the in-flight fleets (spine-ledger remaining fixes + OZ statewide visibility). Stabilize.
2. Fan the SCALE-OUT engine on the certified layers (parcel/zoning/buildable statewide) — the flagship planning conversation (still owed: scope/target/cost-gate with the operator before dispatch).
3. PARALLEL cheap track: wire the statewide-uniform data layers (soils/SSURGO first — Central-TX septic/expansive-clay value — then wetlands, transmission, school districts, more OZ-style). Each = one-source wiring like OZ, cited/dated, no cert project. Rides alongside the scale, does not gate it.
4. HOLD the per-jurisdiction-assembly new layers (utility-availability-from-local-records, easements, historic overlays) until after the scale proves the mold holds wide.

## WHAT NOT TO DO
Do NOT treat "add public layers" as a monolith to finish before scaling — it is two different kinds of work, and only the cheap (uniform-source) kind belongs before/during the scale. Do NOT scale uncertified assembly layers. Do NOT let the parallel data-layer track gate or slow the fan-out.

## REVERSAL CRITERIA
Revisit if: (a) the scale-out reveals the certified mold does NOT generalize (a jurisdiction class breaks assembly) — then fix the mold before more breadth; (b) a specific new assembly layer becomes a hard customer requirement that a live deal hinges on (then certify that one layer as its own scoped project, still not a monolith); (c) a "uniform" layer turns out to have hidden per-jurisdiction variance (then it reclassifies to the assembly track and waits).

## NEXT
The fan-out engine scope/target/cost-gate planning is the owed operator conversation. This decision is its frame: certified-layers-wide is the fan; uniform-data-layers are the parallel cheap track; assembly-new-layers wait.
