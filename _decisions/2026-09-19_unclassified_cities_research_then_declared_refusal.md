# Decision

**The problem.** After P-300/P-338, 3,682 setback parcels stay `unaccounted`: 3,195 in eight no-table
cities the classification lane could not settle at source (Mart 1,495, Crawford 459, Hallsburg 335, Leroy
320, Webberville 245, Ross 242, Golinda 95, Thorndale 4; seven of the eight in McLennan), and 487 in
Thrall, which is classified (b) but whose city-wide line is not coded (its ordinance packet is an
image-only scan with more than one amendment). `unaccounted` is fatal at publish, so these keep the
setback verdicts of McLennan, Travis/Bastrop and Williamson from passing.

**Ruled.**

1. **Now (option A):** one research lane with a real browser (P-378) reads each city's own law where the
   automated pass was blocked (Cloudflare on the code hosts for Mart, Crawford, Golinda; the county's
   record of adopted ordinances for Leroy and Ross; Webberville's own site) and Thrall's current
   ordinance text, and classifies each city at source under ruling 5 with the same evidence rules.
2. **For whatever remains after that (option C):** a city still not settled at source gets a DECLARED
   REFUSAL on its parcels' setback cells ("this city's zoning status is not established at source;
   verify the setback line with the city"), carrying the city, what was read and what would settle it
   (P-379). It is never a class, never a value, never `absent-verified`.
3. **Flagged to come back to.** No phone calls now. The declared refusals are a named, counted population,
   reopened by P-377 when a classification lands.

**Why C is acceptable here, and its cost, stated.** ENFORCEMENT warns that an `unaccounted` count falling
without a matching acquisition is relabelling. C does exactly that for the residue, by the operator's
explicit choice, to let the setback gates close for market. What keeps it honest: the refusal says what
is not known and what would settle it; it is counted as its own population (never folded into ruling 6 or
class (a)); it is reopenable (P-377's class-change trigger); and this record names it as a debt.

# Reversal criteria

Reverse (turn the declared refusals back to `unaccounted`, or classify them) when any of: a source read
settles a city (P-377 reopens its parcels and the writer classes them); the operator makes the calls; or
a customer relies on a declared refusal in a way that misleads (it must read as "not established", never
as "no setback applies").
