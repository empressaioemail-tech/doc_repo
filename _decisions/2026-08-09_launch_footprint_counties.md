# Launch-footprint counties

Operator ruling 2026-08-09: the launch footprint is all of Central Texas plus the Dallas metro. These are the counties where jurisdiction-depth rails (CAD attributes, owner, zoning, envelope, land use, easements) must reach SATISFIED for launch, per the per-rail gate split. This record enumerates the ruling into a concrete county list. The ruling is settled; the enumeration below is the planner's proposal and is provisional until the operator strikes or adds counties.

## Proposed enumeration (28 counties)

**Central Texas core (CAPCOG region, 10):** Travis 48453, Williamson 48491, Hays 48209, Bastrop 48021, Caldwell 48055, Burnet 48053, Blanco 48031, Lee 48287, Fayette 48149, Llano 48299.

**Central Texas corridor extension (5):** Bexar 48029, Comal 48091, Guadalupe 48187 (San Antonio corridor), Bell 48027 (Killeen-Temple), McLennan 48309 (Waco).

**Dallas metro (DFW MSA, 13):** Dallas 48113, Tarrant 48439, Collin 48085, Denton 48121, Ellis 48139, Johnson 48251, Kaufman 48257, Parker 48367, Rockwall 48397, Hunt 48231, Wise 48497, Hood 48221, Somervell 48425.

## Verification note (source-required)

FIPS codes and the DFW MSA composition above are from planner knowledge and must be verified against the Census delineation file and the state FIPS table before the first depth wave targets the list. The corridor extension is a judgment call (colloquial Central Texas), included as the most likely operator intent and the easiest tier to strike.

## Reversal criteria

Strike or add counties by operator edit to this record any time before the first depth wave aims at the list; after waves run, additions are cheap (more waves) and removals only change the launch gate reading, not the acquired data.
