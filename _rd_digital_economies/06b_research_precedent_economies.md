# Precedent Digital Economies: Research Record

Researched 2026-08-13 via web search. Evidence tags: FACT (verified against multiple sources or primary records), REPORTED (single-source or press claim, not independently verified), INFERENCE (our synthesis).

## Headline findings

- Every pure speculation-on-a-person economy died: BitClout (2021), Roll (hacked 2021), Rally (2023), friend.tech (2024). Zero survivors in the "tradable claim on a human with no underlying cash flow" category. FACT.
- The one durable person-linked structure is a regulated security on a contractual cash flow: Dinwiddie's bond (undersubscribed but legal and paid) and the 2026 tZERO//mkt Reg A+ athlete-earnings platform. The lesson is that person-linked value survives only when it is a claim on a contract, not on a reputation. FACT/INFERENCE.
- Consent was the first-order ethical failure: BitClout listed 15,000 people's coins without permission; friend.tech let anyone's account be keyed and traded. Legal exposure and reputational collapse followed. FACT.
- Bonding curves plus tradability turned people into pump-and-dump instruments; extraction beat participation everywhere it was tried (friend.tech founders exited with roughly 44M USD as users lost 98 percent). FACT.
- Place tokens failed completely: MiamiCoin fell about 95 percent, NYCCoin was disavowed by the city, both delisted by March 2023. A token with no claim on the place's cash flows or services is a memecoin wearing a city's name. FACT/INFERENCE.
- Community capital CAN buy and operate real things: LinksDAO bought Spey Bay Golf Club (2023) and Hillcrest Country Club (2025) and operates them with real staff. It converted governance tokens into a conventional operating company with a membership layer. FACT.
- The durable membership economies (Costco, Patreon, Blackbird, Pudgy Penguins, FWB) all share one shape: value flows TO members as consumption benefits, not FROM members as speculative exit liquidity. Costco: 5.3B USD membership fees, roughly 73 percent of operating profit, about 90 percent renewal. FACT.
- Sports fan tokens survive as businesses but not as investments: Chiliz claims 700M USD delivered to sports partners while CHZ and all fan tokens trade 95 percent below the 2021 peak; the team-side revenue was real, the holder-side return was catastrophic. REPORTED/FACT.
- Platform-dependent points die by platform decision: Reddit Community Points were killed unilaterally in 2023 (MOON down 84 percent overnight) citing scalability and regulation. Anything the issuer can delete is not an economy. FACT.
- Regulatory arc matters but is survivable when structured: the SEC's BitClout case was dropped with prejudice in March 2026 and the DOJ indictment withdrawn in February 2025, while tZERO//mkt launched a fully SEC-regulated athlete platform two months later. The compliant path now exists; the uncompliant path is what killed the first generation. FACT.

## 1. Person-linked token economies

### BitClout / DeSo (2021 to present, effectively dead as a creator-coin economy)

BitClout launched March 2021 (founder Nader Al-Naji, "Diamondhands") with creator coins on a bonding curve for roughly 15,000 Twitter accounts, created without the individuals' permission. FACT. CLOUT debuted around 180 USD, briefly touched 192, and collapsed 70 percent to about 60 within its early run; the platform pivoted to the DeSo blockchain after a claimed 200M USD raise that Protos reported was largely a re-labeling of the original funding. REPORTED. The SEC charged Al-Naji in July 2024 with selling 257M USD in unregistered securities and misappropriating about 3M USD; the DOJ charged wire fraud. FACT (SEC press release 2024-91, coindesk.com 2024-07-30). The DOJ withdrew its indictment February 2025 and the SEC dismissed the civil case with prejudice, no sanctions, March 2026. FACT (theblock.co, crowdfundinsider.com, 2026-03). The enforcement climate softened, but the economy itself was already dead: bonding-curve creator coins attracted speculators rather than users, and trading detached from any platform usage. INFERENCE (supported by Benzinga's January 2026 "SocialFi death spiral" retrospective).

What went wrong for the humans: people discovered tradable coins in their own names that they never authorized, with no way to opt out and no share of proceeds. The asset was the person's reputation; the person had no rights in it. FACT/INFERENCE.

Sources: hulkapps.com/blogs/ecommerce-hub/what-happened-to-bitclout; benzinga.com 2026-01 SocialFi death spiral; protos.com bitclout-deso piece; coindesk.com 2024-07-30; theblock.co/post/393611.

### Rally ($RLY) (2020 to 2023, dead)

Rally ran creator coins for musicians and streamers on a custodial Ethereum sidechain. RLY peaked around 1.40 USD in April 2021 and lost more than 96 percent. On January 31, 2023 Rally shut the sidechain for lack of funding, and because assets were not bridgeable to mainnet, creators' and fans' tokens and NFTs were simply stranded at zero. FACT (coindesk.com 2023-01-31; decrypt.co/120285). Creators who had built "economies" worth hundreds of thousands of dollars saw them deleted by an infrastructure decision they did not control. FACT.

Lesson: custodial or platform-chain person-economies inherit the platform's mortality. The creators bore the loss; the platform bore none of the obligation. INFERENCE.

### Roll (2021 hack, faded)

Roll minted branded social money for creators. On March 14, 2021 its hot-wallet private keys were compromised; roughly 3,000 ETH (5.7M USD) in 400+ creators' tokens was drained and dumped through Uniswap, crashing WHALE, FWB, and the whole social-token sector in one day. Roll posted a 500K USD relief fund. FACT (coindesk.com 2021-03-14; techcrunch.com 2021-03-23). The category never recovered its pre-hack trajectory. INFERENCE.

### friend.tech (August 2023 to September 2024, dead)

Keys (originally "shares," renamed under securities scrutiny) in a person's chatroom, priced on a steep bonding curve; at peak it was more than half of all activity on Base with daily fees around 2M USD. By mid-2024 daily fees were under 100 USD and new users in the single digits. September 2024 the team transferred contract ownership to the null address and walked away; press estimated the founders' take at roughly 44M USD in fees. The FRIEND token fell about 98 percent from its May 2024 launch. FACT (dlnews.com; blockhead.co 2024-09-10; coinmarketcap.com academy).

What went wrong for the humans: the curve design guaranteed early buyers profit only from later buyers (pure greater-fool structure); "friendship" was priced as an exit opportunity; when attention rotated, every person's key collapsed simultaneously and the people whose names carried the assets got blamed by their own holders. FACT/INFERENCE.

### Spencer Dinwiddie's tokenized contract (2019 to 2023, completed but undersubscribed)

Dinwiddie tried to tokenize his 34.5M USD Nets contract. The NBA threatened termination under the CBA clause barring assignment of compensation rights; he removed the performance-upside dividend and issued a flat bond (4.95 percent monthly interest, bullet principal) via Securitize as SEC-exempt securities for accredited investors. It sold 9 of 90 tokens (1.35M of a 13.5M target) to eight investors. FACT (forbes.com 2020-01-10; coindesk.com 2020-07-22). The structure worked legally and paid; demand was thin because upside was stripped and the league constrained the design. FACT/INFERENCE. This is the pivotal precedent for anyone twinning athletes: the LEAGUE's contract law, not securities law, was the binding constraint.

### tZERO / /mkt athlete earnings tokens (announced April 2026, live path)

Blockchain Market Inc. (/mkt) partnered with tZERO to sell SEC-regulated Reg A+ securities representing a contracted percentage of professional athletes' future income, open to retail, with secondary trading on tZERO's ATS; first offerings expected 2026. REPORTED (tzero.com press release 2026-04-08; multiple wire reprints). Not yet proven in market, but it is the first retail-accessible, regulated, consent-based person-linked asset design. The consent is contractual (the athlete sells the income share), the claim is on cash flow not reputation, and the venue is a regulated ATS. INFERENCE: this is what survived the 2021 to 2024 extinction event, the Dinwiddie design with the regulatory rails matured.

## 2. Place-linked tokens

### CityCoins (MiamiCoin, NYCCoin) (2021 to 2023, dead)

A Delaware startup issued Stacks-mined city coins with 30 percent of mining directed to a city wallet. Mayor Suarez embraced MiamiCoin (claimed it could one day replace municipal taxes); Miami cashed out 5.25M USD in February 2022. MiamiCoin then fell roughly 95 percent from its September 2021 peak; NYCCoin fell 68 percent in Adams's first months and NYC formally disavowed any affiliation. OKCoin suspended trading March 2023 citing limited liquidity and manipulation risk; effectively delisted everywhere. FACT (futurism.com; qz.com; bloomberg.com 2023-03-21; statescoop.com).

Why place tokens failed: the token carried the city's name but no claim on the city's services, taxes, land, or cash flows, and the city carried no obligation to holders. The only buyer motive was speculation on civic branding. When the city itself sold into the market (Miami's cash-out), the alignment inverted: the place profited from its fans' losses. FACT/INFERENCE.

### Praxis and network-state projects (ongoing, unproven)

Praxis announced 525M USD in milestone-gated financing (500M GEM Digital, 25M Arch Lending) in late 2024 to build a crypto-native city; as of late 2025 it was still in site selection (Latin America / Mediterranean) with no token and no city. REPORTED (theblock.co/post/321268; gizmodo.com). GEM Digital commitments are draw-down facilities contingent on a liquid token, so the headline number is not cash in hand. INFERENCE. No network-state project has yet operated a functioning place economy; CityDAO (section 5) is the closest test and it wound down. FACT.

## 3. Community and membership economies that worked

### Blackbird (2022 to present, alive and funded)

Ben Leventhal's (Eater, Resy) restaurant loyalty and payments network. Flynet mainnet (an L3 on Base) went live February 2025; raised 50M USD in April 2025 (Fortune, TechCrunch 2025-04-08); still shipping in June 2026. FACT. Design: diners earn FLY through check-ins and spend; restaurants keep more margin and own customer relationships; the chain is invisible plumbing, the product is dinner. The token is earned by consumption and redeemed for consumption; speculation is not the interface. FACT/INFERENCE.

### Pudgy Penguins (2021 to present, the NFT membership survivor)

Rebuilt post-2021 as a consumer brand: toys in 3,100 Walmart stores plus Target, Amazon, Walgreens (1M+ units, 13M+ USD retail by mid-2024), 15M+ USD apparel line, Manchester City and NHL collaborations, Visa-network payment card, reported 120M USD 2026 revenue target; NFT holders receive IP licensing royalties. REPORTED (kucoin.com news; coindesk.com research; vaasblock.com 2026). The NFT became a membership and IP-rights position in an operating brand rather than a bare collectible. INFERENCE.

### Friends With Benefits (2020 to present, alive)

Token-gated cultural community (75 FWB for global membership); survived the Roll hack and the 2022 crash by shrinking to fundamentals: annual FWB FEST (fourth edition 2025), product studio work, token migrated to Base. About 5,500 members. FACT (coindesk.com 2025-04-18; fwb.help). Small, but durable because the token gates participation people actually want rather than promising appreciation. INFERENCE.

### Reddit Community Points (2020 to 2023, killed by issuer)

MOON and BRICK rewarded subreddit contribution. Reddit terminated the program November 2023 citing scalability and regulatory cost; MOON fell about 84 percent on the announcement; moderators were removed amid insider-trading suspicion around the shutdown. FACT (coindesk.com 2023-10-17; cryptoslate.com). Lesson: a community economy inside a corporate platform exists at the platform's pleasure; the "rug" risk was the issuer, not the market. INFERENCE.

### The non-crypto benchmarks

Costco FY2025: 5.323B USD membership fee revenue (up 10 percent), roughly 1.9 percent of revenue but about 73 percent of operating profit, 81M paid members, about 90 percent renewal (92.2 percent US/Canada per Q3 coverage). FACT (Costco 10-K FY2025; capitaloneshopping.com stats). Patreon: over 2B USD paid to creators annually, 10B+ USD cumulative, 25M+ paid memberships; direct-to-fan value is over half of a roughly 290B USD creator economy. REPORTED (backlinko.com; contentgrip.com 2025). The benchmark result: the largest membership economies on earth run on renewal and consumption, and none of them let members trade their membership. INFERENCE.

What separates durable membership economies from dead token economies (synthesis, INFERENCE): (1) members are net consumers of value, not net providers of exit liquidity; (2) the unit is earned or purchased for use, not flipped; (3) the issuer has a real business whose margin funds the benefits; (4) no bonding curves, no price as the product; (5) the community outlives any single platform decision because the benefit (golf, dinner, toys, festivals, warehouse prices) is real-world.

## 4. Sports-adjacent survivors and their numbers

### Socios / Chiliz (2018 to present, alive; holders wrecked)

Team-side reality: Chiliz claims over 700M USD in cumulative new income delivered to sports partners; Barcelona's initial BAR sale alone did 1.2M EUR in under two hours (2021); Messi took a reported 20M USD Socios promotion deal; Barcelona later signed a 100M USD digital deal. REPORTED (chiliz.com; komonews.com; malaymail.com 2022). Socios claims about 2.5M users. Holder-side reality: CHZ trades about 95 to 97 percent below its March 2021 peak of roughly 0.88 USD (market cap around 370 to 415M USD); ALL fan tokens combined are roughly 240M USD market cap versus a 1B+ peak; fan tokens confer polls and perks, not equity or tickets, and fan groups criticize them as paid pseudo-engagement (rival fans can vote in your club's polls). FACT/REPORTED (coingecko.com; beincrypto.com SportFi analysis; thenationalnews.com 2022-04-17). Chiliz's 2026 to 2030 roadmap pitches yield-bearing assets backed by club revenue streams, which is an admission that the utility-only token was not enough. REPORTED (chiliz.com/vision2030). US fan tokens launched 2026. REPORTED.

### Sorare (2018 to present, alive; shrinking and in legal jeopardy)

Fantasy-football NFT cards. Revenue fell to 43M EUR in 2024, forecast flat at about 42M EUR for 2025, reserves declining from 78M to a projected 35M EUR. FACT (sbcnews.co.uk 2025-06-13). UK Gambling Commission criminal prosecution for unlicensed gambling, trial now postponed to June 2027; Sorare contests the characterization. FACT (gamblingcommission.gov.uk; blockchaingamer.biz). A real business with real revenue, but the "is this gambling" question attaches to any consumptive game where card values fluctuate. INFERENCE.

### NBA Top Shot / Dapper Labs (2020 to present, alive; about 99 percent off peak)

Peak: 224M USD in trades in February 2021. By October 2022, monthly secondary volume was about 2.7M USD; Dapper cut 22 percent of staff (Nov 2022) then 20 percent more (Feb 2023). Cumulative sales crossed 1B USD. The product still ships (2025-26 NBA season live) and The Ringer's February 2025 retrospective frames it as a niche collectibles business with open questions on license economics. FACT (cnbc.com 2022-11-03; frontofficesports.com; theringer.com 2025-02-26). Current monthly volumes are low single-digit millions at best per CryptoSlam trackers. REPORTED.

### What still has real revenue in 2026 (synthesis, INFERENCE)

Team-side token sale income (Socios model), Sorare's card sales (about 40M EUR/yr), Top Shot as a small collectibles line, Pudgy Penguins' merchandise and licensing, Blackbird's payments take. What does NOT have real revenue: any product whose only revenue was trading fees on person- or place-linked speculation. The survivors all sell something a fan consumes; the deaths all sold price exposure.

## 5. RWA-linked community economies

### ConstitutionDAO (November 2021, folded honestly)

Raised about 47M USD in ETH from 17,000+ people in a week to bid on a Constitution copy; lost the auction (the raise size was public, so the rival could simply outbid; and costs meant the full 47M could not be bid, which was poorly communicated). Refunds via Juicebox were gas-eaten for small contributors; a floated replacement token (WTP) was shelved after community revolt; core team made key calls without votes. PEOPLE persists as a memecoin unrelated to any asset. FACT (vice.com aftermath piece 2021; okx.com/learn guide). Lessons: transparent war chests get front-run; L1 gas made small-holder refunds uneconomic; "DAO" governance was actually a core-team multisig. FACT/INFERENCE.

### Krause House (2021 to present, alive at reduced ambition)

Raised about 4M USD to buy an NBA team; the 1.3B+ USD franchise floor made that impossible, so it bought what it could: the Ball Hogs (BIG3 league), becoming the first DAO to own a pro basketball team, and watches the NBA's private-equity minority-stake path as its realistic route. FACT (decrypt.co/86601; krausehouse.club; boardroom.tv). Honest record: community capital scaled to a minor-league asset, two orders of magnitude short of the headline dream. INFERENCE.

### LinksDAO (2022 to present, the RWA success case)

Raised about 10.5M USD in NFT memberships (January 2022); actually closed the purchase of Spey Bay Golf Club, Scotland in early 2023 (reported price above 750K USD), installed a general manager and head greenskeeper, funded renovations, and in Q1 2025 announced acquisition of Hillcrest Country Club in the US, doubling to two operated courses; also announced a LINKS token. FACT (golfdigest.com; coindesk.com 2023-03-16; linksdao.io/spey-bay; sportingcrypto.com newsletter 2025). This is the cleanest proof that a token-coordinated community can buy and OPERATE real venues, and it did so by wrapping conventional legal entities and professional operators around the community, with the NFT functioning as a founding membership rather than a security. FACT/INFERENCE. Note the scale honesty: two modest courses, not Pebble Beach (the original meme).

### CityDAO (2021 to 2024, orderly wind-down)

Raised about 8.5M USD from 5,000+ citizens; first DAO to legally own US land (40 acres, Park County, Wyoming, about 100K USD) under Wyoming's DAO LLC law. Never built a functioning economy on the land; voted 92.6 percent to wind down May 2024 (CIP 212) and returned 3.076M USD to 4,808 addresses; Parcel 0's disposition remains unclear. FACT (daotimes.com timeline; wyomingpublicmedia.org). Lesson: owning the asset is the easy half; operating it into cash flow is the part no place-DAO achieved. INFERENCE.

### WAGMI United / Crawley Town FC (2022 to 2024, cautionary)

Crypto consortium bought a real fourth-tier English football club (April 2022) promising NFT-funded promotion to the Premier League; first season: five managers, near-relegation, star striker sold to a rival, dressing-room chaos; by 2024 the blockchain framing was quietly dropped even as the team briefly contended for promotion. FACT (vice.com; fourfourtwo.com; sportico.com 2024). Lesson: community capital can acquire a club, but operating a sports organization is a professional discipline the token layer does not supply, and fan-investors absorb operational failure emotionally as well as financially. INFERENCE.

## Implications for our design

- Never mint value on a person without a signed contract conveying specific rights. BitClout's original sin (unauthorized coins in 15,000 people's names) is the single most repeated ethical failure. Our athlete twins must be opt-in, revocable in defined ways, and the athlete must be a paid counterparty, not a referenced asset. FACT-grounded.
- If a token's price references a human, the claim must be on a defined cash flow (contract share, royalty, league distribution), never on reputation or attention. Every reputation-priced instrument died; the Dinwiddie/tZERO lineage (regulated claim on contracted income) is the only living branch. Our master/sub tokens should be structured as claims on franchise economics, with the human twin as verification, not as the priced asset.
- Ban bonding curves and any design where members' returns require later buyers. friend.tech is the controlled experiment: greater-fool mechanics plus a person's name equals guaranteed collapse plus personal blame landing on the person. Fan-layer tokens should be consumptive (Blackbird FLY shape: earn by attending, spend on experiences), with tradability confined to the master/sub franchise layer.
- League-owned venues funded by token liquidity must clear the ConstitutionDAO and CityDAO bar: a real operating plan with professional operators BEFORE capital deploys, and LinksDAO's wrapper pattern (conventional legal entity, hired GM, community as founding members) as the template. Buying is easy; operating is the graveyard.
- The issuer must never be positioned to profit from members' exit (Miami cashing out MiamiCoin, friend.tech's 44M USD fee walk). League treasury sales into fan-held liquidity should be structurally constrained (lockups, buyback symmetry, disclosed schedules) or the alignment inverts exactly when trust matters most.
- Infrastructure mortality is inherited: Rally's sidechain and Reddit's points died by issuer decision and took every participant's value with them. Twins and tokens must live on credibly neutral rails with exit/bridge rights, and the truth layer (verified twins) must be exportable, not hostage to our own platform.
- Model revenue on the survivors' P&L shapes: Costco (membership fees at about 73 percent of operating profit, about 90 percent renewal), Patreon (2B USD/yr consumptive payments), Socios team-side (700M USD to partners even as holders lost 95 percent). Design the league's income from renewal and consumption; if the business only makes money on trading volume, we have rebuilt Top Shot's decay curve.
- Expect the gambling and securities perimeter to find us: Sorare faces a 2027 UK criminal trial for a consumptive fantasy game, and the SEC's retreat on BitClout does not generalize. Anything with fluctuating tradable value tied to athlete performance should be lawyered as either a regulated security (tZERO path) or a genuinely consumptive game with no cash-out loop, decided per layer at design time, not after launch.
