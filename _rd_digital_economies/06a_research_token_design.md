# Token economy design research (R&D 06a)

Research date 2026-08-13. Scope per dispatch: (1) master/sub token architectures in practice, (2) dual-token design lessons and the reflexivity failure mode, (3) treasury and liquidity design for a small-cap issuer, (4) token-linked-to-real-cash-flows design and the 2026 US posture, (5) the modeling literature and what to model first. Claims are tagged FACT (verifiable in a cited primary or strong secondary source), REPORTED (secondary source, not independently verified), or INFERENCE (our synthesis).

## Headline findings

- The Chiliz/Socios model is the closest live analogue to our center-and-nodes design and it is a cautionary tale on value distribution: the master token (CHZ) survived and accreted utility (gas, staking, buybacks) while nearly every node token (team fan token) lost 90 to 95 percent from peak and trades thin. REPORTED.
- The single most reliable killer of dual-token economies is reflexivity: when token demand IS the product (buy the token to earn the token), demand collapses the moment expected returns dip, and the death spiral is mechanical. Axie (SLP/AXS), StepN (GST/GMT), and Terra (UST/LUNA) all died this way. FACT.
- Survival correlates with demand that exists independently of token price: real consumption sinks, fees from external users, and consumptive (non-financialized) member layers. Our non-tradable member layer is the textbook mitigation. INFERENCE from the failure record.
- Hub tokens do not capture spoke value by default. Cosmos ATOM anchored a technically successful ecosystem and captured almost none of its economics; value accrual to the master token must be an explicit wired mechanism (fee routing, mandatory settlement, buybacks), not a narrative. FACT (on Cosmos) / INFERENCE (design rule).
- Sub-token liquidity is brutal at the node level: mid-tier fan tokens do tens of thousands of dollars in daily volume (Galatasaray roughly 72k USD/day in 2026), and delistings actively erode what liquidity exists. Model each sub-token as effectively illiquid. FACT (spot volumes) / INFERENCE (planning posture).
- The 2024 low-float/high-FDV launch pattern is now a documented failure mode: Binance Research estimated roughly 155B USD of unlocks 2024 to 2030 with sell pressure loaded onto the future; the larger the launch FDV, the larger the subsequent drawdown. FACT.
- The US regulatory window materially opened: the SEC/CFTC joint guidance of 2026-03-17 created a five-category taxonomy and a decentralization-linked path to commodity treatment, and revenue-linked buybacks became mainstream in 2025-2026 (Hyperliquid, Aave, Jupiter, dYdX). But there is no blanket approval; the analysis is still facts-and-circumstances under Howey. FACT.
- The clean precedent for tokenizing the real asset leg (stadium) is the security-token wrapper: St. Regis Aspen raised 18M USD under Reg D 506(c) via a REIT SPV with a registered broker-dealer ATS for secondary trading. Registered security token for the asset, separate non-security tokens for network utility. FACT.
- Buyback-and-burn is the dominant 2025-2026 cash-flow linkage because it avoids explicit distribution claims, but research on the 19B USD "buyback meta" finds most programs do not actually shrink supply and do not durably support price. REPORTED.
- The credible pre-launch modeling stack exists and is cheap relative to the downside: cadCAD/TokenSPICE agent-based simulation (BlockScience lineage), the Cong-Li-Wang RFS valuation framework, and stress tests of the exact reflexive loops that killed the comparables. FACT (tools exist) / INFERENCE (priority).

## 1. Master/sub token architectures in practice

### 1.1 Chiliz / Socios mechanics

The stack: CHZ is the utility and gas token of Chiliz Chain, an EVM Layer-1 for sports; Socios.com is the flagship app where clubs issue fan tokens. FACT. ([Chiliz explainer](https://onekey.so/blog/ecosystem/what-is-chiliz-chz-the-token-revolutionizing-fan-engagement/); [Klever overview](https://klever.io/blog/what-is-chiliz/))

Issuance mechanics. Each team token launches through a Fan Token Offering (FTO): a limited number of tokens sold at a fixed price for a limited window, purchasable only with CHZ; CHZ also clears transactions and fuels contract execution across the network. After the FTO the token floats on secondary markets. FACT. ([Socios FTO example, Flamengo 2021](https://www.socios.com/mengo-fan-token-offering-fto-to-launch-on-socios-com-on-tuesday-october-19th/); [CoinMarketCap fan token explainer](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/what-are-fan-tokens); [ThePuntersPage guide](https://www.thepunterspage.com/fan-tokens/)) Historically the original Socios design bonded fan tokens against locked CHZ; current public documentation emphasizes the FTO fixed-price sale plus market float rather than a live bonding curve, and Chiliz has announced newer "mint and burn" gamified fan token tokenomics (Fan Token Play) executed via smart contracts. REPORTED, mechanics thinly documented. ([Chiliz mint-and-burn announcement](https://www.chiliz.com/chiliz-group-announces-gamified-fan-tokens-including-disruptive-new-mint-and-burn-tokenomics/))

Node-layer utility is consumptive and non-financial in form: poll voting weighted by holdings, contests, meet-and-greets, collectibles, loyalty rewards; holding grants permanent access. FACT. ([Socios fan tokens](https://www.socios.com/fan-tokens/); [Cardlines](https://cardlines.com/what-are-socios-com-fan-tokens/))

Money flows. Clubs earn primarily from the FTO raise plus ongoing platform activity; Chiliz reports over 700M USD in revenue delivered to sports partners over the program's life across roughly 170 partner organizations (about 80 on Chiliz Chain). REPORTED (Chiliz's own figure). ([KuCoin 2026 explainer](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/chiliz-explained-the-backbone-of-sports-fan-tokens-in-2026); [Chiliz five-year retrospective](https://www.chiliz.com/fan-tokens-turn-five/)) Barcelona's initial 2020 FTO famously generated 1.3M USD in under two hours. REPORTED. ([Fox coverage](https://www.foxnews.com/sports/barcelona-generates-1-3-million-with-virtual-fan-tokens)) In the current model, 10 percent of fan token sale revenue across supported chains is earmarked for CHZ buybacks, an explicit master-token value-accrual wire. REPORTED. ([KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/chiliz-explained-the-backbone-of-sports-fan-tokens-in-2026))

What accrued where, 2019 to 2026. The master token got the structural utility: with the 2023 Chiliz Chain launch CHZ became gas and staking collateral; the May 2024 Dragon8 "Tokenomics 2.0" upgrade (passed on-chain with 85 percent support) added an 8.8 percent initial inflation decaying toward 1.88 percent, 65 percent of emissions to validators/delegators, and EIP-1559 fee burning, so usage makes CHZ scarcer. FACT. ([Chiliz docs, Dragon8 proposal](https://docs.chiliz.com/chiliz-chain-changelog/governance-proposals-and-decisions/may-2024-dragon8-tokenomics-2.0-proposal); [Blockchain.news](https://blockchain.news/news/chiliz-chz-chain-announces-tokenomics-20-with-inflation-model-and-burn-mechanism); [Chiliz whitepaper v1.0, Aug 2025](https://www.chiliz.com/CHZ_whitepaper_v1.0.pdf)) The node tokens got the drawdown: PSG peaked at 49.90 USD on 2021-08-09 (Messi signing), fell below 5 USD by May 2022, below 3 USD by early 2024, trading near 2.80 USD even on a Champions League final spike in May 2025; Barcelona sat near 3 USD in 2024. Exchange delistings (CoinDCX delisted PSG June 2025) further eroded access. FACT on prices, REPORTED on causes. ([Cryptopolitan PSG history](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/psg-coin-price-prediction/); [Blockchainreporter](https://blockchainreporter.net/psg-coin-price-prediction-will-this-football-fan-token-regain-value/); [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/psg-fan-token-jumps-23/)) Chiliz's own 2024 aggregate claims (fan token holder count up 200 percent year over year, average total fan token market cap 260M EUR) show the category alive but small. REPORTED. ([Chiliz retrospective](https://www.chiliz.com/fan-tokens-turn-five/)) Chiliz is now pushing a 2026 to 2030 "SportFi" vision including US-market fan tokens and a "sovereign stadium" narrative. REPORTED. ([Chiliz 2030 vision](https://www.chiliz.com/vision2030/); [Chiliz 2025 chain review](https://www.chiliz.com/the-chiliz-chain-in-2025-from-fan-tokens-to-a-sovereign-stadium/))

INFERENCE, the core Chiliz lesson for us: the center kept the durable asset (settlement rail, gas, staking, buyback flow, the partnership pipeline) while node-token holders absorbed nearly all price risk, because node tokens were priced by speculation on sentiment events (signings, finals) rather than by any claim on node cash flow or verified asset state. A design that wants sub-tokens to hold value must give them something the fan token never had: a wired link to node-level economics or center-owned asset state.

### 1.2 Other hub-and-spoke systems

Exchange token plus launchpad (BNB). The hub token is the mandatory access key: Binance Launchpad allocates new-project tokens pro-rata to committed BNB, and Launchpool farms new tokens against staked BNB. Demand for every spoke launch routes through the hub asset. This is the strongest-performing hub-token pattern in crypto history, but it rides on an exchange's external cash-flow engine, not on the spokes themselves. FACT on mechanics. ([Coin Bureau Launchpad vs Launchpool](https://coinbureau.com/analysis/binance-launchpad-vs-launchpool); [99Bitcoins review](https://99bitcoins.com/best-crypto-launchpads/binance-launchpad-review/))

Cosmos hub and zones, the anti-pattern. Hundreds of chains use the Cosmos SDK and IBC; fees flow to the app-chains, not the Hub; dYdX, Osmosis, and Injective benefited from the stack without accruing value to ATOM; Interchain Security (the Hub renting security to zones) failed to find product-market fit and is being deprecated; Cosmos Labs is now overhauling ATOM from high-inflation staking toward a fee-based model. The ecosystem succeeded technically while the hub token lost economic relevance. FACT/REPORTED. ([TipRanks analysis](https://www.tipranks.com/news/cosmos-powers-the-internet-of-blockchains-can-atom-capture-the-value); [BlockEden on the Osmosis merger](https://blockeden.xyz/blog/2026/03/12/cosmosis-osmosis-cosmos-hub-merger-ecosystem-consolidation/); [ATOM Endgame forum essay](https://forum.cosmos.network/t/atom-endgame-moneyness-security-liquidity/12220); [BeInCrypto on the redesign](https://beincrypto.com/cosmos-atom-resign-price-decline/))

Blackbird (restaurant loyalty network), the franchise-shaped consumer case. Ben Leventhal's Blackbird runs FLY, earned by diners checking into restaurants and spendable across the network, on Flynet, a Base layer-3 that went mainnet February 2025; restaurants pay platform fees in FLY; a second token, F2, handles gas with 13 percent airdropped to early users and restaurants; the company raised 50M USD in April 2025. The design intent is a circulating pool of value among many independent venues, network effects without giving any single venue a tradable instrument. FACT/REPORTED. ([CoinDesk on Flynet mainnet](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2025/02/26/blackbird-blockchain-restaurant-loyalty-app-goes-live-with-flynet-mainnet); [TechCrunch on the raise](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/08/blackbird-gobbles-up-50m-for-its-blockchain-based-payment-loyalty-app-for-restaurants); [Leventhal's FLY essay](https://www.thesupersonic.blackbird.xyz/p/fly-designed-to-make-restaurants); [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91162243/restaurant-loyalty-program-blackbird-is-launching-payments-and-introducing-guest-value-scores))

INFERENCE: across these systems, the hub token holds value only where a mechanism forces spoke activity through it (BNB launch allocation, CHZ as FTO settlement plus buyback, FLY as network fee currency). Where routing is optional (Cosmos), the hub starves. Our master token needs mandatory settlement or fee-routing roles at every node touchpoint.

## 2. Dual-token and multi-token design lessons

### 2.1 The failure record

Axie Infinity (AXS governance, SLP uncapped utility). SLP emissions scaled linearly with player count; the only meaningful sink was breeding; when growth stalled the faucet kept running and the sink dried up. SLP fell from 0.39 USD (July 2021) to under 0.004 USD by mid-2022; AXS and SLP both dropped over 98 percent from peaks; Sky Mavis cut Adventure-mode emissions in February 2022 explicitly "to avoid permanent economic collapse." FACT. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2022/02/08/axie-infinity-reduces-slp-emissions-to-prevent-collapse); [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/axie-infinity-token-axs-gains-40-after-taking-steps-to-avoid-permanent-economic-collapse); [ChainClarity tokenomics review](https://chainclarity.io/axie-infinity); [data-driven analysis](https://medium.com/@laurainweb3/axie-infinity-a-data-driven-analysis-of-gamefi-tokenomics-and-dao-evolution-f2dd16ab5c18))

StepN (GMT capped governance, GST uncapped reward). Same skeleton, faster death: GST peaked at 9.36 USD in April 2022 and fell 98 percent in two months once user growth slowed in May/June 2022; more GST was earned by walking than consumed by sneaker upgrades; as GST fell, the payback case for buying sneakers deteriorated, cutting new-user inflow, cutting GST demand further. Active users fell 80 to 90 percent within months. Vader Research called the collapse inevitable ex ante on the emission/sink math. FACT on prices and mechanism, REPORTED on ex-ante calls. ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/is-move-to-earn-dead-stepns-gst-token-tumbles-98-in-2-months/); [Vader Research](https://defivader.medium.com/why-stepns-collapse-is-inevitable-5259a6584a98); [Phemex explainer](https://phemex.com/academy/what-is-stepn-gmt-token-move-to-earn))

Terra (LUNA/UST), the seigniorage limit case. UST held its peg only through mint/burn arbitrage against LUNA; when UST slipped below peg in May 2022, redemptions hyper-inflated LUNA supply, crashing it from over 80 USD to fractions of a cent within a week and vaporizing roughly 45 to 60B USD. The lesson generalizes beyond stablecoins: any two-token system where one token's stability is defended by minting the other has a confidence-run failure mode that reserves cannot outrun. FACT. ([Forbes death-spiral account](https://www.forbes.com/sites/rahulrai/2022/05/17/the-death-spiral-how-terras-algorithmic-stablecoin-came-crashing-down/); [OKX retrospective](https://www.okx.com/en-us/learn/terra-collapse-ust-luna-stablecoin); [simulation-based academic study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2096720925000910))

### 2.2 The reflexivity failure mode, named

In all three collapses the token's demand curve was the product: users bought in to earn the token, so demand was a function of expected token appreciation, which was a function of demand. That loop is stable only while growth accelerates. The design tell is an uncapped emission paid for participation, with sinks that are themselves investments in future emission capacity (breeding Axies, minting sneakers), so even the "sinks" were levered long the token. INFERENCE, synthesized from the sources above; the emission/sink framing is standard in the post-mortem literature. ([DeSpread on StepN economy and content design](https://research.despread.io/web3-game-stepn/); [sustainability study of P2E token economies](https://ideas.repec.org/a/gam/jsusta/v16y2024i15p6587-d1447864.html))

### 2.3 What correlates with survival

From the post-2022 design literature and the surviving economies: (a) demand exogenous to token price, meaning people would consume the thing even if the token never appreciated; (b) significant, recurring, scheduled sinks live before launch (crafting, upgrades, fees), not one-time burn events; (c) emissions gated by consumption rather than by headcount; (d) governance/points layers kept non-tradable or soul-bound until the economy has organic demand; (e) revenue from outside the token loop (Chiliz partner fees, Binance trading fees, Blackbird restaurant fees) funding the center. REPORTED/INFERENCE. ([Sabol, lessons from web3 gaming](https://medium.com/@Steven_Sabol/lessons-from-web3-gaming-sustainable-economics-and-tokenomics-on-the-blockchain-4abcc7939c9c); [sustainable web3 economy breakdown](https://adamhannigan81.medium.com/web3-game-breakdown-designing-sustainable-economies-4fc0506c8962); [ResearchGate P2E sustainability paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/382817943_Exploring_the_Sustainable_Development_of_Web3_Game_Token_Economy)) The academic finding that user churn spikes when token profitability ends is the quantitative face of the reflexivity trap. REPORTED.

INFERENCE for our architecture: the non-tradable consumptive member layer at each node is exactly the survival-correlated feature, and its integrity is a hard invariant. The moment member-layer instruments become tradable (secondary markets in passes, transferable memberships with price discovery), the reflexive loop re-enters through the side door.

## 3. Treasury and liquidity design for a small-cap issuer

### 3.1 Supply schedules and vesting

The 2024 cohort demonstrated the low-float/high-FDV trap: launch with a sliver circulating, optically small market cap, enormous FDV, then years of unlocks. Binance Research (May 2024, "Low Float & High FDV: How Did We Get Here?") estimated roughly 155B USD of tokens unlocking 2024 to 2030, structural sell pressure absent matching capital inflow. FACT. ([Binance Research PDF](https://public.bnbstatic.com/static/files/research/low-float-and-high-fdv-how-did-we-get-here.pdf); [TokenInsight definition](https://tokeninsight.com/en/tokenwiki/all/what-is-low-float-high-fdv)) Unlock effects are not one-day events; they depress price for months, and cliff-heavy schedules are worse than long linear ones. Keyrock's data showed the larger the launch FDV, the larger the subsequent drawdown. REPORTED. ([MEXC unlock explainer](https://blog.mexc.com/crypto-knowledge/what-are-token-unlock-and-vesting-why-tracking-token-unlock-schedules-is-critical-for-investors/); [Tokenomist glossary](https://tokenomist.ai/learn/glossary-term/low-float-high-fdv)) Design consensus post-2024: launch with a meaningful float (higher float, lower FDV), long linear vesting for insiders, and emissions that respond to usage. REPORTED. ([Tokenomics-learning on launch models](https://tokenomics-learning.com/en/high-float-low-fdv-token-launch-strategy/))

### 3.2 Market making and its regulatory hazards

Small-cap issuers face listing-volume pressure and routinely hire market makers; the line between liquidity provision and manipulation is where enforcement lives. On 2024-10-09 the SEC charged four market makers (ZM Quant, Gorbit, CLS Global, MyTrade) for generating artificial token volume; a 2026 DOJ sting found wash trading "far more common" than expected; academic work (Cong, Li, Tang, Yang, "Crypto Wash Trading") and a Forbes study estimating 51 percent of reported BTC volume as fake frame the base rate. Wash trading concentrates in exactly our category: small tokens on lightly regulated venues, where inflated volume simulates liquidity and demand. FACT on the enforcement actions and studies. ([CertiK on manipulation](https://www.certik.com/blog/unmasking-crypto-market-manipulation-wash-trading-spoofing-and-more); [CoinDesk on the DOJ sting](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/04/02/doj-sting-exposes-crypto-wash-trading-continues-to-be-far-more-common-than-expected); [Cong et al. paper](https://cowles.yale.edu/sites/default/files/2022-11/cryptowashtrading040521-crypto-wash-trading.pdf); [Chainalysis 2025 manipulation report](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/crypto-market-manipulation-wash-trading-pump-and-dump-2025/)) Standard MM structures (token loans with call options to the MM) also transfer upside to the MM and create sell pressure at option strikes; treat MM agreements as material economic terms, not plumbing. INFERENCE from practitioner literature.

### 3.3 Liquidity concentration vs fragmentation, and what niche liquidity really looks like

Fan tokens are the ground truth for sub-token liquidity. Category-level numbers look respectable (Binance processed over 3.2B USD annual fan token volume across 45+ organizations in 2025; occasional event spikes like the November 2022 World Cup at 300M USD average daily, or a 1B USD 24-hour category print) but individual mid-tier tokens are thin: Galatasaray around 72k USD daily volume, AS Roma around 2.7M USD on a spike day and falling 60 percent day over day; volumes swing 80+ percent on news and collapse between events; delistings (CoinDCX/PSG) remove venues entirely. FACT on the spot figures, dated 2026 checks. ([Dataintelo market report](https://dataintelo.com/report/fan-token-market); [Benzinga on World Cup volumes](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/cryptocurrency/22/11/29900270/fifa-world-cup-frenzy-triggers-volatile-fan-token-trading-as-nov-average-daily-volumes-hit); [The Block on the 1B spike](https://www.theblock.co/post/319978/fan-token-trade-volume-1-billion); [CoinGecko GAL](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/galatasaray-fan-token); [CoinGecko ASR](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/as-roma-fan-token))

INFERENCE: with N sub-tokens, liquidity fragments roughly N ways while the master token concentrates the deepest book. Realistic planning assumption for a niche sub-token: five to six figures USD daily volume in steady state, event-driven spikes, wide spreads, and material price impact on any treasury-sized order. This argues for (a) keeping the tradable surface small (master token deep, sub-tokens few and large, or sub-tokens issued only where a node has real standalone demand), (b) master-token-denominated routing so sub-token trades add master-token flow, and (c) never relying on sub-token liquidity for treasury operations.

## 4. Linking tokens to real cash flows and assets

### 4.1 The 2026 US posture

Two regimes now coexist. First, the SEC/CFTC joint interpretive guidance of 2026-03-17 (a 68-page release) established five categories: digital commodities, digital collectibles, digital tools, stablecoins, and digital securities; Howey still governs, classification follows "economic reality" not labels, and a token's status can shift over time as facts change. FACT. ([Morgan Lewis analysis](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2026/03/crypto-clarity-sec-and-cftc-issue-comprehensive-crypto-asset-guidance-part-1); [Orrick](https://www.orrick.com/en/Insights/2026/04/SEC-Issues-Interpretive-Guidance-on-Crypto-Asset-Classification); [fintech blog summary](https://www.fintechanddigitalassets.com/2026/04/sec-clarifies-the-application-of-the-securities-laws-to-cryptoassets/)) Chair Atkins's Project Crypto (November 2025) promises a "Regulation Crypto" rulemaking wave in 2026 including an innovation exemption. REPORTED. ([Latham US crypto policy tracker](https://www.lw.com/en/us-crypto-policy-tracker/regulatory-developments)) Second, the CLARITY Act: passed the House July 2025, cleared Senate Banking 15-9 in May 2026, merged Senate text landed 2026-07-22, but votes were not there before the August recess; its key device is a decentralization threshold (no person or affiliated group over 20 percent of supply or controlling governance) defaulting mature tokens to CFTC commodity jurisdiction. Not yet law as of this writing. FACT on status. ([Datawallet CLARITY explainer](https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/clarity-act-explained); [CoinUnited research](https://coinunited.io/en/research/crypto/crypto-clarity-act-sec-rules-traders-guide-2026))

Implication for a center-and-nodes issuer: our center, by construction, holds the brand/territory and the real assets, and will fail any decentralization threshold. INFERENCE: plan for the master token to be analyzed as an investment contract unless structured otherwise; do not build on the hope of commodity treatment.

### 4.2 The three linkage patterns

Buyback-and-burn (or buyback-and-hold). The 2025-2026 mainstream: Hyperliquid routes roughly 97 percent of fees to HYPE buybacks (fund crossed 2B USD by May 2026); Aave's DAO runs a permanent 50M USD/year buyback; Jupiter locks 50 percent of protocol fees into three-year JUP reserves (Litterbox Trust); dYdX allocates 25 percent of net fees to buybacks. FACT. ([BeInCrypto survey](https://beincrypto.com/crypto-token-buybacks-boosting-value/); [DWF Labs research](https://www.dwf-labs.com/research/547-token-buybacks-in-web3); [blocmates 2025 roundup](https://www.blocmates.com/articles/top-projects-with-buybacks-2025-edition)) Caveats: an unlocks.app study of the roughly 19B USD 2025-2026 "buyback meta" found only 2 of 11 major programs actually shrink net supply (emissions outrun burns), and buybacks are not a blanket safe harbor; the securities analysis still depends on what was promised and the buyer-team relationship. REPORTED/FACT. ([unlocks.app insights](https://insights.unlocks.app/buyback-and-burn-explained-what-they-are-who-is-doing-them-and-whether-they-actually-work/); [WEEX regulatory framing](https://www.weex.com/news/detail/the-revival-of-token-buybacks-navigating-new-regulatory-landscapes-245924))

Distributions (fee switch, dividends). Explicit pro-rata cash distributions are the strongest Howey trigger; projects historically avoided them and the 2025-2026 revival still concentrates in buybacks rather than distributions; token holders lack the legal cash-flow claims shareholders have, and governance can change the terms at any time. REPORTED. ([BlockBeats fee-switch survey](https://m.theblockbeats.info/en/news/57258); [crypto.news on revenue-driven valuations](https://crypto.news/bitwise-cio-sees-crypto-valuations-doubling-on-token-revenue/))

Registered security token. The clean path for the real-asset leg: St. Regis Aspen (2018) raised 18M USD under Reg D 506(c), accredited-only, 1 USD tokens representing shares in a single-asset REIT SPV owning 18.9 percent of the resort, issued and traded via Templum Markets, an SEC/FINRA-registered broker-dealer ATS. The wrapper approach (SPV shares tokenized, not "utility" claims on the building) is the durable pattern, and the SEC's January 2026 statement on tokenized securities reaffirmed that tokenizing a security changes nothing about its regulation. FACT. ([Krypital case study](https://medium.com/krypital/security-token-case-analysis-aspen-coin-the-first-real-estate-security-token-offering-bbbcc52ace5); [SEC statement on tokenized securities](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/corp-fin-statement-tokenized-securities-012826-statement-tokenized-securities); [Norton Rose on the guidance](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/f587fc3c/sec-issues-guidance-on-tokenized-securities))

### 4.3 Non-US postures that bind a sports-adjacent issuer

EU/MiCA: fan-token-like instruments fall in the residual "other crypto-asset" bucket; classification follows actual rights conferred, not labels; utility tokens granting access to an existing, functioning service are exempt from the public-offer whitepaper regime (with conditions). FACT on the framework. ([Legalnodes MiCA guide](https://www.legalnodes.com/article/mica-regulation-explained); [ESMA MiCA page](https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/digital-finance-and-innovation/markets-crypto-assets-regulation-mica)) UK: fan tokens are "qualifying cryptoassets" under FCA financial-promotion rules since October 2023, classified Restricted Mass Market Investments (mandatory risk warnings, no refer-a-friend incentives); the ASA banned Arsenal's Socios fan token ads for trivializing investment risk; broadcast crypto ads to mainstream audiences were banned September 2024; first FCA enforcement under the regime landed February 2026. FACT. ## 5. Literature and what the tokenomics team should model first

### 5.1 Worth reading

Valuation theory: Cong, Li, and Wang, "Tokenomics: Dynamic Adoption and Valuation" (Review of Financial Studies 34, 2021, 1105-1155): equilibrium token value aggregates heterogeneous users' transactional demand rather than discounted cash flows; adoption follows an S-curve (slow, then volatile, then tapering); tokens accelerate adoption by letting users capitalize platform growth. The right formal frame for our member-driven demand. FACT (paper exists and says this). ([RFS](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/34/3/1105/5891182); [NBER w27222](https://www.nber.org/papers/w27222); [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3222802))

Systems theory: Voshmgir and Zargham, "Foundations of Cryptoeconomic Systems" (WU Vienna working paper): cryptoeconomies as complex socioeconomic systems requiring economic-systems-engineering methods; purpose-driven tokens as incentive instruments. The intellectual root of the BlockScience/token-engineering school. FACT. ([paper PDF](https://research.wu.ac.at/files/19008630/Foundations%20of%20Cryptoeconomic%20Systems.pdf); [summary](https://medium.com/crypto3conomics/foundations-of-cryptoeconomic-systems-beb1ab41d9fb))

Simulation practice: cadCAD (open-source Python, BlockScience lineage; state variables, policy functions, deterministic and stochastic runs; used for MakerDAO, Uniswap v3, and Ethereum economic-security modeling) and TokenSPICE for EVM-in-the-loop agent-based simulation; commercial practitioners in this lane include BlockScience and Gauntlet. FACT/REPORTED. ([Token Flows cadCAD guide](https://tokenflows.xyz/posts/workingwithcadcad/); [simulation tools survey](https://medium.com/coinmonks/simulation-and-verification-tools-for-token-engineering-its-usage-8917dbd29092); [Hacken tokenomics design principles](https://hacken.io/discover/tokenomics-design-principles/))

Failure-mode empirics: the Binance low-float/high-FDV report, the Cong et al. wash-trading paper, the Terra simulation study, and the Vader Research StepN pre-mortem, all cited in sections 2 and 3, are the empirical canon for what to stress-test.

### 5.2 What to model first, in order

1. The reflexivity stress test: an agent-based model of the member layer with demand as a function of consumptive value only, then re-run with a speculative demand component, and measure how much speculative share the system tolerates before node-token drawdowns propagate to the center. This is the Axie/StepN test applied to our topology.
2. Master-token flow accounting: every wired route by which node activity forces master-token demand (FTO-style settlement, fee routing, buyback percentages), against emission and unlock schedules, Cosmos-style leakage as the null hypothesis.
3. Liquidity realism: order-book depth scenarios per sub-token at fan-token-observed volumes (10^4 to 10^6 USD daily), treasury operation price impact, and MM agreement terms as explicit model parameters.
4. The RWA linkage loop: how verified asset state changes sub-token demand, and whether that loop is stabilizing (asset improves, consumption rises) or reflexive (asset value priced off token, token priced off asset claim), Terra as the degenerate case.
5. Unlock/vesting overhang: launch float, insider schedules, and the FDV drawdown curve from the Keyrock/Binance data as priors.

## Implications for our design

- Wire master-token value accrual explicitly at every node touchpoint (settlement, fees, buyback percentage of node raises, Chiliz's 10 percent is a live benchmark); assume zero accrual by default, per Cosmos.
- Keep the member layer non-tradable as a hard invariant, including secondary-market leakage (transferable passes with price discovery re-create the reflexive loop); this is simultaneously the survival feature and the marketing-compliance firewall in the UK/EU.
- Give sub-tokens something fan tokens never had: a verifiable link to node economics or center-owned asset state; sentiment-only sub-tokens are a documented 90-95 percent drawdown pattern with five-figure daily liquidity.
- Structure the stadium/RWA leg as a registered security in an SPV wrapper (Aspen Reg D 506(c) pattern, broker-dealer ATS for secondary), separate instrument from the utility-layer tokens; never blend the claims.
- Choose buyback-linked (not distribution-linked) cash-flow expression for the master token, and size it so it actually nets against emissions; document that it is discretionary and carries no promised return.
- Assume the master token gets securities-style analysis in the US (a franchise center cannot pass a decentralization threshold); design disclosures and offering path accordingly rather than betting on CLARITY.
- Launch high-float/low-FDV with long linear vesting; treat MM agreements as material economic terms and prohibit volume-simulation practices contractually (SEC charged four MMs for exactly this in October 2024).
- Fund the tokenomics team's first sprint on the section 5.2 model stack (cadCAD or equivalent), with the reflexivity stress test and master-token flow accounting as the two gating deliverables before any instrument design is frozen.
