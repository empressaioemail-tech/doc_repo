## The six rules

1. **Three lanes, never mixed.** Member lane: consumptive, non-tradable, never profit language. Capital lane: tradable on purpose, securities-side by design, tokenomics team plus counsel. Real-asset lane: center-owned, node-assembled, twin-disclosed, linked only through the capital lane.
2. **The center issues only what it owns.** Master and sub-tokens link to the center's own assets (brand, territory), never to membership interests in node entities.
3. **The economy must be solvent with the token turned off.** Member revenue stands alone; the token amplifies a working economy, it never is the economy.
4. **Every linkage claim is verifiable.** A sub-token's claims about its node (member graph, athlete assets, venue state) resolve against continuously maintained twins. No twin, no claim.
5. **Humans are twinned with consent and paid for participation.** Member accounts and athlete records are the person's own; value linked to a person routes value to that person; consent keys stay self-custodied.
6. **We own the model; partners get the flagship.** Every deployment feeds the template, never a partner's fork.
