## The position

A trust layer that earns its confidence and refuses to capture you.

In operational language: the verified ground truth layer that AI agents call when they operate on buildings, land, permits, code, zoning, or municipal records. Every agent acting on physical space needs jurisdiction aware context, and the substrate is that provider.

## The two roots

Everything rests on two commitments. They are not features and not strategy. They are the conscience of the company, the things held even when holding them costs a win.

**Calibration.** The relationship to truth. Confidence is earned, not asserted. A confidence number means something only when it is backed by a track record of being checked against what actually happened, so that when the system says it is highly confident, it has historically been right at that rate. Calibration is measurable, it improves with use, and its engine is network compounding: the judgment of the whole network makes every member sharper, and the value lands concretely on the contributor's own work, the architect's plans, the code reasoning, the city's review.

**Sovereignty.** The relationship to the person. Participants own their data and can leave. The substrate refuses data estate absorption, switching costs, and rip and replace. Ownership is the line least open to compromise.

These two are the mirror of the two ways an institution betrays trust: by claiming unearned authority, and by capturing you. The substrate is a refusal of both at once.

## How the roots relate

Calibration and sovereignty are usually in tension. The normal way to earn trust at scale is to become indispensable, which means lock in, which is the opposite of sovereignty. The bet is that calibration is a retention mechanism that is not lock in. People stay because the thing is right more often, not because they are trapped, which means the company can afford to let them leave. Calibration earns the right to offer sovereignty. The mental model is a battery: two poles, both required, and the tension between them is the source of the work, not a problem to be removed.

## The four commitments that serve the roots

The architecture expresses the two roots through four commitments. They are recoverable from the roots, which is why the framework is two things to remember, not six.

1. **AI accessible by default.** Agents read the substrate and write adjudications back. Serves both roots.
2. **Verifiable by default.** Provenance and reasoning are checkable, which is what makes each adjudication a usable calibration point and lets a participant verify the record without trusting the company. Serves both roots.
3. **Integrative by default.** Connects without absorbing, so outcome signal flows in from many systems and no source is captured on the way in. Serves both roots.
4. **Portable by default.** The participant can carry the data out. Serves sovereignty most purely.

## The value proposition

Other systems tell you how sure they are. This one tells you how often it has been right when it was that sure, it gets less wrong every time it is used, and it never owns you.

For the code-reasoning surface specifically, the position compresses to one line: **the code tells you the rule; Hauska tells you what it means for your building, reconciled with every other code that applies.** The code text is commodity and authoritative-elsewhere; the reasoning - what the rule means in context, harmonized across all applicable codes (the precedence/reconciliation engine, ADR-021), calibrated by outcome (arrow-two) - is the product. We store reasoning, not code text. ## The participation model

The reward for participation is intrinsic. Contributing feedback makes the contributor's own work better, so there is nothing to financialize and no token is needed. The model tiers by the calibration grade of the contribution.

- **Wide surface, browser extension.** High volume, lower grade signal. Free in exchange for contribution. This surface is a calibration and demand acquisition engine, read as acquisition cost, not a profit center.
- **Professionals, architects and engineers.** Expert grade judgment. Contribution earns credits against the monthly fee.
- **Cities.** The highest grade adjudication in the system. Returns the richest value, framed as cost recoupment, never profit.

The collective knowledge layer has both doors open. A participant can earn access through contribution or pay for it in cash. Contribution is a second currency accepted alongside cash, never instead of it.

Spillover is compensated. A contributor's adjudication improves the whole network, not only their own work, and that positive externality is compensated through a mutual or cooperative dividend, returned as reduced cost or a share of value, never as a tradeable token.

A guardrail protects sovereignty inside this model: the isolated tier stays genuinely complete and competent. It is never crippled to herd participants into the collective. The isolated product learns from its own user. The collective product learns from everyone. The collective is additively better, never the price of a working tool.

## The keystone

The city data sharing cooperative. Cities pool logged institutional experience and recoup technology and staff overhead, learning from each other rather than selling regulator knowledge to private parties. Structurally it is the same moat the incumbent COG aggregator holds, an inter governmental network that grows with every city and is hard to leave, except built on sovereignty terms the incumbent cannot match. The cooperative network is the moat. Non capture is the displacement wedge. The cooperative adopts existing legal structures, interlocal cooperation and COG style vehicles, designed with municipal counsel rather than invented.

## The architecture that already instantiates this

The stack embodies the theology more completely than the words have. IPFS is sovereignty made physical, content addressed and portable rather than held in a proprietary database. The ledger, already part of the SDK, is verifiable by default made cryptographic and is the substrate for attributing contribution and calibrating contributors, justified by letting a participant verify the record without trusting the company. The SDK payment layer is the value return rail, sitting exactly where the only legitimate monetary reward lives, the spillover. This infrastructure stays invisible. The buyer hears that the data is theirs and provably attributed to them, never the underlying mechanism.

## Go to market

Lead with connect your systems and become AI first. That is present tense single surface value that does not depend on a network existing yet. The cooperative is named as the trajectory. The rev share and IP profit are a latent bonus the buyer does not weigh up front.
