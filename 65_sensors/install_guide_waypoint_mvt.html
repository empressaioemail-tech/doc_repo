Sensor Installation Guide

Freeze & water watch — risers, common spaces, and one unit. About 1 hour total. No wiring; nothing beyond zip ties, tape, and one wrench turn at the pressure port.

Nothing needs pairing on site. Every sensor in this box was configured and tested before shipping. It is already talking to the gateway. Your whole job is: put each device in the right place, then send photos.

Each device has a tag matching a station below. Install in order — the gateway must go in first.

Station 1 Gateway — install this first GW-1

Location: office or mechanical room — somewhere central with an internet router or wall jack. Not inside a metal cabinet.

  1. Screw both antennas on and point them straight up.
  2. Plug the ethernet cable into the gateway, and the other end into the router or wall jack.
  3. Plug in the power adapter.
  4. Wait about 2 minutes. The status light should settle on solid green.
  5. Photograph it in place and move on.
Don't put it in a metal cabinet, a basement corner behind equipment, or unplug it after setup — every sensor reports through this box.
Station 2 Riser pipes — do this twice RT-1 · RT-2

Location: the two marked water risers (see the site notes sheet in the box). Pick the coldest accessible spot — near an exterior wall, at the lowest floor you can reach.

  1. Peel back (don't cut off) a hand-width of pipe insulation.
  2. Hold the metal probe tip flat against the bare pipe. Zip-tie it in two places, snug.
  3. Wrap the foam tape over the probe, then fold the insulation back over everything.
  4. Mount the white sensor body on the wall or strut nearby with a zip tie or command strip — hanging in air, not touching the pipe.
  5. Photograph it, then repeat at the second riser with RT-2.
Don't leave the probe dangling in air or outside the insulation — it must touch the pipe metal directly, or it reads room temperature instead of pipe temperature.
Station 3 Water heater — temp + leak MT-1 · LK-1

Location: the main water heater / mechanical room.

  1. Strap the MT-1 probe to the hot water outlet pipe, same method as the risers: tip on bare metal, two zip ties, tape over.
  2. Mount the MT-1 body on the wall nearby.
  3. Lay the LK-1 sensing tip flat in the drain pan (or on the floor's low point if there is no pan).
  4. Mount the LK-1 body up on the wall or the heater strap — above where water could ever pool.
  5. Photograph both.
Don't let the leak sensor body sit on the floor — only the tip on the cable is waterproof.
Station 4 Water pressure — supply line PS-1

Location: the threaded port named on your site notes sheet (a spare valve, the laundry tee, or the water heater drain). This is the one station that touches plumbing — it senses a leak anywhere in the building before water ever reaches a floor.

  1. Confirm the port's valve is closed.
  2. Wrap the sensor's brass threads with 3 wraps of the thread seal tape in the box (clockwise, looking at the tip).
  3. Thread it on hand-tight, then snug it about a quarter turn with a wrench. Do not crank it.
  4. Open the valve slowly all the way. Watch the joint for 30 seconds — it should stay completely dry.
  5. Mount the body on the wall above the port and photograph everything.
If it drips, or the threads don't match the port: close the valve, take it off, and text us a photo of the port. Skip this station — do not force it. Everything else still works.
Station 5 Common area AM-1

Location: main hallway or laundry room.

  1. Pick an interior wall spot at about chest height, away from vents, radiators, exterior doors, and direct sun.
  2. Stick it on with a command strip (or one screw through the bracket).
  3. Photograph it.
Station 6 The test unit AU-1 · LK-2

Location: the one unit that agreed to the pilot. Two devices, five minutes.

  1. Mount AU-1 on a living-area wall at chest height — same rules as the hallway sensor.
  2. Open the kitchen sink cabinet. Stick the LK-2 body to the cabinet side wall.
  3. Lay the sensing tip flat on the cabinet floor, under the drain trap.
  4. Photograph both. Done.
Final Verify & send
  1. Text all photos to the number on the site notes sheet, labeled by station.
  2. We check every sensor is reporting from our side and reply with a green-light or ask you to adjust one placement.
  3. Optional 10-second test: touch a wet rag to a leak sensor tip — you'll get a confirmation that the alert fired, then dry it off.

Gateway light quick reference

Solid greenWorking. Leave it alone. Blinking / amberStarting up or no internet — check the ethernet cable, give it 2 minutes. No lightNo power — check the outlet and adapter.

Install checklist

  • Gateway: solid green light
  • Riser 1 — probe strapped, insulation back over (RT-1)
  • Riser 2 — probe strapped, insulation back over (RT-2)
  • Water heater — probe on pipe (MT-1), leak tip in pan (LK-1)
  • Pressure sensor threaded on, valve open, joint dry (PS-1)
  • Common area sensor mounted (AM-1)
  • Unit — room sensor (AU-1) + under-sink leak tip (LK-2)
  • All photos sent
