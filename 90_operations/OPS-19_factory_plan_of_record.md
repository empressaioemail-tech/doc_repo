# OPS-19 — The Factory: plan of record

## Why this document exists

Three factories ran as hand-carried lanes with a human as the only joint, evidence in `P:/tmp`, holds in a file, and no owner for publish. The blueprint's rule register lists twenty-two `BP-` rules and twenty of them have no executor. This plan is the structural answer: the Factory is the executor column of that register, one row per layer, stage, or capability, each naming the rules it arms, the instrument that grades it, and what blocks it, plus an append-only amendment log. Texas cleanup rows stay `P-` in OPS-16 and are referenced here; no row lives in two plans.

**Definition of done.** Texas complete on three finish lines graded separately by rule id: county manifest 254 x rails every node `populated` or `not-applicable` or `absent-verified` with scope; city manifest 1,223 x rails the same; a verify walk of known parcels on production Smart Site passes. Then one Utah county from discovery to visible on production through every layer and stage. A row graded by narration or by percentage fails.

## Governing rules

1. **The baseline table never gets edited.** Every scope change is a dated row in AMENDMENTS.
2. **No dispatch without a row.** Every dispatch names `PLAN-ROW: F-xx`. Enforced by `scripts/dispatch.mjs` and the canon-gate hook through `_catalog/plan_registry.json`.
3. **Statuses re-grade only by instrument** (Factory ledger, SQL, `gh`, live probe on the target site, `50_grading` rule-id output). The GRADE LOG records each pass.
4. **The model law wins.** `19`, `_blueprint/10_model`, `_blueprint/20_pipeline`, `_blueprint/40_rule_register`, `51`, `24`, the adopted layering target state, and the published package as tiebreaker. Where a row or a design disagrees with them, the row is the defect.
5. **One machine.** Nothing reaches a serving store except through L4 publish; only reconciled atoms enter the served graph, only through views. Every stage is a run with a record and a termination record; refusals are runs.
6. **Staging first, identical job second.** A production publish with no passed staging sibling refuses.
7. **Scoped leases.** One bulk writer per `(store, entity_type, county_fips)`; one heavy-scan scope per database. Custody is a table. This supersedes the OPS-16 write-slot law for Factory runs once F-02 lands, and AGENT_CONTRACT section 3 is amended at that time.
8. **Option A on the existing path.** P-82-lite lands as a bug fix with BP-WRITE-01; Bexar 48029 finishes on the current shape; no new county is written on the old shape. New fills wait for the conformant stage E writer.
9. **Agents propose, the operator approves, the machine records.** State recipes, adapter specs, setback tables, registry freezes, cert grades, metro re-acquires, and tier 3 adjudications are approvals in the console with evidence attached.
10. **Fewer agents, tighter contracts, harder gates.** Every lane cites the row and the stage; verification is never delegated below the lane planner; subagents do not commit.
11. **No regression of completed work.** Existing rows are adopted as level-one instruments; upgrades write provenance and verdicts; P-69 declares the unrecoverable. A row that would delete or overwrite adopted data without a verify walk is a defect.

## Lineage

Predecessors: OPS-14, OPS-16 (P-rows), `27a_jurisdiction_factory_engine_spec.md` (ledger is the product; G1 to G8), the three factory runbooks, `_inbox/2026-08-25_factory_operating_instructions.md`, the 2026-08-26 loader program (P-81 through P-84) with its adversarial review.

**Baseline v0 (2026-08-26 09:33, unratified, superseded the same day before any dispatch compiled against it).** Fourteen rows F-00 to F-14 named stations after the three factories (acquire, fabric, depth, publish, verify) and drained borrowed keys into per-parcel rows. The operator's question about the shape of nodes, atoms and edges surfaced that the design used the store's current shape rather than the model law. v1 below re-baselines on the four layers and five stages, adds the contract-type dependency (F-15), the shared resolution stage (F-16), reconcile and promote (F-17), intensional demotion (F-18), replay and idempotency (F-19), and records option A on the existing path. Row ids F-00 to F-14 keep their numbers where the subject survived; their scope is restated.
