# Positioning — Smart Site + Smart City

External-altitude. How we say it to a realtor, a developer, a city, a buyer — not how we reason internally.

## THE ONE-LINER
A smart site is everything you need to know about a place, in one place.
A smart city is a whole community of smart sites — connected, current, and usable.

## SMART SITE

### What it is (plain)
A smart site is any place — a lot, a building, a corner, the park you're standing in — with all the information that governs it pulled together and made usable: the property lines, what you can build, the setbacks, the flood risk, the terrain, the codes, the utilities. One place, all the layers, always current.

### The problem it kills
Today that information is scattered across a dozen systems that don't talk to each other — the county GIS, the zoning PDF, the FEMA map, the utility records, the code book. Answering one real question ("what can I build here, and what's the risk?") means logging into everything and hoping it's current. Point solutions. Disparate apps. Switching between systems. Everybody hates it, and nobody's data agrees with anybody else's.

### What we do differently
We put it all on one map, connected, and we make it answer questions — not just show you data, but tell you what it MEANS for this place, cited to where it came from, with a confidence level and a date. Two ways in: a human opens the app; an AI agent calls it directly. Same truth, either door.

### The X-ray
A smart site X-ray is the deep read on a place. Not a folder of documents — an X-ray: you see through the surface to every layer underneath (what you can build, the buildable envelope drawn, flood and drainage, terrain, codes, utilities), each one sourced and dated. It's the thing a realtor shares with a client, an architect hands to a reviewer, a developer runs before they buy. "Here's the X-ray of your smart site."

### Who it's for (external ICPs)
- Realtors / brokers: "look like the most informed person in the room — share your client a smart site."
- Architects / developers: "run the X-ray before you design or buy — know what the code lets you build and what the water does to it."
- Investors: "diligence a place in minutes, cited, defensible."

### The line that lands
"I'll share your smart site with you." — a realtor says it, the client gets it, nobody had to explain a single piece of jargon.

## SMART CITY

### What it is (plain)
A smart city is a whole community's worth of smart sites, connected into one operating picture — every parcel, road, utility, permit, and record, current and talking to each other, with the right people seeing the right things.

### The problem it kills (for cities)
A city runs on fifteen vendor systems that don't connect — permits here, finance there, fleet somewhere else, records in email and PDFs that evaporate as data. Staff switch between all of them. The city's own reality is fragmented across tools it doesn't control, and the durable record of what was decided and why just... disappears.

### What we do differently
We connect a city's systems into one place, so staff stop app-switching and start seeing the whole city at once — and underneath, the city's information becomes a durable, current, access-controlled record it actually owns. Public information is public; a department's private data stays private to the people it authorizes. The city's reality stops being scattered copies and becomes one operating truth.

### The line that lands
"Stop switching between fifteen systems. Your whole city, one place, current — and the record of it is finally durable."

## THE RULE (internal reminder — do not violate in market copy)
NEVER lead externally with: "digital twin," "RWA," "tokenization," "atom/node substrate," "on-chain," "blockchain." Over 100 people tested: unless they're web3-native, "digital twin" means nothing. The felt pain is DISPARATE SYSTEMS / APP-SWITCHING and SCATTERED, STALE INFO. Sell the relief. The web3/RWA audience finds the deeper capability on its own and speaks that language natively — we don't need to lead with it. (Full two-altitude rule: doc 42.)

## POSITIONING SPINE (the through-line across both)
One place. All the layers. Always current. Cited. Yours.
- Smart site = that, for a place.
- Smart city = that, for a community of places.
- The X-ray = the deep read of one.
