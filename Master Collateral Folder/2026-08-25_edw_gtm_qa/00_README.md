## Space rules

1. **This space is GTM and QA.** Positioning, pricing, demo script, FAQ, glossary. Nothing else.
2. **Do not upload** factory notes, inbox closes, SHA pins, county leftover logs, atom or substrate docs, SmartCity OS packs, or Command Center runbooks. Those stay in doc_repo.
3. **Do not invent claims.** If a sentence is not in 01, 02, 04, or 05, do not put it on a slide or a listing. Ask Nick.
4. **Two altitudes stay split.** Market copy never leads with digital twin, atom, node, graph, substrate, RWA, tokenization, on-chain, or blockchain.
5. **The live product is the demo.** https://smartsite.cloud. There is no sales call on this product.
6. **When the product changes,** refresh 03 first. Pricing changes refresh 02. Claims changes refresh 01, 04, and 05 together.
7. **doc_repo `_smartsite_masters/` is the legal register.** If this pack and the masters disagree, the masters win and this pack gets a dated refresh. Do not edit the masters from this space.
8. **SmartCity OS is a different product and a different space.** Do not mix city pitches here.

## What this pack will not answer

How the factory onboarded a county. How inspect binds a vintage. How billing webhooks work. Those questions go back to Nick or the planner, not into this project.
