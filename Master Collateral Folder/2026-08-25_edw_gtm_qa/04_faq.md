# FAQ

Speak these. Do not paste them onto a landing page without a pass from Nick.

**What is Smart Site?**
Everything you need to know about a place, in one place. Lines, what you can build, setbacks, flood, terrain, codes, utilities. One map. Cited. Current.

**Who is it for?**
Professionals who analyze property for a living. The homeowner gets a share. The professional is the buyer.

**What does it tell me that the county map does not?**
The county map shows where the lines are. Smart Site says what they mean for this lot, and shows the receipts.

**What is the X-ray?**
The deep read of one smart site. Envelope drawn, flood and drainage, terrain, codes, utilities, each sourced and dated. Not a folder of PDFs.

**What other reports are there?**
Flood and Drainage is the other live report. Feasibility and Comparison are reports on the locked menu. They are not live generate paths today; do not pitch them as live. Comparison is also the side-by-side tool. Brief is the inspect dock, not a report. Records is a courthouse request, not a report.

**Is this a valuation tool?**
No. We tell you what you can do with a place, not what it is worth.

**Wasn't this Property Explorer?**
Yes. That name is retired. The product is Smart Site.

**What is your coverage?**
Nationwide United States, rolling. Open the parcel. If a layer is not verified there, the product says so. We will not recite a county list, and we will not "check and get back to you."

**Where does the data come from?**
Public records: county parcels, city codes, FEMA, terrain, hydrography, licensed code libraries where we have the right to use them. Every fact carries a source.

**What if you do not have a fact?**
The card says it is not verified and names the miss. That is the reason you can trust the facts that are present.

**Is it accurate enough to build on?**
Accurate enough to decide with. Approximate, not survey grade. Not a boundary survey. Not a permit. Verify with the city and licensed professionals.

**How do I know the AI is not making it up?**
It can only cite records that exist for that property. Every citation opens. If there is no record, it says so. There is no free-floating chatbot.

**Who owns my research?**
You do. Public facts stay public. Your saves, notes, and attachments stay yours.

**What does it cost?**
Free to browse and share. Solo $49/mo or $490/yr. Studio $129/mo or $1,290/yr. Team $299/mo or $2,990/yr for up to 10 seats, then $25 a seat. Or $15 to unlock one property for 30 days.

**Why 30 days, not forever?**
The answer has a date on it. Codes get repealed. Thirty days is a freshness bound, not a punishment.

**Can I get a demo call?**
The inspect card is the demo. There is no sales team on this product. Cities and custom builds are different businesses.

**Can a city buy this?**
Cities buy SmartCity OS. Hand that conversation to that track.

**Can I just use ChatGPT?**
A general model will invent a setback. This assistant will not cite a record it does not have.

Do not name competitors in customer material. If someone already has a GIS or a data subscription, the line is: they still have to assemble the answer. That assembly is what they pay us for.
