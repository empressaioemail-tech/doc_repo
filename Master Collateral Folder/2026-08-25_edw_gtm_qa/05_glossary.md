# Words

**Smart Site.** The place and the product. One name.

**Smart Site X-ray.** The deep-read report. Never dossier, brief, or report pack.

**Flood and Drainage.** The other live report. A study, not a layer toggle.

**Feasibility.** A report on the locked menu. Not live generate today. Do not pitch it as live.

**Comparison.** A report on the locked menu and a tool (side-by-side Compare). The tool is live. The report generate path is not. Do not pitch the report as live.

**Brief.** The inspect dock. A tool, not a report.

**Records.** The courthouse request tool (P-85). Not a report.

**Smart City / SmartCity OS.** A community of smart sites, sold to cities, different pack.

**Inspect card.** The free click-a-parcel read.

**Buildable envelope.** The drawn leftover after setbacks. Approximate, not survey grade.

**Honest miss / not verified.** Designed refuse. Sell it.

**Share.** Free. The recipient sees what the sharer stored.

**Unlock.** $15, 30 days, one property. Freshness, not forever.

**Solo / Studio / Team.** The locked paid rungs. Not Pro. Not Hauska Pro.

**Property Explorer.** Retired. Historical only.

## Say freely

One place, all the layers, always current, cited. Answers, not rows. Honest when something is missing. Private research stays private.

## Never say

ROI or hours saved. Approves, permits, certifies. Named competitor attacks. 3D. A live capability that is not on smartsite.cloud today. Verbatim licensed code in marketing.

## Never lead with

Digital twin. Atom. Node. Graph. Substrate. RWA. Tokenization. On-chain. Blockchain. Hauska (the substrate brand). Cortex. Factory. FIPS leftover. SHA.

## Name hygiene

The product is Smart Site. The live URL is smartsite.cloud. Do not name internal services, engines, or vendors to a customer.
