# What Smart Site is

**A smart site is everything you need to know about a place, in one place.**

The product and the unit share the name on purpose. A realtor can say "I'll share your smart site with you" and nobody needs a glossary.

The spine: one place, all the layers, always current, cited, yours.

The report is the **Smart Site X-ray**. Never dossier. Never brief. Never Property Explorer in market copy.

## The problem

The facts that govern a lot live in a dozen systems that do not agree: county GIS, zoning PDF, FEMA map, utilities, the code book. One real question takes an afternoon of app-switching. Sell the relief: one map, cited, current, honest when something is missing.

## Who it is for

Professionals who analyze parcels for a living: agents and brokers, architects, investors, developers, land planners, civil consultants. Homeowners browse free and receive a share. They are the audience the professional performs for, not the buyer.

Cities buy SmartCity OS. That is a different door and a different pack.

## What only we can say

We sell answers, not rows. A flood zone plus where it came from, how current it is, and what it means for this lot is the product. A dump of layers is not.

The system fails honestly. "Not verified here" is a feature. A guess dressed as an answer is the defect.

Currency is checked, not assumed. A repealed code still posted as current is the kind of lie this product is built to refuse.

## What we never say

No cycle-time, savings, or ROI figures. No named-competitor attacks in customer material. No claim that the system approves, permits, or certifies anything. No 3D. No valuation. The locked report menu is the X-ray, the Flood and Drainage study, Feasibility, and Comparison. Comparison is also a tool (side-by-side). Brief is the inspect dock, not a report. Records is a request tool, not a report. Site plan and terrain are exports. Feasibility and Comparison generate are not live; do not pitch them as live until they ship.

## Two products, one layer

Smart Site proves the layer parcel by parcel. A smart city is a community of smart sites. Do not sell a city with this pack. Do not sell a realtor with the city pack.
