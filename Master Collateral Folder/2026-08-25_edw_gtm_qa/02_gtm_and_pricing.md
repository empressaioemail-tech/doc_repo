# Who pays and what it costs

**Free browse, paid deep.** The map and the inspect card are genuinely useful without an account. Conversion happens at professional need, not at a login wall.

There is no sales team on Smart Site. The product is the demo. Team closes self-serve. Custom builds and cities are other businesses.

## Locked ladder

Do not re-derive these numbers. Monthly amounts locked 2026-08-10. Annual amounts locked 2026-08-24 (two months free). Present monthly as the default. Annual is the other toggle. The 10 × monthly note lives on the Team column, not as a header chip.

| Tier | Monthly | Annual | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | $0 | Map, layers, inspect card, save, 3 AI chats per property, share |
| Solo | $49 | $490 | X-ray, Flood and Drainage study, unlimited chat, unlimited properties |
| Studio | $129 | $1,290 | Solo plus site plan CAD (DXF, IFC), terrain export, owner data |
| Team | $299 up to 10 seats, then $25/seat | $2,990 | Studio as a firm. Shared saves, one bill |
| Prospect | coming soon | coming soon | Set-level answer. Studio and Team later. Do not sell it now |

**Property unlock:** $15 once, 30 days. Not forever. About 3.3 unlocks equal one Solo month. Surface expiry as freshness ("verified on [date]"), never as a meter.

Retired. Never quote: Pro $149 / $99, unlock forever, Browse vs Free-account split.

## Rules that must survive any slide

Share is free. A shared smart site carries everything the sharer stored, at full fidelity, even if the recipient is on Free. What is gated is what the recipient can produce on their own account.

One unlock moment. Reaching for any paid thing, or the fourth chat on a property, opens one flow. Never a different wall per button.

Checkout stays on the map. Start Solo / Studio / Team opens payment in a popup. A full-page checkout that kills the map is retired.

Owner data is Studio, not Solo.

Tiers split on what the output is, not on volume of the same thing.

## Payments status (internal)

Live Stripe is deferred until the current QA list is cleared. Until then, sandbox only. Do not tell a prospect that card charges are live. Operator turns live mode on himself.

## Coverage (what to say)

Speak of coverage as nationwide United States. Do not read a county list as "all we have." The product answers coverage on the parcel: where a layer is not verified, the card says so. That is the safeguard. Do not offer to "confirm a jurisdiction on request." That puts a human in a product that has no sales team.

## Channel

The share loop is the channel. Affiliate is 20 percent, recurring, 12-month cap, when that rail is on. Identity and funnel stage may reach analytics. Research, saves, and report contents never leave the tenant.
