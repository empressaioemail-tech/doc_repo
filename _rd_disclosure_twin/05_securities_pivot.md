# Redirect: securities

**The ruling:** the target is the securities market - tokenized securities and the market infrastructure moving onchain - not RWA asset-twin services deal by deal. Docs 01/04's platform-channel logic survives but the product reframes.

**The securities framing (working):** disclosure for securities today is document-speed - EDGAR filings, quarterly cadence, data rooms and investor letters for private securities, nothing verified between filings. Tokenized trading makes the SECURITY machine-speed while its DISCLOSURE stays human-speed. The service: the issuer/security twin - a continuous, verified, machine-readable disclosure record keyed to the security. EDGAR at machine speed. Same doctrine: attest state, never value; honest absence; provenance on everything; metered machine access for the agents that will trade these markets.

**Why this frame is stronger than the RWA frame:** (1) it is market infrastructure, not a per-deal service - the DTCC/EDGAR-analog ambition; (2) private securities (the entire Reg D/A+/CF world, which is Securitize's actual business) have NO EDGAR at all - tokenized private securities trade on worse disclosure than public ones; greenfield; (3) the cockpit precedent aligns even better (security-master graph + reconcile are literally securities-shaped); (4) the portfolio-token thought (rd_de_09: "investor disclosure is the twin") generalizes to every tokenized issuer; (5) the regulatory tailwind is sharper - disclosure modernization for tokenized securities is a live SEC topic, and the comment-letter/standard-bearer play fits exactly.

**The fork - CALLED by Nick (2026-08-15): (c) PUBLICLY TRADED COMPANIES.** The objective: machine-readable formats for public companies' disclosure/data rooms, becoming, eventually, the standard of disclosure for public markets as trading tokenizes. Private-first instinct overruled. GTM reframe follows in this doc's companion discussion: the wedge is "investor relations for the agent age" plus the claim-your-twin motion (build twins of public companies from public disclosure first - the Texas-parcel playbook applied to tickers - and let companies claim, verify, and enrich them). XBRL honesty: structured financials are already mandated machine-readable; the twin is the layer above it - narrative, KPIs, between-filing state, provenance, attestation, agent-native serving - not a duplicate of it.
