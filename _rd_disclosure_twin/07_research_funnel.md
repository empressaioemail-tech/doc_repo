# The research funnel

## The structure (Nick, verbatim shape)

One research funnel per publicly traded company, wrapping everything into full-depth research an agent can pull in one place - with a human interface to the same funnel:

| Funnel layer | Content | Exists as |
|---|---|---|
| Company node | the issuer as identity anchor, everything we collect keyed to it | cockpit security-master graph (nodes/edges/identifier index) |
| Data room | all publicly available disclosure, provenance-carrying | Smart Files + the EDGAR adapter (doc 06) |
| People roster | leadership twins - officers and directors, verified from filings | actor-record atoms; fed by proxy statements (DEF 14A) and insider filings (Form 4) - public, provenanced, and insider-transaction data is people-data with receipts |
| Market structure | price chart, pattern formation, supply/demand zones, regime | cockpit: zones (calibrated zone atoms), signal engine (regime forecast, crossover), market-data plumbing, adaptive panel |
| Synthesis | the full-depth research read, cited across all layers | the funnel's composition layer; LLM writes interpretation ONLY over real data (adaptive-panel pattern: model narrates, never generates numbers) |

## Two doors (per the twin thesis two-door rule)

The agent door: MCP - search_issuers through full-funnel research pulls, provenance on everything, metered depth. The human door: a research interface over the same funnel - and Nick's UX insight: "it almost becomes like sports betting, essentially, but it's trading." Modern sportsbook UX is the best research-to-decision funnel grammar in consumer software (stats, props, live state, one screen); apply that grammar to company research. The cockpit's tile system is the nearest existing surface.

## The sports symmetry (ties to the ATX Bulls thread)

The same funnel structure pointed at sports: team node, athlete roster (A1-verified twins with receipts), game/market data, synthesis - full-depth research for sports outcomes. The Bulls program is already building the verified layer no sportsbook has. STANDING GUARDRAIL: we are the research/data layer, never the book - attest state not value becomes serve-research-not-odds-advice; gambling law is its own heavy surface (Sorare criminal-trial warning on the register); the posture if ever pursued is licensing verified data to licensed operators (doc 15 parked note, now with a structural home).

## Reading order with doc 06

Doc 06's thin mount (EDGAR adapter + gate, 3-5 issuers) is the first slice OF this funnel, not a competing plan: node + data-room layers first, people roster next (proxy/Form 4 adapter is bounded), market-structure layer via cockpit composition, synthesis last. The funnel is the vision; the mount is the door being built first.
