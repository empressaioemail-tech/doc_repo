# Capture plan: own the category

## The claim being staked

Nobody offers continuous, verified, machine-readable disclosure twins for tokenized assets. The evidence of the gap is in our own sourced research: RealT died at the asset-truth layer; the flagship deal trades at zero volume partly on trust; no tokenized venue precedent exists; the platforms are litigating patents over wrappers while nobody builds the underlying. First here is provable and defensible (time-moat asymmetry).

## The sequence

**A - Own the words (publication wave).** Publish the manifesto and positioning narrative with the Disclosure Twin named as the service. File trademarks (Hullihan; publication-bar question answered FIRST - the one narrow IP gate). Author the standard-bearer artifact: a public whitepaper / SEC comment letter on continuous disclosure for tokenized RWAs (precedent: platform CEOs position via comment letters; the author of the disclosure standard becomes its reference implementation).

**B - Build the exhibit.** One real asset, live twin: Smart Files room + attestation feed + honest-absence + metered gate access. Institutional cut of the demo video. Candidate assets in order of speed: a Texas CRE property we already twin deeply; the Bulls venue when siting starts (construction-phase twin = the Maldives-shaped comp); a cooperating issuer's live deal (best press, needs the channel first).

**C - Channel + anchor.** Platform conversations (list below), Texas-first warm-up, one PAID pilot issuer. The press moment: "the first tokenized asset you can actually see inside - live." Pricing shape: per-asset onboarding + monthly twin subscription + per-reference agent access (aligns with existing metering).

**D - Become the standard.** Publish the attestation-feed spec openly (operator-led standard play, same as the contract); industry-group participation; regulator visibility. The endgame is regulatory tailwind: when continuous disclosure for tokenized RWAs becomes required, the working standard wins by decree.
