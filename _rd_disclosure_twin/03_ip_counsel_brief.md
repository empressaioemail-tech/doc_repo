## Context counsel should have

1. The service concept (`01_service_concept.md`) and the running precedent (`02_cockpit_precedent.md`).
2. **The space is already litigating patents**: Securitize and tZERO - the two largest tokenization platforms - entered patent litigation against each other circa June 2026 over tokenization methods. IP position matters in this market now, both offensively and as freedom-to-operate.
3. Our own publication history: `@empressaio/atom-contract` is published on npm (open, versioned, dating to 2026 and earlier under a prior name), several SDK packages are public, the positioning narrative and manifesto are being prepared for publication, and the architecture has been discussed with prospective partners. Anything protectable needs the publication-bar clock assessed against what is already public and what is about to be.

## Trademark questions

1. Registrability and clearance: **Empressa** (and the "Empressa Solutions" entity-vs-brand question), **"We build digital economies"** (slogan), **"Tokens Need Twins"** (slogan/title), **Disclosure Twin** (service name candidate; likely descriptive - counsel's view on protectability vs picking a coined name), **Smart Files / Smart Athlete / Smart Site** family, **Hauska** (existing use).
2. Classes and priority given imminent publication of the manifesto and the service pitch.

## Patent-landscape questions (candidate subject matter, counsel to triage)

1. Continuous attestation-of-state for tokenized assets: the linkage-definition -> twin -> drift-detection -> tamper-evident feed method (the generalized reconcile mechanism).
2. The measurement-provenance ladder (measured-how tiers carried on every published value, with method-quality rendering and calibration attach-at-read).
3. Honest-absence as a machine artifact: verified absence with mandatory provenance scope, store-enforced.
4. The custody-gradient (per-key-class custody states with chained transitions and self-custodied consent keys).
5. Freedom-to-operate check against the Securitize/tZERO patent families before the platform-channel conversations go deep.
6. Counsel's frank view on patentability vs cost vs the publication bar - we hold no illusions that all of this is patentable; we want the triage.

## Trade-secret and open-boundary questions

1. What should deliberately remain trade secret rather than patented: calibration data and outcome ledgers, evidence-chain corpora, adapter know-how (the time-moat assets that cannot be reverse-engineered from the published contract).
2. The open-standard boundary: the atom contract is deliberately published (operator-led standard strategy). Counsel's guidance on keeping the standard open while protecting the operating machinery - what the license on the published packages should be going forward.
3. Employee/contractor IP assignment hygiene across Legacy Group ATX LLC / Hauska Inc. as a precondition for any of the above (ties to the entity-consolidation thread in `_rd_digital_economies/09`).

## What we want out of meeting one

A triage: what to file, what to keep secret, what is already lost to publication, what the freedom-to-operate risk is in the tokenization-platform channel, and clearance to publish the manifesto without foreclosing anything worth keeping.
