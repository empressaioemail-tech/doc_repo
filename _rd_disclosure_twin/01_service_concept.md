# The Disclosure Twin service

## The market moment (from this session's sourced research)

NYSE-Securitize MOU for tokenized securities; Computershare (world's largest transfer agent) partnered into tokenization; regulated onchain equity trading live since May 2026; tokenized RE past $10B; RWA $32B+ ex-stablecoins. The wrappers are going machine-speed; the disclosure about the underlying remains quarterly PDFs and trust. RealT's 2026 collapse was a failure at the asset-truth layer, not the blockchain layer. The gap is structural and growing with every issuance.

## Three tiers

1. **The twin behind the tokenized asset.** Issuer onboards the asset (building, fund, credit pool, construction project) to a twin: documents in provenance-carrying rooms, cash flows and obligations tracked, physical state where physical, encumbrances, continuously maintained. Token metadata carries the twin's DID; investors get a living disclosure surface. (The dossier-subscription concept, aimed at an industry actively creating demand.)
2. **The attestation feed.** Continuous machine-checkable verification that what the token claims matches what the twin shows: collateral present, milestone reached, obligation current. **We attest state, never value** - no opinions, no ratings, no NAV blessing (keeps us out of auditor and rating-agency regulatory regimes; counsel to formalize the line). Honest absence is a first-in-industry feature: "not verified, and here is where we looked" on an institutional disclosure surface.
3. **The machine-readable underlying for trading agents.** As tokenized trading goes agentic, agents need the underlying at machine speed: the metered, MCP-native gate with provenance on every answer. Same infrastructure, different customer on the key.

## The channel

Sell to the tokenization platforms, not issuer-by-issuer: Securitize, tZERO, Texture, RedSwan hold the issuers, carry the blast radius when an underlying goes bad, and own no referent layer. "Every asset on our venue carries a live verified twin" is platform differentiation that cannot be quickly copied (verification is bought with time, not money - the bottleneck asymmetry). Endgame: continuous-disclosure requirements for tokenized RWAs are a when, not an if; the working standard at that moment becomes infrastructure by decree.

## Built vs needed

**Built:** rooms (smart files, access passes, watermarks, audit chains); attestation atoms with provenance; obligation tracking; the metered gate; honest-absence discipline; deep physical-asset referent (RE and construction - where tokenization is most active; the venue twin IS this product for ground-up deals). **Needed:** asset-class adapters beyond real property (fund NAV inputs, private-credit loan tapes - bounded adapter builds on the existing pattern); subscription/feed productization; the counsel-drawn attestation boundary. Packaging-and-adapters gap, not an architecture gap.

## Sequencing (order only)

Start where the referent is deepest: tokenized real estate and construction-phase deals. Prove the twin on assets we verify better than anyone; follow the platforms' asset mix outward. The loop this closes: twin-behind-the-token was the session's first thesis (Blue transcript), became the Bulls venue plan, the AF1 RWA lane, our own portfolio-token disclosure, and now a standalone service for the tokenization industry - same product, four buyers, discovered in order of increasing size.
