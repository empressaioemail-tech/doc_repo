# Coverage research findings

## 1. The short answer

Five findings would change the product. Four are defects in the moat itself; one is a territory.

The no-hindsight invariant is false today, through two free parameters. Read the code-level half of this as reported rather than verified: it comes from subagents who state they read the files in session, the synthesiser did not re-open them, and section 7 records that limit for every code-level finding in this summary. The cockpit's FRED client sends no realtime_start or realtime_end, so every macro value held is the current revised value rather than the value knowable on its date. The SEC's XBRL Frames API returns, by documented design, the fact "that is last filed". Any claim graded against either was graded against information that did not exist at claim time. Both cheap fixes are parameters on credentials already held: ALFRED serves vintages on the same key, and companyfacts carries the filed date per fact. ALFRED is not the authority fix, because it archives what FRED published on a date rather than what the Board, the BLS or the BEA published on it, which reinstalls the redistributor at the layer where provenance is most load-bearing; the authority fix is the Philadelphia Fed's Real-Time Data Set, named in section 2.1, and the two are not the same claim. Until they land, the defensibility claim is false for the macro and fundamentals legs, because those legs are retro-fittable by anyone. The same defect recurs in tape revisions and in intraday ordering, where section 2.4 shows claims being resolved that should be unresolvable.

The security master looks like a live universe. That is an inference from two counts rather than a read of the master: roughly 9,984 instruments against 10,387 current tickers in the SEC's own file, where a master carrying dead tickers would sit far above the current-universe count rather than just below it. If the inference is wrong the survivorship finding shrinks considerably. Delisting certificates are free on EDGAR as Forms 25-NSE, 25 and 15, and unused. Worse, no free authority publishes historical symbol validity eras, so the master's history must be manufactured by diffing daily snapshots going forward. Every day not captured is permanently unrecoverable. That is a ratchet, and it is not the only one: any authority that overwrites its posting rather than archiving it ratchets the same way, and this report names several candidates without ever asking the question, including Rule 6c-11 daily ETF holdings, Rule 605 and 606 postings, the roughly 150 pipeline bulletin boards, the halt feed, closing-auction imbalance dissemination and the OCC series master. Which of them archive was not tested. Capturing the ones that do not costs a cron job rather than a research programme, which is why it belongs here rather than in a roadmap.

An absence verdict is an unversioned, ungraded, non-expiring claim about the world, and it is the one class of assertion the platform never registers, grades or supersedes. Absences go retrospectively false on schedules: a foreign private issuer's Section 16 exemption, correctly not-applicable for five decades, died on 2026-03-18, and died only partially. Three verdicts cannot say withheld, prohibited, statutorily suppressed, bounded by a reporting floor, or not yet due.

The claim substrate has one subject, no edges, and no events. Every family is a claim about one symbol, so relative, cross-sectional and portfolio claims are unrepresentable rather than unbuilt, as are ownership and supplier relationships, as is any claim about a dated event. That bounds the feature space L3 can ever learn, because those label shapes never existed while the record accrued, and it is what makes most of the free authority feeds in this report unusable.

The largest genuinely new territory is Regulation AB II asset-level loan tapes on EDGAR: free, monthly, loan-level credit books of listed companies, ahead of their own reporting.

## 2. The territory map

Organised by layer of the financial universe. Each section carries an authority table; the reasoning is in the prose. Gaps that merely widen coverage have been cut.

### 2.1 The macro, monetary and liquidity layer

The organising finding is not a missing series. It is that every macro value in both catalogs arrives through FRED, and FRED is a redistributor rather than a publisher of record. It re-publishes the Board's H.4.1, the New York Fed's SOFR, Treasury's TGA and the BLS's CPI, and it flattens each one on the way through. An architecture built on naming authorities is currently naming a redistributor for its entire macro leg. That single observation generates most of what follows.

The flattening is specific and costly. FRED's SOFR series is one scalar per day, the volume-weighted median. The administrator publishes the full distribution: the New York Fed's Markets API returned, for 2026-08-17, a median of 3.66 alongside the 1st, 25th, 75th and 99th percentiles, a volume figure, and a revision indicator. In funding stress the median barely moves while the tail blows out, because the 99th percentile is where the balance-sheet-constrained marginal borrower prints. Holding only the median makes the platform structurally blind to the earliest funding signal in the US system, and no amount of additional history fixes it, because the information was never retrieved. "The marginal borrower is paying 40bp over the median" is a claim family that costs one additional call and cannot be stated today. It also breaks the driver contract's shape: a distribution-valued driver where four percentiles and a volume are jointly meaningful does not decompose into five sibling series without destroying the thing that makes it informative.

The Treasury General Account is the same story with a frequency loss instead of a shape loss. The cockpit holds WTREGEN, the H.4.1 Wednesday-average level, weekly. The Bureau of the Fiscal Service publishes the Daily Treasury Statement with the TGA opening balance and every deposit and withdrawal itemised by agency, and it publishes it on api.fiscaldata.treasury.gov, the host already wired as the treasury_fiscaldata source for auction records. The highest-frequency liquidity dataset in the US sits on a credentialed, integrated host. The conclusion it unlocks inverts the sign of a trade: reserves draining because Treasury is rebuilding cash after a debt-ceiling resolution is a different world from reserves draining because the Fed is tightening, and weekly averaging destroys precisely the event you would act on.

Two further Fed gaps change what can be concluded rather than widening what can be plotted. First, the Standing Repo Facility has no FRED equivalent at all, and a non-zero take-up is the cleanest public evidence that repo has repriced through the Fed's ceiling. The same Markets API publishes every repo and reverse-repo operation with participating and accepted counterparty counts, submitted versus accepted amounts, and award rates by collateral type, which is what separates "one dealer is stuck" from "the system is stressed". Second, WALCL is a headline where the release is an accounting identity. H.4.1 sets factors supplying reserve funds, including a Loans block itemised by facility, against factors absorbing them, with reserve balances as the residual. Passive runoff, a swap-line repayment and a discount-window spike are three different worlds behind the same falling headline, and in March 2023 the Loans block was legible days before consensus. Representing it is a genuinely new object class: thirty values jointly constrained to sum, where the discrepancy is itself the signal. That shape recurs across the balance of payments, the national accounts and the flow of funds, which is the argument for building it once rather than special-casing it.

Two gaps here are unusual because the authority is already doing what the platform claims as its own moat. The Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia publishes the Survey of Professional Forecasters together with formal error statistics: a pre-registered forecast, a stated horizon, and a published graded record including the misses. That is an external, adversarial, unfakeable benchmark that neither party can retro-fit, and the platform currently cannot ask the question. A competitor who benchmarks against the SPF and publishes the comparison has a stronger honesty claim than one that only grades itself against itself. In the opposite direction, the Summary of Economic Projections is the most market-moving scheduled object the Fed produces and it has no machine-readable authority: statements, minutes and projection materials are PDF and HTML only, at every meeting. Every structured dot plot in commercial existence is a third party's extraction from a PDF. That is a differentiation opportunity rather than a coverage gap. Rendering the dot plot as an Observation extracted from a named PDF, with the absence of any machine-readable authority stated on the page, is the product working on the highest-visibility number in macro, and nobody else in the category will say it.

Two more, compressed. The non-centrally-cleared bilateral repo segment is the largest and most opaque part of the repo market; the OFR collects it under a statutory rule, publishes research from it, and does not expose it as a public series. Probing the obvious mnemonics returned a clean "Invalid mnemonic", and the discovery endpoints are authentication-gated, so this cannot be settled by probing. The correct verdict is neither absent-verified nor lookup-failed: the data demonstrably exists, held by a named authority, deliberately unpublished. Separately, the Board's quarterly Dodd-Frank section 1103 disclosure names every discount-window borrower, with amount, rate and collateral, on an approximately two-year lag. That is nearly useless for trading and extremely valuable for L3, because it is a retrospectively revealed ground truth: two years later you learn by name which institution was in trouble on a given day. It turns SRF take-up, the SOFR tail and the H.4.1 Loans block from an unsupervised pattern hunt into a supervised problem with unforgeable authority-published labels that only a party who captured the features contemporaneously can use.

On M2 the brief's worked example needs adjudicating, because it is half wrong and the correction is more interesting than the example. The cockpit does hold M2: econ_catalog.json carries 84 FRED series including M2SL, WALCL, RRPONTSYD, WTREGEN, WRESBAL, SOFR, NFCI and DFF in a fed_liquidity group. The twin's 33-driver catalog is a separate, instrument-scoped set containing none of the monetary universe. The real finding is a split brain: two catalogs holding disjoint macro universes, with the customer-facing one holding none of the monetary layer. Underneath that sits the harder problem, which is the case where more data makes the platform worse. US M2 includes retail money market funds and small time deposits. The euro area's nearest analogue is M3, which additionally includes repos, money fund shares and short debt securities and has no coverage-equivalent M2. Japan publishes M1, M2, M3 and broadly defined liquidity on definitions specific to its deposit and postal-savings system. Converting four incompatible constructs to dollars and charting them together produces the confident and wrong picture every macro commentary account already produces. The honest move, refusing the comparison and naming the reason, is unavailable today, because the contract cannot express that two values can share a layer, a provenance class, a unit and a named authority and still not be comparable. The same problem wears a second costume in policy rates: the BIS publishes comparable levels across roughly 38 jurisdictions, but a floor system, a corridor system and a tiered remuneration structure printing the same number are not the same machine, so pooling across them violates the platform's own no-pooling invariant applied to the world rather than to its own code.

### 2.2 The credit and fixed income layer

This territory's authority map is bimodal, and the split is the finding. Parts of it are among the richest free machine-readable datasets in finance and are under-used because they are hard to parse rather than hard to get. Other parts have no publisher of record at all, which is not a licensing problem but an absence of authority. A system that renders absence as a finding is unusually well placed in the second half and unusually under-supplied in the first.

The single highest-value item is Regulation AB II asset-level disclosure. Every SEC-registered auto loan, auto lease and CMBS securitisation files a monthly Form ABS-EE whose EX-102 exhibit is XML with one record per underlying asset: 72 standardised fields per auto loan, 152 per commercial mortgage. A live file pulled this session, Bridgecrest Lending Auto Securitization Trust 2023-1 filed 2026-04-15, ran to just under 100MB and carried per-loan obligor FICO, payment-to-income ratio, vehicle make and model, collateral value, income and employment verification level, obligor state and current payment status. In one quarter EDGAR carried 2,372 ABS-EE filings from 686 distinct trusts, including Carvana's captive servicer, Ally, GM Financial's AmeriCredit, CarMax and Santander Consumer. This is not securitisation coverage. It is the credit book of a listed equity, reported monthly, weeks before the quarter, and it lets an analyst compute FICO drift, payment-to-income drift and delinquency migration by vintage on a named issuer's actual originations ahead of the earnings call. The structural cost is real: the five layers have no slot for a population of assets whose statistics are the instrument, and the sponsor-to-trust join is not in EDGAR and has to be built.

The same mechanism carries commercial real estate. Every conduit CMBS files property address, property type, most recent net operating income, occupancy, DSCR, appraisal value and date, servicer watchlist status and special-servicing transfer, monthly, free. That is the only free property-by-property marked-to-appraisal record of US commercial real estate, and its equity payload runs three ways: office and retail REIT asset quality, an independent read on the marks regional banks carry in the same submarkets, and the appraisal-reduction events that precede REIT impairments. Representing it forces something the security master does not have, because a property is not an instrument and has no CUSIP, so it cannot be a node. Revolving assets were deliberately excluded from asset-level disclosure, so card trusts instead file a monthly Form 10-D with pool gross yield, net charge-off rate, delinquency buckets, monthly payment rate and excess spread; Amex, Capital One, Citi and Bank of America each filed on the 15th of April, May and June. Monthly payment rate is a consumer-stress leading indicator with no equity-side equivalent, and the monthly cadence lets a claim family be registered and graded twice per quarter rather than once, which is a sample-size argument as much as a coverage one.

Three no-authority findings here are worth more than most of the data. Non-agency RMBS has an asset-level rule that binds on nothing, because the market moved to Rule 144A private placement and Regulation AB II reaches only registered offerings; of the 686 ABS-EE filers in a quarter, zero carry a residential name. An asset class of roughly the systemic significance of the one that caused 2008 now reports to no machine-readable authority, and the SEC opened a concept release in 2025 that amounts to the regulator acknowledging the hole. US leveraged loans and the CLOs that own about two-thirds of them have no public transaction tape, no public holdings file and no free identifier; the only authority publication is the semiannual interagency Shared National Credit report, which covered 6,857 borrowers and 6.9 trillion dollars of commitments and published not one name. The jurisdictional asymmetry is the sharp part: the EU Securitisation Regulation Article 7 requires loan-level reporting to ESMA-registered repositories, so the same instrument type has an authority in Europe and none in the United States, and a single verdict per layer cannot say that.

Municipals are the best proving ground in finance for the absence doctrine, and the reason is statutory. The 1975 Tower Amendment bars the SEC and the MSRB from requiring municipal issuers to file anything, so every disclosure obligation is a contractual undertaking the issuer gives the underwriter, which the SEC reaches only by prohibiting the underwriter from buying the bonds without it. There is no federal filing deadline, no standard taxonomy and no comparable penalty regime, and the consequence is measurable: across 250,649 filed annual statements from 33,000 obligors the median lag from fiscal year end to posting improved from 318 days in 2012 to 188 days in 2018, and 188 is the improved number. A missing filing there is a fact about the issuer rather than a fact about the pipeline, which makes "this obligor's audit is 240 days past year end, its three-year median is 190, and 14 comparable obligors have filed" a genuine credit claim built entirely out of an absence and gradeable against subsequent rating actions and spread moves. That requires something the contract does not have: an expected-filing calendar, so an absence can be evaluated against an obligation rather than merely observed. Giving absence a due date is the most product-changing idea in this territory and it generalises far past munis. The content underneath is not trivial either, because for a large nonprofit hospital system, a private university or an airport authority the continuing disclosure on EMMA is the only audited financial statement that exists in public, and airport monthly enplanements arrive there far ahead of the FAA's own lag.

Four smaller items each change a conclusion. TRACE disseminates every corporate bond trade but caps the size field at 5 million par investment grade and 1 million high yield, publishing "5MM+" and "1MM+", so every volume, flow or trade-size statistic computed from it is right-truncated exactly where the information is, and the truncation is asymmetric between rating classes, which makes cross-rating comparisons of trade size structurally invalid. That is a censored measurement, and a derivation over a censored input that does not declare the censoring is dishonest by the platform's own standard. Credit rating action histories are free at the authority and almost nobody knows it: SEC Rule 17g-7(b) requires every NRSRO to publish its full rating history as an XBRL file on its own site, and ESMA operates CEREP for EU-registered agencies, which makes a rating action, the most naturally claim-shaped object in credit, into a ready-made gradeable family with a hard timestamp and an unambiguous outcome. The CDS public tape is free and transaction-level at DTCC's dissemination platform, carrying execution timestamps, cleared flags, platform, block election, spread and underlier, and the ISDA Determinations Committees publish credit-event determinations, which are the cleanest possible outcome labels for a credit claim. And covenants are the purest vocabulary gap in the report: indentures and credit agreements are filed in full text on EDGAR, access is not the constraint, and restricted-payments capacity is a computable legal ceiling on the two things equity holders are promised. The platform has no grammar for a basket, no lifecycle for incurred, used, restored, amended or waived, and no invalidation rule, so no covenant claim can be formed at any data volume. The honest limit is that notes issued 144A-for-life are never registered, so no indenture is ever filed and the covenant package for a large share of speculative-grade issuance is not on EDGAR at all.

Two items repair rather than extend. The platform holds Treasuries, which means yields, and holds neither the supply side nor the intermediation side: Treasury's refunding statements and the Borrowing Advisory Committee set issuance composition, and the New York Fed serves FR 2004 primary dealer positions, financing and fails as free JSON. A platform holding Treasuries with no supply data can watch a yield move and never say why, and the August 2023 refunding is the canonical case of an issuance-composition announcement rather than a Fed action driving a global duration selloff and an equity drawdown. Separately, bond identity has no free authority: CUSIP and the US ISIN are licensed commercial property, the free alternatives are FIGI for instruments and LEI for entities, and every credit signal in this section is only equity-load-bearing if the issuer of a bond, trust or municipal obligor can be joined to a listed parent. GLEIF Level 2 is the free authority for that join and the platform holds none of it.

### 2.3 The ownership, positioning and flows layer

Start with a correction, because building against the assumption would have produced a driver that silently serves stale data. Form N-PORT is not monthly-public. The 2024 amendments requiring monthly filing had their compliance dates extended to 17 November 2027 for fund groups above 10bn dollars and 18 May 2028 for the rest, and in February 2026 the SEC proposed scaling public disclosure back from monthly to quarterly at 60 days after quarter end. Public N-PORT today is quarterly, on roughly the same cadence as 13F. What makes it worth having is content, not frequency, and the content is considerably richer than anyone treats it as. A live filing parsed this session carried per-position payoff profile (long or short), fair-value level, an is-loaned flag, a securities-lending block with collateral flags, a named borrowers block giving each borrower's LEI and the aggregate value on loan (nine named counterparties in one filing), derivative blocks with counterparty name, LEI and notional, per-class monthly total return for all three months, and monthly sales, redemption and reinvestment dollars. Two things follow. Fund flows, which the industry buys from trade associations and vendors, are published per fund per month by the regulator in machine-readable XML, and a trade association estimate is not an authority record. And securities-lending supply is observable today, by named counterparty, while the designated authority for lending rates does not publish anything until 2029.

That timing point is general enough to need its own verdict. Two rules that would create the missing short-side authority have been serially delayed. Rule 13f-2 and Form SHO, adopted in 2023, now begin with a January 2028 reporting period and first filings due 14 February 2028, following a Fifth Circuit remand and a December 2025 exemptive order. Rule 10c-1a and FINRA SLATE have reporting pushed to 28 September 2028 and public dissemination to 29 March 2029; where secondary sources give earlier dates, FINRA's own page is authoritative and says 2028. These are records that are legally mandated, have a named publisher, and have a known future start date. A platform whose product is honest absence should be able to say "this will exist on 2028-02-14, published by the SEC, and does not exist now", and today it can only say absent. The same shape covers ESAP in July 2027, EU T+1 on 11 October 2027, and the amended Rule 605 corpus beginning 1 August 2026.

One absence in this layer is permanent by decision rather than by delay. Economic ownership assembled through cash-settled swaps is invisible: 13F captures only Section 13(f) securities, the 2023 beneficial-ownership amendments declined to deem cash-settled derivatives beneficial ownership, and on 12 June 2025 the SEC formally withdrew proposed Rule 10B-1, so any future rulemaking starts from a fresh proposal. Every ownership figure the platform ever serves is therefore incomplete by an unknown and unbounded amount, and it can now state exactly why and that the gap is permanent. The partial offset almost nobody names is that the transactions are public even though the positions are not: Regulation SBSR requires security-based swap dissemination through registered repositories, and ICE Trade Vault publishes a free public ticker.

The largest structural gap in this layer is that ownership is bipartite and the twin is instrument-major. Ownership is not a property of an instrument, it is an edge between a holder and an instrument at a date, and the twin's three shapes have no slot for the entities that do the holding: a 13F manager that is not a registered fund, or a natural person filing Forms 3, 4 and 5 across several issuers. The identity problem is real and authority-assigned, because 13F filers may file combination reports naming other included managers so that one manager's holdings appear under another's CIK. The platform already built exactly this machine for the other side, a bitemporal graph where names are aliases with validity eras, and the holder side has the identical problem and no machine. Without it, aggregation is not merely limited but wrong: summing across CIKs double-counts included managers and undercounts confidential positions.

Four items in this layer are cheap and change what can be claimed. Form 144 is the only pre-trade insider disclosure in the US regime, a notice of proposed sale filed before the affiliate sells, and it became mandatory on EDGAR only on 13 April 2023, which is why almost nobody holds a clean history. It has a graded outcome built into it: a Form 144 never followed by a Form 4 is an abandoned sale, which is a pre-registered hypothesis with an automatic resolution rule. Rule 10b5-1 plan data is what separates an insider trade from an insider signal, and since 2023 it exists in two places: a mandatory checkbox and plan adoption date on Forms 4 and 5, and Item 408 of Regulation S-K requiring quarterly disclosure of any director or officer adopting, modifying or terminating a trading arrangement, with name, date, duration and amount. Raw insider data is close to noise because most selling is pre-planned; the information is in the distance between adoption and trade, and a plan adopted and terminated before it ever traded is invisible in any Form 4 feed. Third, short interest is a twice-monthly position snapshot: positions are reported by 6 p.m. Eastern on the second business day after the designated settlement date and disseminated about a week later on FINRA's published schedule, per finra.org/filing-reporting/short-interest read this session, which cannot support a claim at any horizon shorter than a fortnight, while short sale volume is published daily; the honest complication is that FINRA covers only off-exchange volume and each exchange publishes its own file, so the consolidated daily series has no publisher of record and must be assembled, with a coverage denominator attached. That cadence is a rule rather than a fact of the world, and the rule is contested: FINRA filed SR-FINRA-2026-012 to adopt Rule 4321 and amend Rule 4560 expressly to increase the frequency and granularity of the short interest information it collects and disseminates, then withdrew the filing, which FINRA's own rule-filing page records. The cadence therefore stands with its own authority on record as having tried to change it and no scheduled date on which it will. Fourth, the SEC publishes semi-monthly fails-to-deliver together with an explicit interpretive caveat that fails are not evidence of naked short selling and that the age of the balance cannot be determined. That is an authority publishing both a number and the warning about how it must not be read, and carrying the caveat as part of the value rather than as small print is exactly the evidence discipline the platform claims.

Two closing items. Index membership and rebalance dates drive some of the largest predictable flows in equities and no regulator publishes constituents; the authority is a private licensor whose contracts prohibit redistribution, which is the clean counterexample to an architecture founded on naming authorities. The partial substitute is authority-mandated: SEC Rule 6c-11 requires every reliant ETF to post its full holdings each business day before open on its own site, so a rules-tracking fund's daily holdings are a mandated public proxy for the index it tracks, federated across issuers with no central repository. And the platform already holds Commitments of Traders for eleven roots out of a universe of 947 historical contract codes, but the sharper problem is vocabulary rather than breadth: the CFTC publishes four different trader taxonomies over the same open interest, and applying the legacy commercial and non-commercial split to a Treasury or equity-index contract labels a dealer's client-facing book as a hedger. That is a category error, not a rounding error, and it makes a true edge grade as a contradiction, which is the worst possible outcome for a learning loop.

### 2.4 Derivatives and market microstructure

The sharpest item in this territory is not a coverage gap, it is a validity gap in the record itself. A daily OHLCV bar does not encode intra-day path order, so any claim whose success condition is an ordering, touched the stop before the target, held the zone before breaking it, an R-multiple on a bracketed entry, is being resolved from a bar that cannot distinguish the two orderings. The platform's own invariant says a component that cannot compute an honest answer must refuse; the graders instead assert an ordering they never observed. Every such graded outcome is at best unverified, and because L2 is what L3 trains on, an unfalsifiable label class propagates into the model as noise the model cannot distinguish from signal. The audit needs no new data: identify which registered families emit ordering claims and re-tag their outcomes as unresolvable at the held resolution. The fix needs a required-resolution field on the claim, so a family declares the granularity at which it is falsifiable, and an honest-refusal path in the grader when the held series is coarser. Note that the market layer has no notion of series granularity at all, so a daily bar and a tick series are indistinguishable to a consumer. The free authority-published microstructure resource, incidentally, is SEC MIDAS, which publishes per-security trade-to-order ratios, quote lifetime distributions, cancel-to-trade, hidden and odd-lot rates from January 2012, built from all thirteen exchanges' proprietary feeds; the raw tape itself is fee-bearing under the CTA, UTP and OPRA plans, and there is no free authority tape.

The second item is identity. The Options Symbology Initiative gave the industry a 21-character descriptor, and OSI is a string format: it carries no validity era, no deliverable and no adjustment history. The registry and the adjustment record are separate OCC publications, and when OCC adjusts a contract after a split, merger or spin-off the adjusted series gets a new root and a non-standard deliverable, so 100 shares is no longer the multiplier and the equity ticker is no longer the root. The platform's entire options identity is a frontend string builder that uppercases the equity ticker and pads it to six characters, carrying a comment assuming options are near-dated. Every option claim keyed on that string then silently points at a different contract or at nothing. This is architectural, not a coverage problem: no volume of options data fixes an identity layer that mints its own keys, and the twin has no shape for an option series. The access terms could not be established, because both the OCC series-update endpoint and the contract-adjustment memo host returned 403 to automated fetch, so whether the series master and adjustment memos are programmatically retrievable is the first thing to check from a browser. If they are not, there is no second authority and the territory is reseller-only, which is itself the finding.

Third, the tape is revised. The CTA output specification defines a same-day cancel or error message and a separate prior-day trade cancel or error, and market centres must report within 90 seconds with slower reports designated late. The high, low, close and volume of a session are therefore not final at the close, and a bar re-pulled a week later can differ from the bar served that evening. If a grader re-reads bars at grade time it may resolve a claim against data that did not exist at claim time, and if a claim's frozen geometry was computed from a bar later corrected, the snapshot the whole design depends on was frozen around a value that has since moved. The platform is bitemporal for identity and not bitemporal at all for prices, which is the asymmetry that makes this easy to miss and expensive to discover late. The self-audit is cheap and publishable either way: re-pull the historical bars for every graded window and count how many outcomes flip.

Futures have the same disease in three places. A root is not an instrument; the contract month is. The daily settlement price is determined by a documented per-product procedure, a settlement-window VWAP or a bid-ask convention, not by the last trade, and settlement is what margin, profit and loss, and every option-on-future valuation key off, so a futures claim graded on a vendor close is graded against a number no counterparty ever paid. And a continuous series is a Derivation whose formula materially changes results, because back-adjustment injects a drift that corrupts return calculations while ratio adjustment does not, so two analysts on identical raw data reach different conclusions from different splicing choices. The platform cannot state its formula because it does not hold the contract months the formula operates on, which means today's futures bars are unclassifiable under its own provenance rules. Meanwhile the tick-size table is hand-seeded from a file carrying its own comment telling the operator to verify it against the broker before relying on it for live trading, while contract specifications are authority-published and free.

Options analytics have no declarable inputs. A Black-Scholes-family implied volatility requires a discount curve, a borrow rate and an expected dividend stream beyond the option price and the underlying. The platform holds none of the three, and the implied volatility currently surfaced is passed through from an upstream broker session and re-emitted with neither a formula nor an input, which makes it unclassifiable rather than merely under-populated. The sharper point is that this is not fixable by deriving in-house, because borrow has no publisher of record until SLATE in 2028 and forward dividends have no publisher of record at all. The honest architecture is therefore forced: for futures options, take CME's own published settlement delta and implied volatility as an authority value; for equity options, either carry a permanent stated absence on the borrow input or refuse the derivation. That in turn needs something the six classes cannot express, which is an input-level annotation marking one input of an otherwise valid derivation as unavailable or assumed.

Three more, each of which is a claim family rather than a series. Same-day expiry options now dominate US index options, running at roughly 63 to 65 percent of SPX volume in 2026 with a monthly average daily volume record above three million contracts, and a 0DTE contract has no daily bar, no overnight open interest and no multi-day life, so it is not a thin version of what the platform holds, it is a thing the data model cannot represent. Its flow is not incidental to the underlying, because same-day expiry concentrates dealer hedging into the last hours of the session, which is a first-order driver of the intraday path the platform grades zones against. Dealer gamma exposure is the clearest case in finance of a derivation sold as data: every commercial product computes it from open interest plus a sign assumption about who is on which side, and no authority publishes account-class open interest at the series level, so the load-bearing input is unobservable and the only holder of ground truth is the regulator via CAT. A platform that can say the formula is F, the critical input is assumed rather than observed, and we will not grade claims on it as though it were measured, is saying something no competitor will. And the official closing price is a single-price call auction outcome, not a continuous print: both Nasdaq and NYSE disseminate imbalance information from 3:50pm, Nasdaq puts closing activity at almost 16 percent of its volume, and index reconstitution flow lands entirely there, which means a class of end-of-day claims may be measuring a mechanical rebalance.

The cheapest thing in this whole territory is the SRO rule-filing corpus. Every mechanic in US market structure exists as a Form 19b-4 filing at the SEC with the operative rule text in Exhibit 5: auction algorithms, order types and priority, fee and rebate schedules, options price-improvement auction mechanics, extended-hours rules. It is complete, continuously updated, free and machine-fetchable. This is the answer to the brief's fourth question more directly than any data territory here: the platform's L1 covers zones, a chart phenomenon inferred from price, and has no doctrine for phenomena that are rules. Why a stock gaps at 9:30, why volume detonates at 15:59:59, why a fill lands between the quote, why a stock stops for five minutes, why a rebate changes where an order routes. These are not regularities to be discovered from folklore upward; they are documented mechanisms with a regulator's filing behind them, so they enter the evidence discipline at proven on day one, and no competitor's codex contains them because the category derives its vocabulary from charting tradition rather than from the rulebook. The adjacent free item is halts: the Nasdaq trade-halt feed returned 106 live records this session with millisecond halt times, reason codes and, where published, resumption quote and trade times. That gives an absence with a published end time, which is the strongest form of honest absence available anywhere, and it closes a hole in the grading loop, because an outcome graded across an interval when the instrument was not tradeable is not an outcome, and halts bias measured win rates upward on any claim with a downside trigger.

### 2.5 The non-US disclosure and market-structure layer

"Non-US anything" is on the known-thin list, so nothing here is offered as a headline. What follows are the specific mechanisms inside that headline which a US-shaped contract cannot represent, and which therefore turn into silent errors rather than honest absences.

Europe has no consolidated tape. In the US, Reg NMS SIPs make "the price" and "the volume" of an equity legally defined objects. In the EU and UK no consolidated tape provider is authorised yet: ESMA has selected Ediphy for bonds in July 2025, EuroCTP for shares and ETFs in December 2025 and Etrading Software for OTC derivatives in July 2026, with authorisation ongoing and no live date. Until then the only authority for a European instrument's trading data is each venue operator plus approved publication arrangements for off-venue prints, and ESMA's own research puts lit continuous exchange trading at roughly 43 percent of volumes. Every volume-derived indicator in the eventual codex carries an unstated assumption that the volume series is the instrument's volume; in the US that assumption is underwritten by law, and in Europe a single-venue bar sees a minority of real volume whose share moves with the auction and systematic-internaliser mix. A VWAP computed on one venue's prints is not the VWAP a European desk would recognise, and this needs deciding before the codex is written rather than retrofitted after the first non-US symbol lands.

Identity is licence-encumbered on exactly the legs a global master needs, and the free legs are already known. ISIN allocation runs through national numbering agencies under ANNA, whose lookup licence caps export at 1,000 active records per day and forbids onward distribution; the US numbering agency is CUSIP Global Services, so every US ISIN is a CUSIP with a country prefix. SEDOL is licensed. Free and open: LEI from GLEIF, FIGI via OpenFIGI with no stated limits, and the ISO 10383 MIC list from SWIFT. The decision is legal rather than technical and it is irreversible in practice because aliases accrete: choosing ISIN as the spine means the security master cannot be served or redistributed without a licence, and the constraint bites on the serving side, which is exactly where a twin with two doors lives. The right answer is FIGI for instruments, LEI for issuers and MIC for venues as the free spine, with ISIN, CUSIP and SEDOL held as licensed aliases carrying a redistribution flag, so the twin can resolve on them internally and refuse to emit them. That refusal is a distinct and honest answer no competitor gives, and the contract has no way to state it.

Three regimes break the absence model outright. Canada's SEDAR+ terms of use prohibit robots, automated searches, database construction, storing public information in databases, and mass or commercial distribution; bulk machine-readable access is a paid licence with no advertised price. A G7 market's entire disclosure record is behind a legal rather than a technical wall, and the wall is aimed precisely at database construction, so reporting Canada as lookup-failed forever is a false statement about the world. Russia is the reference case for lawful incompleteness: a 2022 decree gave issuers the right not to disclose at all, succeeded by decrees enumerating categories issuers may lawfully withhold, with the withholding instrument itself published. The authority exists, functions, publishes, and the record is incomplete on purpose in a known direction, so absent-verified is an affirmative falsehood. And in the EU, insider transactions under MAR Article 19 have no central database: the issuer is the publisher, national registers are scoped to their own issuers, Germany's retains only twelve months, and Article 19(8) sets a 5,000 euro annual floor below which nothing is notified at all. So "no insider trades" in Europe means "no trades above a statutory floor, in the subset of registers we can reach", which is a bounded absence and is not expressible today. Major-shareholding thresholds compound it, because the Transparency Directive rungs are extended and varied nationally, so the same 4 percent stake is a disclosed event in one jurisdiction and invisible in another.

Two jurisdictions prove that the authority row cannot be a scalar. Japan splits statutory disclosure from price-sensitive disclosure across two publishers: EDINET is the Financial Services Agency's statutory system with a free API and a registered key, and TDnet is the Tokyo Stock Exchange's timely-disclosure system carrying earnings summaries and guidance revisions, offered only through four paid commercial services. The exchange is both the authority and the vendor for the same record, which means a Japanese room can look populated on filings while missing every event that moves the price. In mainland China a disclosure is legally effective when published on any CSRC-designated venue, and the CSRC designates several: cninfo plus four newspapers of equal legal standing for some categories, with the exchanges publishing in parallel. So "not on cninfo" does not mean "not disclosed", and a completeness check against a single venue manufactures false absences in the world's second-largest equity market. Hong Kong inverts it: HKEXnews publishes to humans and every machine path located was a commercial scraper, which a platform built on naming authorities cannot honestly cite as a source of record.

Three findings show that upstream authorities change their own measurements, and that the platform's no-pooling discipline protects only against its own behaviour changes. On 13 July 2026 the UK repealed public disclosure of named individual net short positions at 0.5 percent and replaced it with an FCA-calculated anonymised aggregate built from reports at 0.2 percent, published daily at noon on a two-day lag, with the pre-change named register archived. One conceptual series changed granularity, identifiability and threshold on a single known date, and a naive ingest splices the two and produces a graded record whose labels mean different things either side of it. On 12 April 2024 HKEX, with the Shanghai and Shenzhen exchanges, stopped publishing Northbound real-time turnover, masked the quota balance above 30 percent and the short-selling balance above 300,000 shares, and moved Northbound shareholding to quarterly, deliberately reducing the resolution of the flagship foreign-flow indicator in Asia. That is not a data-quality defect anyone can fix; it is the world changing, and the platform has nowhere to record it. And Japan's Tokyo Stock Exchange required simultaneous English disclosure for Prime Market issuers from 1 April 2025 while explicitly accepting an excerpt or summary, so a translated filing served as a Record is the strongest available provenance for a document the rules permit to omit material, and the same instrument's English history has two regimes.

Two closing items with practical bite. Europe's corporate reporting has roughly thirty national officially appointed mechanisms today, several operated by private companies under national appointment, filing in inline XBRL against an issuer-extensible IFRS taxonomy where extension elements are anchored rather than fixed, so a naive mapping produces confidently wrong line items rather than honest absences; ESMA began collecting for ESAP on 10 July 2026 with the public platform opening in July 2027, which makes it the first live case of an authority row that changes on a known date. And in several Asian markets a share's price and availability depend on the buyer's nationality: India's depositories publish daily foreign-investment headroom per company with a caution list at 3 percent remaining and forced divestment on breach, and Thailand runs separate local and foreign boards for the same share line at different prices. That is worse than a coverage gap, because the twin would serve a technically correct number that the reader cannot act on, with no field in which to say so.

### 2.6 Physical commodities, power, administered markets, crypto and private markets

Three findings dominate this layer, and the first is a defect inside territory the platform already holds. The EIA publishes US petroleum stocks twice, by two different methods, and says so: the Weekly Petroleum Status Report is a sample drawn from the respondents to the monthly surveys, while the Petroleum Supply Monthly is the census, and the agency states plainly that it considers the monthly data definitive and that the weekly figures are not generally revised because they are a snapshot. Typical divergence is within about two percent. The market trades the weekly; the truth is the monthly; they are never reconciled. A single EIA crude stocks driver with no estimator identity is exactly the fabricated precision the platform's invariants exist to prevent, and this is not a vintage problem, because the two values are different measurements rather than different versions of one. It also hands the platform a rare self-resolving experiment: pre-register on the weekly, grade against the census that arrives on a known lag.

Second, in physical commodities the settlement reference is frequently a private editorial judgment that legally settles a Record-class contract. Dated Brent is the archetype: large parts of physical energy and metals settle against a price assessed by a price reporting agency under a published methodology, governed by the IOSCO principles for oil price reporting agencies and, in the EU, by the Benchmarks Regulation, under which ESMA maintains a public register of authorised administrators and benchmarks that a supervised entity must consult before referencing a commodity benchmark. The price itself has no public authority publisher and is reseller-only; what is authority-published is the permission structure around it. This inverts the provenance ordering, because a class ranked fifth of six is the definitive value for trillions of dollars of Record-class contracts, and any commodity coverage that does not confront it will either mis-class the assessment as Record, which is a lie, or under-rank it, which is useless.

Third, several of the largest commodity markets have supply set by decision rather than by price, on a published calendar, by a named institution, with legislated floors. OPEC member states set production by ministerial decision; the US Department of Energy releases and refills the strategic reserve by policy; EU ETS supply is set by the cap trajectory and the market stability reserve with installation-level verified emissions published through the Union registry; and the California and Quebec joint auctions run with a statutory price floor, with the November 2024 auction settling at 31.91 dollars against a 24.04 floor. A detector that does not know about a legislated floor will find support at 24.04 and call it a zone, which means the platform would be measuring an act of legislation and labelling it technical structure. That is a vocabulary gap first: it requires a doctrine entry for an administratively determined level that is explicitly not a technical level, and an evidence tag separating a statutory floor, whose mechanism is known and proven, from a discovered one.

Four more items in this layer are worth naming because each unlocks a conclusion rather than a chart. Deliverable stock is the physical constraint that shapes a curve, and it is a legal state rather than a level: the LME publishes daily total, on-warrant and cancelled-warrant stocks per metal, location and country, and since 1 April 2025 also daily off-warrant stocks, while CME publishes free daily depository stocks and delivery notice reports naming which clearing firms issued and stopped delivery. Backwardation alone is ambiguous; backwardation plus falling on-warrant stock plus a spike in cancellations plus a concentrated issues-and-stops pattern is a squeeze, observable days before the price completes. Contract terms are a versioned object and the version is authority-published: under CFTC Part 40 a designated contract market must file a self-certification including the product's full terms and conditions before listing or changing a rule, so deliverable grade, par and discount schedules, delivery points and settlement procedure all change on filed dates. The platform's security master is bitemporal over symbols, which models a ticker change and does not model a terms change, and a continuous series spanning a deliverable-grade amendment is two different commodities wearing one symbol. Authority release calendars with physical lockups are the cheapest source of clean claim and outcome pairs in existence: WASDE is released at noon Eastern on a published date with the lockup area's doors secured and communications blocked until release, which is the only situation in finance where the publisher itself certifies that nobody knew the answer until an exact instant, and the platform has no representation of a scheduled event at all. And power is the one major commodity that cannot be stored, whose price is location-specific at thousands of nodes simultaneously and whose scarcity price is set by an administrative adder; ERCOT publishes locational marginal prices by bus, node, hub and load zone through a free registered API with indicative prices every five minutes, and a nodal price is not a quote but a field over geography that decomposes by construction into energy, congestion and loss.

Two authority-shape findings recur across this layer and elsewhere. FERC requires each interstate gas pipeline to post operational capacity, scheduled quantity, available capacity and point-level flows on its own site, with the vocabulary standardised by NAESB, and FERC aggregates none of it: roughly 150 pipelines each publish to their own site, and the only consolidated views are commercial scrapes. The regulator compels the emission of data and publishes none of it, which means the honest answer to "name the authority" is a mandating regulator plus a set of obligated private publishers. The same shape governs stablecoin reserves under the GENIUS Act, where a permitted issuer must publish monthly reserve composition including average tenor and the geographic location of custody, examined by a registered accounting firm and certified by named officers to the regulator, with no consolidated authority dataset proposed. Both need an authority row that is a set with per-member coverage, and stablecoin reserves additionally need a provenance class for a determination examined by a third party against a standard, which is the same class the price reporting agency assessment needs.

Two closing items. In crypto the record has no publisher: chain state is produced by a consensus process, observed by whoever runs a node, and its validity is probabilistic and revisable, with block height rather than wall-clock time as the authoritative index. Classing that as Record asserts an authority that does not exist; classing it as Observation discards that the record is cryptographically self-verifying, which is a stronger guarantee than most Record-class sources offer. What did change in 2026 is that the EU produced the first authority-published crypto disclosure record: ESMA maintains registers of authorised service providers, of crypto-asset white papers, and of non-compliant entities, with the Article 143(3) transitional periods running to an outer boundary of 1 July 2026, or until an entity is granted or refused authorisation, whichever is sooner, and with member states free to close earlier, which several did: Ireland set 29 December 2025. That is ESMA's own MiCA page and its published list of grandfathering periods, plus the Central Bank of Ireland's MiCAR page, all read this session, and it corrects an earlier draft of this report that gave one EU-wide conclusion date. The non-compliance register is an authority publishing a negative finding, which is the rarest shape of disclosure and a direct match to the thesis. And in private markets, the claim that no authority exists is false in a way worth correcting: the SEC publishes Form D data sets quarterly as structured tables, Form ADV Schedule D 7.B.1 carries a row per private fund with gross asset value, auditor, prime broker, custodian and administrator, and quarterly Private Fund Statistics aggregate Form PF, whose per-filer filings are confidential by statute and always will be. The highest-value private-markets item is one no reseller sells: the same private borrower is held by several business development companies, each of which independently marks it every quarter under Level 3 with a disclosed valuation technique in a filed schedule of investments, so the spread between three marks on one name on one date is an observable measure of valuation uncertainty. That is measuring what the market does not know, computed from authority-filed data, on the asset class where opacity is the complaint.

### 2.7 The entity, event and corporate-action layer

This is where the security master lives, and where the cheapest repairs are.

Corporate actions have no machine-readable publisher of record, and that is the finding rather than the data. Four partial authorities exist and they disagree. DTC is the operational authority for US book-entry securities and publishes through a subscription ISO 20022 service across roughly 1.3 million active issues with no open tier. The listing exchange is the authority for listing-level actions. FINRA's OTC Daily List is the only free machine-readable corporate-action feed, with archives back to 2014, and it covers OTC-quoted securities only, so there is no free NMS-wide equivalent. And the issuer is the authority of first instance via 8-K. This matters more than a coverage gap because a corporate action retroactively rewrites the price series a claim was graded against: if a split or spin-off lands and the bar history is re-adjusted, every outcome computed on the pre-adjustment series is wrong, silently, with no error raised, and an append-only ledger will faithfully preserve the corrupted grade. It is the one class of event that can corrupt an append-only record without touching it. For depositary receipts the record of origin is a commercial bank rather than a company or a regulator: an ADR ratio change is announced by the depositary under the deposit agreement, which matters because ADRs are the most likely first non-US instruments, being US-listed and US-settled and already in the master's shape, and a ratio change is effectively an unannounced split whose record lives in a PDF on a bank's website.

The delisting and survivorship repair is free and nobody uses it. Form 25-NSE is filed by the exchange to notify the SEC of a delisting, Form 25 is the issuer-filed counterpart, and Form 15 is the subsequent deregistration; delisting is effective ten days after filing and deregistration ninety. These are the authoritative dateable death certificates for US listed securities, queryable through the same EDGAR APIs already in use. Bankruptcies, going-private deals and compliance delistings are precisely the outcomes a trading platform most needs in its record and exactly the names a live-universe master cannot see, so every aggregate performance figure the platform publishes is biased upward by construction until this closes. Underneath it sits the harder half: no free authority publishes historical ticker validity eras. The SEC's ticker file is a flat current-state map and Nasdaq's directory is a daily snapshot, so eras must be manufactured by diffing daily captures going forward. The one genuine exception found is valuable and narrow: the EDGAR submissions API returns former entity names with real from and to dates, which are entity name eras rather than ticker eras. A ticker recycled to a different issuer after a delisting silently welds two companies' histories into one node, and the corruption is invisible in the record.

Two free structured event surfaces are sitting unused inside filings the platform already ingests. The EDGAR submissions API exposes 8-K item codes as a structured field, so Item 4.02, non-reliance on previously issued financial statements, is queryable structurally rather than by text mining. An Item 4.02 filing invalidates every claim conditioned on the withdrawn numbers, and without it those claims stay in the record as if they were sound, which is a grading-integrity failure rather than a coverage gap. The same field opens a whole free event calendar nobody has named: bankruptcy or receivership, costs associated with exit or disposal, auditor change or dismissal, officer departure, shareholder vote results. Adjacent and equally unused is Form 12b-25, the late-filing notification, which is a dateable distress signal filed before the bad news. Also here: the SEC's XBRL Frames API returns the last-filed value by documented design, so the obvious fundamentals path is hindsight-contaminated at the source, while companyconcept and companyfacts carry the accession, filed date and form per fact, and DERA's Financial Statement Data Sets are published explicitly as filed and unchanged from 2009 onward.

Two absences here are structural and permanent, and both are worth stating on the page. There is no publisher of record for earnings transcripts. Regulation FD guidance recommends but does not require an issuer to post a replay or transcript; the earnings press release is typically furnished under 8-K Item 2.02, which places it outside Section 18 liability and excludes it from incorporation by reference unless the issuer opts in; and the unscripted question-and-answer session, which is the highest-information part of the event, exists only as a webcast the issuer hosts. So for the most-used qualitative input in equity research the top rung of the provenance ladder is permanently unreachable: any transcript the platform holds is at best an Observation, a vendor's transcription of a broadcast, never a Record. Every competitor serves transcripts as if they were authoritative text, and a contract that forces the honest label is making a true statement no competitor's schema can express. Similarly, no regulator, exchange or self-regulatory organisation publishes a forward calendar of earnings dates. The commercial substitute is a vendor and not an authority, however the market treats it, and the vendor's own documentation states the critical fact plainly: a date is either confirmed by the company or unconfirmed, in which case it is forecast from historical reporting trends by an analyst. The sweep that reported this did not name which vendor, which leaves the claim unauditable until it does. One field, two provenance classes, Record and Judgment, with no schema-level separation downstream. A claim-registration system is a scheduling system, and being wrong about the date does not produce a failed claim, it produces a claim graded over the wrong window that then enters the record as evidence.

The sharpest instance of a regulator that is forbidden from publishing is the FDA. Under 21 CFR 314.430 the agency generally may not confirm that a marketing application even exists until the sponsor makes it public, so there is no forward calendar of pending action dates; the dates reach the market only through the sponsor's own press release or 8-K. Meanwhile the retrospective record is completely open and machine-readable: openFDA returned live counts this session for approvals, drug enforcement recalls and device adverse events, all keyless, and ClinicalTrials.gov v2 is likewise keyless. The asymmetry is exact. Everything that already happened is free and authoritative; the one thing with predictive value is unpublishable by law. Biotech single names move 40 to 80 percent on a binary decision, so a platform that claims to know an instrument's drivers and is silent on the one event that determines its value is making a false completeness claim, and the honest absence here has a citable statutory cause, which is the strongest form available.

Three more, compressed, each of which changes what the drivers layer can be. The curated driver catalog is macro-only, which means the twin can say what moves the index and not what moves this specific company; a parallel class of federal authority publishes instrument-specific events with hard timestamps, free and keyless, and none of it is named. NHTSA returned 23 recall campaigns for a single vehicle model with campaign numbers, report dates, components and park-outside severity flags; EPA's ECHO services returned live facility-level enforcement data. These are dateable, attributable, repriceable events from the regulator itself, and they are unusable without the corporate hierarchy, because the recall is filed by a subsidiary and the instrument is the parent, which is why GLEIF Level 2 is a precondition rather than an adjacent nicety. Second, the SEC runs one of the best public data APIs in government and publishes its own enforcement record as web pages: litigation releases, administrative proceedings, trading suspensions and accounting and auditing enforcement releases are HTML index pages with no JSON endpoint and no bulk file. An accounting enforcement release is the ex-post confirmation that reported fundamentals were false, which retroactively voids every fundamentals-conditioned claim on that name, and a trading suspension means the market layer for that instrument has no valid content at all. Third, WARN notices are a labour-market leading indicator with an explicit non-authority: the Department of Labor states that it neither maintains a database of WARN notices nor requires employers to provide them to the Department, so the publishers are roughly fifty state units publishing voluntarily and inconsistently. A WARN notice typically precedes the exit-cost 8-K by weeks and the headcount disclosure by months, and the acquisition topology, fifty heterogeneous public-record sources normalised into one contract with per-jurisdiction coverage stated, is the same shape as work this organisation has already done at county level.

One live regulatory correction belongs here because getting it wrong produces a confidently false absence. US mandatory climate disclosure has moved off EDGAR. The SEC proposed rescission of its climate rules in their entirety on 29 May 2026, following the 2024 stay, the 2025 decision to end its defence and the Eighth Circuit holding the petitions in abeyance. California became the operative US authority, and reports go to a CARB public docket rather than to EDGAR. The first-year Scope 1 and 2 deadline is exactly the kind of claim this report should not print at second hand: two dates circulate in the secondary sources, 10 August 2026 as adopted and 10 November 2026 as a later change, and every CARB path tried in this revision returned 404, so the deadline is recorded as unresolved pending a primary read rather than stated. If the room looks at EDGAR, finds nothing and returns absent-verified, that is a false absence, and it is the first concrete proof that the disclosure regime for US issuers is not coextensive with EDGAR, which quietly undermines the assumption the room layer is built on.

## 3. Vocabulary gaps

These are the phenomena the platform could never form a claim about, grade, or learn from, no matter how much data arrived. They divide into three questions: what a claim can be about, what a claim can be about in the world, and what makes a claim trustworthy.

### What a claim can be about

A claim has exactly one subject. The atom carries a single entity type and entity id, a single claim key and a single claim value, and every family in the registry is a claim about one symbol at one timeframe. The two things that look relational are not: the propagation family pairs a fast and a slow timeframe of the same instrument, and the composition family pairs a zone with a regime of the same instrument. So the platform can say "this instrument will do X" and cannot say A outperforms B, this sector leads that one, the spread converges, breadth is deteriorating while the index holds, or anything at all about a book. That excludes most of what a professional trades, and it fixes the feature space permanently, because a model trained on this record learns single-instrument absolute-direction structure and can never learn relative structure, since no label of that shape was ever written. A benchmark-subtracted return does not fix it: subtracting the same symbol's own benchmark makes an absolute claim honest, it does not create a claim whose subject is a pair. A relative claim needs a composite subject with its own stable identity, its own validity era because constituents change, and its own corporate-action semantics. The favourable point is that the machinery is already the right shape, since the security master is a graph where a node is a stable join key and symbols are aliases with validity eras, and a composite is a node whose aliases are a constituent set. Nothing mints one.

There is no edge. Ownership, supplier and customer, competitor, merger counterparty and exchange ratio, index membership, sponsor to trust, adviser to fund, CDS reference entity, named lending counterparty: all of these are relationships between two subjects, and all five layers are facets of one addressed node. This is the structural blocker under a large fraction of everything in section 2, because a recall is filed by a subsidiary, a contract is awarded to a division, an enforcement action names a limited liability company, and none of it attaches to the tradeable instrument without a hierarchy. It has an L1 consequence as well: with no edge vocabulary the platform can never form a claim of the form "A's guidance cut propagates to B", which is the most common real analyst inference about a company, even though it already runs propagation families at the price level. Note that the contract already anticipates exactly one edge, the fund adviser relationship deferred to v0.2, and flags that building it will exercise the edge model in a way nothing else does. That is one narrow case about to define a general facility by accident, and it should be ruled on before it ships.

There is no event grammar. The entire claim grammar is level-shaped, inherited from zones: touch the proximal, react away, hold or break the distal, resolve to a target within a horizon. An event claim has a different skeleton, an announcement instant, an estimation window, an expected-return model, an event window, and an abnormal return measured against that model. The only causal machinery that exists is rule replay, which is a fine intervention grader and is not event-study doctrine. Wing A is indicators and Wing B is patterns; neither has a slot for it. This is why the free authority feeds in this report are unusable rather than merely unacquired, since they are overwhelmingly event-shaped: 8-K item codes, Forms 4 and 144, halts and limit states, auction and settlement events, WASDE and FOMC releases, recalls and enforcement. Point-in-time catalyst capture is necessary and not sufficient; with a perfect earnings feed the platform still could not register a single claim about an earnings event, because there is no grammar of that shape.

There is no forward calendar object. All five layers describe what is or was. Nothing represents a dated future event with its own provenance and confirmation state, which is what a pre-registration system fundamentally needs. The same slot would later hold action dates, index rebalances, lock-up expiries, contract expiries, quarterly refunding, WASDE and FOMC. Building it makes time a first-class citizen of the contract, and it is what turns a lockup-protected release into an externally certified pre-registration boundary.

And there is no non-instrument subject. A sector, an index, a currency bloc, a repo segment and a fund complex are not nodes in the master, and the union layer is CI-gated against minting one. So "money funds moved 50 billion dollars out of repo into bills", "median CPI did not confirm the headline" and "off-exchange share in this sector is 62 percent" are statements with no addressable subject. That is a hard blocker rather than a schema gap, and it caps L1's vocabulary at the instrument permanently. It should be decided explicitly rather than inherited from a CI gate written for a different reason.

### What a claim can be about in the world

The word regime is in the substrate and the concept is not. Every regime the platform can condition on is a function of the price it is conditioning: one family defines regime as the sign of a fast-minus-slow moving-average gap, another from directional-movement indicators, and the flagship composition family stamps zone and regime pairings, which conditions a price-derived claim on a second statistic of the same series at a different lookback. That is close to conditioning a claim on itself. The platform does hold an exogenous state label, a growth and inflation quadrant computed from industrial production, retail sales, payrolls and core CPI, and it is display-only, never stamped on an atom, with no version identity and no claim grammar, and it is read at the current vintage. The recession spans drawn beside it come from a series whose values are back-assigned to months the dating committee labelled long afterward, so as chart shading that is fine and as a conditioning variable it is a direct hindsight violation. The favourable fact is that a free, authority-published, deeply backfillable exogenous state vocabulary exists: Cboe publishes VIX and SKEW from 1990, VVIX from 2006, the VIX term structure, implied correlation from 2006 and dispersion from 2014, all as anonymous CSV. Because they backfill, adding them late costs nothing in history, which is rare on this list.

Beyond that, five phenomena have no doctrine at all, and each was reached independently from a different territory. An administratively determined level, a statutory price floor or a facility rate, is not a technical level, and a detector without the distinction will report legislation as structure. Physical tightness, the warrant state machine and the deliverable specification, has no claim form, so a curve move caused by scarcity cannot be distinguished from one caused by roll flow. A covenant has no grammar, no lifecycle and no invalidation rule, so the computable legal ceiling on buybacks and dividends cannot be asserted. A censored measurement, a capped TRACE size or a block-delayed swap notional, has no representation, so a derivation over it cannot declare its own truncation. And definitional equivalence has no representation, so two values sharing a layer, a class, a unit and an authority can be incomparable with nothing to say so. Add a general one: there is no doctrine for knowledge that arrives later about an earlier date, which covers revisions, restatements, tape corrections, delayed statutory disclosure and the 13F lag, and which is the vintage problem approached from the other side.

Finally, a conditional claim is ungradeable as specified. Nothing in the atom or the experiment definition distinguishes a conditional from an unconditional claim, so a claim of the form "if the Fed cuts, then X" resolves only on the subset where the antecedent occurred, and that subset is selected by the market rather than by the pre-registration. A conditional with a rare antecedent is unfalsifiable within any horizon and can be surfaced indefinitely, and one that never fires is indistinguishable in the record from one that fired and was right. This matters most for calibration, because calibration is a comparison and there is currently nothing on the other side of it. The other side exists and is free: the Federal Reserve Bank of Minneapolis publishes market-based probability densities as plain CSV with mean, standard deviation, skew, kurtosis, decile points and explicit probabilities of a large decline or increase, per asset, weekly, covering macro assets and individual listed names; the New York Fed publishes averaged probability distributions over policy outcomes from its market expectations survey. Those are dated third-party probabilistic claims about the future, which is precisely the object the grading machinery is built to score, made by someone else.

### What makes a claim trustworthy

Four vocabulary gaps sit in the grading loop itself, and they are the ones most likely to change the product rather than widen it.

The registry mechanises optional stopping. Every experiment definition carries a next-review trigger with values like "ten new independent market days", which is a rule for re-running a fixed-threshold test each time data arrives and stopping when it passes. Under repeated testing at a fixed threshold the probability of eventually crossing goes to one even when the null is true, so the platform has built a machine that reliably manufactures a passing result on any family kept alive long enough, inside the loop that is supposed to be the moat. False-discovery control is implemented in exactly one place, within one family's bucket set, as a single-look correction, and the second time it runs on a grown sample it is invalidated. Counting registrations in a ledger is a denominator, not a correction, and it does not count looks at all. The right doctrine is anytime-valid inference, e-values and confidence sequences, valid under arbitrary optional stopping and composing across families by multiplication. That single object also serves continuous decay monitoring and cross-family control, which is why it is worth writing once and properly.

The no-pooling invariant corrects clustering in time and nothing corrects clustering across instruments. Twenty symbols firing the same setup on the same day is one market event, and capping per-symbol and per-day concentration limits concentration without correcting for correlation: twenty evenly distributed, highly correlated symbols pass every concentration condition and still contribute roughly one independent observation. Reported sample size is therefore inflated by an unknown factor that varies with the market's correlation state, so a lower bound computed on it is not a lower bound. The conditioning variable is the free implied-correlation history above. The operational consequence is worse than the statistical one: a platform that grades twenty families independently and sizes on their individual records holds one position twenty times in exactly the states where its claims collapse into a single claim, and no amount of single-instrument grading can surface that.

The outcome unit is a modelled quantity presented as an observation. The grading primitive returns a hardcoded minus one on a stop touch regardless of the gap, so a bar that gapped through the stop grades at minus one when the realisable fill was worse: a fabricated value in the most load-bearing function in the grading path, biased one way, in the tail where a trading record's truth lives. Ambiguous resolutions, where a bar touches both stop and target, default conservatively and are not recorded as ambiguous, so the platform cannot state what share of its record rests on an intrabar ordering it never observed. Maximum favourable excursion is tracked and maximum adverse excursion is not, so the record can say how far a claim ran and not how much heat it took, which is the half that determines whether a real position survived to collect it. And no cost touches the graded record at all, while the universe is built with no liquidity floor, so a family can be true and unexploitable with nothing to distinguish the two. Capacity is a property of the individual claim rather than a filter on the universe, because the same claim is true at ten thousand dollars and false at ten million, and nothing expresses the size at which it stops being true.

Two closing gaps. L1 asserts mechanisms and L2 measures rates, and there is no arrow between them: an entry's evidence status is categorical, so it makes no numerical commitment, so when its family grades at 48 percent the entry cannot be wrong by any stated amount and can only be relabelled. Meanwhile L2 already speaks in Beta-Binomial posteriors, so the two halves of the system speak different languages. Giving each entry a prior with an explicit strength in pseudo-counts makes it falsifiable at registration rather than at relabelling, gives calibration a stated expectation to be measured against, and gives L1 the feedback path it currently lacks, which is the only way the doctrine layer participates in the fourth capability at all. Separately, there is no vocabulary for the platform's own effect on what it measures. Published predictors decay by about 26 percent out of sample and 58 percent after publication, with roughly 32 points attributable to publication itself, so a product whose evidence status is customer-visible is a decay mechanism aimed at its own record. Under its own rules a disclosure should fork a version, since a behaviour change forks a version and populations never merge, but disclosure is not in the trigger set, so pre-disclosure and post-disclosure outcomes pool silently, which is exactly the failure the invariant exists to prevent on the one axis it does not watch. The asymmetry worth acting on is that this platform is one of very few that can measure its own publication effect cleanly, because it knows the date and audience of every disclosure and holds the pre-disclosure record.

## 4. What the contract cannot represent

Where a new layer or a new provenance class is needed rather than a new source. Four agents independently proposed a seventh provenance class and each proposed a different one: an attested determination for price reporting agency assessments, a licensed record for index constituents, a modelled estimate by an authority for term premia, and a foreign claim for third-party forecasts. Adjudicating between them is the wrong move, because they are not competing candidates for one slot. They are coordinates on different axes.

Read the six classes as answers to a question and they answer different questions. Record is who produced it, an authority. Observation and Derivation are how it was produced, measured or computed. Attention is what it is about, marks by a cohort. Judgment is who plus epistemic standing. Synthesis is what kind of artefact it is. Those are at least three orthogonal axes wearing one enum, which is why every territory reached for a seventh and why none of the sevenths would have fixed anything. The proof is inside the codebase rather than in theory: a driver test asserts that each row's provenance is either verified-live or carried-from-code, which is a self-audit axis called provenance coexisting with the six classes, and the spine separately carries a freshness enum and a confidence-basis enum distinguishing asserted from backtest and live, with a docstring warning against dressing an asserted number as an earned one. So the platform already reasons in four axes and the contract flattens them into one ordinal per layer. The fix is a tuple: producer (authority, issuer, private licensor, us, a third party), production mode (recorded, measured, computed, judged, narrated), our verification state (verified-live, carried-from-code), and evidence basis (asserted, backtest, live). Every proposed seventh class becomes a tuple coordinate, and the enum stops growing once per territory.

The ordering itself should go with it. As a total order it is wrong in both directions. A derivation over filed records with every input stated ranks below an observation that may be one venue's tick from a market with no consolidated tape. Record is defined as an authority published it, which is binary and takes no view on credibility, so a filing made under a regime that enumerates categories the issuer may lawfully withhold, an editorial assessment that legally settles a futures contract, a scraped HTML index, and an 8-K exhibit furnished rather than filed are all Record and all rank above everything. And where an ordering is actually needed, two Records that disagree, it is silent, because they are the same rank. The one useful thing it encodes, do not trust the narration, is a boolean.

Six further limits, each of which forces a change rather than a source.

Values are not bitemporal. The contract is bitemporal for identity and not for values, and that asymmetry is an omission rather than a design choice, because the upstream already implements it: atoms carry valid_from, valid_to and an indexed knowledge_time, with an index whose comment describes an as-of read, and the securities resolver carries an explicit supersession rule over knowledge time. The same discipline has been re-invented by hand at least twice more, in a contract-spec snapshot carrying both a business date and a retrieval timestamp, and in an artifact store's staleness indicator. The twin exposes none of it and has no as-of parameter on the request at all, so a caller cannot ask what the twin would have said on a past date, even though the spine can answer. Partial cannot rescue this, because partial covers a missing slice of a layer, not the temporal validity of a value. This is one problem wearing many costumes, and closing it once at the contract closes macro revisions, restated financials, tape corrections, amended filings, delayed statutory disclosure and chain reorganisations together.

Conflict between authorities is modelled upstream and deliberately hidden from the read path the twin uses. The cockpit implements a conflict side-table keyed on node, claim key and scope, whose own docstring states that supersession within one source must never silently pick a winner across sources, that both source atoms remain stored and immutable, and that resolution is a separate derived reconciliation atom to which the current-atoms projection repoints "so existing one-hop readers stay single-valued and unchanged". The twin is a one-hop reader of that projection. So when two authorities disagree, the twin receives a derived reconciliation atom, and a layer declared Record presents it as Record. That is not an unanticipated gap; it is a live provenance misstatement manufactured by the union layer's own read discipline, and it bites exactly where section 2 says it will: four disagreeing authorities on a US corporate action, five designated venues of equal legal standing in China, two EIA estimators of the same physical quantity, and a tape correction revising a print after the fact. The twin needs a per-value authority reference and a contested state, and a reconciliation atom must never inherit the class of its inputs.

Provenance is declared per layer, and the only live layer already mixes classes. The provenance table assigns volume profile to Derivation while the market layer specification places volume profile inside market quotes and market is Observation, so the live layer cannot honestly declare one class. Drivers is worse and also v0.1, because a level is Record, a year-over-year transform is a Derivation, and a proxy standing in for an underlying is a judgment about substitutability that fits neither, which is presumably why proxies were already segregated into their own array. Layer-level class is a lossy compression that erases exactly the distinction the product sells, on the two layers that ship first. Move the class to the value and keep a derived layer summary naming the weakest class present.

The absence block is a prose control in a repo that has measured prose controls failing. The contract records its own honest limit, that absent-verified and lookup-failed share a field set so swapping them still validates and the distinction is producer-enforced rather than schema-enforced, and the only field carrying why something is absent is free prose. This repo's own operations record states that prose-shaped controls run zero for three while structure-shaped controls run one for one. The model to copy is already published by an authority: GLEIF separates a typed category, which fact is missing, from a typed reason enum for why, including codes for a natural-person parent, no LEI, non-consolidating and non-public, and it carries validity dates on the exception record, so an absence there has a validity era. The twin collapses category and reason into one modality verdict plus prose. The right split is verdict as modality, reason as a typed enum grown deliberately, and basis demoted to a human note no logic reads. On the verdict side, resist growing the enum to nine. The modalities actually needed are: we looked and there is nothing; we could not look; category error; not yet due, carrying the obligation and the date, which also supplies the expiry an absence needs; and withheld, carrying the withholding party and the instrument. Everything else in section 2, prohibited by terms of use, suppressed by statute, bounded by a reporting floor, suppressed by the subject, published but not consolidated, unrecorded by design, is a reason code under one of those five.

Absences also need claim identity. An absence verdict is an assertion about the world with no version, no expiry and no supersession, so it is never re-read and never graded, while the quality standard requires every assertion to be a registered claim with a version identity and every claim to be graded. Absences go retrospectively false on schedules, and the disagreement rate on re-determination is itself a calibration number for the honesty claim: it is the only measurement that makes absence auditable rather than merely virtuous, and it creates a graded outcome stream from the disclosure side, which today contributes nothing to the training record at all.

The authority row is a scalar, carries only extent, and carries no legal state. Three territories break it independently. It must be able to be a set with per-member coverage, because Japan has two authorities with different access classes for one issuer, China designates several venues of equal legal standing, gas pipeline postings are one mandating regulator plus roughly 150 obligated publishers, and stablecoin reserves are one statute plus N issuers. It must carry an effective era, so ESAP can be added as a second authority for the same documents on a known date without rewriting history. It must carry the legal state of the relationship, open, registration-gated, licensed, prohibited or statutorily suppressed, because coverage as extent cannot distinguish an authority that publishes everything from one that publishes what the law permits it to publish. And values and identifier aliases need a redistribution attribute with a serving-time filter, because the platform can hold identifiers it may not restate, and "identifier withheld, licence" is a distinct and honest answer no competitor gives, which the contract currently cannot say. The upstream already carries a licence field on atoms; the twin has no field to receive it.

Two shape problems and one identity problem close this out. Values have shapes the contract does not admit: a distribution-valued driver where percentiles are jointly meaningful, an accounting identity where thirty terms are constrained to sum and the residual is the signal, an event-shaped driver with a calendar rather than a series, a location-keyed price field decomposing into components, and a bounded observation the authority deliberately truncated. Derivations need input-level provenance, because a derivation with one assumed input is materially less trustworthy than one with all inputs observed and today both render identically; the same annotation is forced independently by the missing borrow rate in options analytics, by the sign assumption in dealer positioning, and by block-capped swap notionals, which is evidence that the extension is right rather than local. And entitlement-caused absence is not a fourth verdict but a threat to payload identity: a document that exists and the caller may not see produces a trilemma where shortening the list silently renders partial as populated, saying absent-verified is a false statement about the world, and saying it truthfully discloses the existence of a tenant-private document. No verdict resolves it, because all three existing verdicts are statements about the world or about the lookup and this is a statement about the caller. The deeper cost is that the payload becomes a function of who asked, so one contract and two doors holds across the doors and not across two callers, and the auditability test has no canonical object. The workable split is to make the anonymous public-free payload the canonical auditable artefact and deliver entitlement-gated content as a documented additive overlay, and to state in the contract which artefact a stranger is auditing.

## 5. Where honest absence is worth most, and where it is a liability

The strongest cases are the ones where the market convention is to interpolate silently and the interpolation is invisible. Four stand out.

For most of the bond and municipal universe there is no authority-published price on a given day. The MSRB states in its own concept release that there is no central location in the municipal market through which pre-trade pricing information is made broadly available to the public in a comprehensive manner, and that where pre-trade information exists, access is limited to participants engaging directly with venues. Post-trade, EMMA publishes what traded, and most municipal issues do not trade on a given day, so for the overwhelming majority of the universe the only price in existence is an evaluated or matrix price produced by a private pricing service from comparable-bond models. The SEC's own architecture concedes this, because Rule 2a-5 exists specifically for instruments with no readily available market quotation and requires a fund's board or valuation designee to approve, monitor and evaluate each pricing service used. The regulator's position is that the number is a good-faith determination requiring oversight, while every retail-facing surface renders it as a price. The honest payload is that no authority has published a price since a stated date and the circulating figure is a private party's derivation the platform does not hold, and the fair-value hierarchy already filed per position in N-PORT gives the platform an authority-classified version of its own provenance ladder for free.

Dealer positioning is the clearest case of a derivation sold as data. No authority publishes account-class open interest at the series level, so the sign assumption underneath every gamma exposure product is unobservable, and the only holder of ground truth is the regulator through an audit trail that publishes to nobody. Saying that the formula is F, that the critical input is assumed rather than observed, and that claims will not be graded on it as though it were measured, is a sentence the entire category exists to avoid.

Halted and suspended names are where a silent null is actively dangerous, because during a halt the last print stays on the tape and the reason code is the whole signal. A volatility pause resumes in five minutes and says nothing about the company; a news-pending halt means material information is landing; an exchange-questions halt has no statutory time limit; a regulatory suspension is capped at ten business days and usually terminal. Rendering all of them as no current price, or worse as a stale price with no flag, destroys a distinction that changes the trade, and the feed is free and structured with published resumption times, which yields an absence with an authority-published end time. That is the strongest form of honest absence available anywhere: not "we do not know" but "nobody may know until 16:00, by the exchange's own notice".

And there is an entitlement-caused absence imposed by law rather than by contract. Under the amended Rule 15c2-11, where an issuer lacks current public information, quotes may be published only as unsolicited quotes distributed to broker-dealers, institutions and accredited investors, so a retail-facing caller legitimately cannot be shown a quote that demonstrably exists, and the cause is neither a vendor licence, nor a missing record, nor a lookup failure. That is a fourth cause, and the remediation differs completely by cause: a licence is a procurement decision with a named counterparty and a price, an entitlement tier is an upsell path, and a statute is permanently unavailable to that reader class no matter how much money is spent. Collapsing the three tells an operator nothing actionable.

Now the liability, stated plainly because it is not hypothetical. The twin today serves 250 bars, an entitlement-bounded room, and three stated absences. That is a page whose majority is a well-reasoned account of what it does not have. The failure mode is not that the platform refuses too much; it is that an absence is cheap to emit and expensive to read. One field for the system, a paragraph for the reader. Left unmanaged, information density falls as honesty rises, which is exactly the inversion that lets a less honest competitor with broader coverage win the demo. The second liability is subtler: refusal is not free of error, absent-verified is an affirmative claim, and a system that refuses confidently and never re-checks has relocated its dishonesty from the value to the verdict while presenting it with the product's full moral authority. That is worse than a missing field, because the reader trusts it more.

Three disciplines follow. Measure the served-answer ratio per shape, the fraction of layers returning content rather than a verdict, and use it as a ship gate rather than as a feeling. Require every absence to be actionable or silent: an absence earns its space only if it names who would close it and how, with an authority, an access class, and where known a date or a price, and an absence that cannot name its closer should collapse to a compact omission rather than an essay. And give absences version identity, expiry and re-verification, so that a stated absence older than its re-check interval is downgraded rather than left standing as a verified finding.

On who pays for this, the ranking matters because it decides which door gets the investment. The buyer is the agent operator. A human reading an empty panel infers that the platform does not have it and moves on, at the cost of disappointment. An agent reading an empty field has no such prior and will fill the gap from parametric memory, producing a confident sourceless assertion its operator carries. For that buyer the typed distinction between absent, not-applicable, withheld and not-yet-due is the difference between a tool that can be put in a loop and one that cannot. The second buyer is the valuation and model-risk function, which under Rule 2a-5 needs a defensible dated record of which inputs were observed and which were modelled, which is precisely the observed-versus-assumed distinction the provenance model cannot yet express. The third is compliance around licensed identifiers and restricted quotes, where "we hold this and may not restate it" has a dollar value. Retail is not a buyer for absence discipline: a retail user pays for an answer and every refusal is a cost to them. So the absence discipline pays on the MCP door, where the verdict taxonomy and cause codes have to sit in the tool's declared output schema for an agent to branch on cause without parsing prose, and costs on the web door unless the default there is content-first with absences collapsed behind one honest summary line. Same payload, different render, which is what one contract and two doors is for.

## 6. The case against

Not softened.

The arithmetic kills the record before the methodology does. Grading started in July 2026, so at the date of this report the maximum number of distinct market days available to any claim in the entire programme is about 33, and market days are correlated, so the effective independent sample across the whole platform is a few dozen observations of the market's state. Against that sits a hypothesis count that reaches the hundreds once families, variants and parameter grids are counted, and it is not stored. Under any honest multiple-comparisons correction nothing in this record clears, and no amount of engineering changes that, because the binding resource accrues at exactly one day per day. The framing "a competitor who starts grading tomorrow starts at zero, and we started 2026-07" inverts the truth: the platform started at zero six weeks ago, a competitor starting tomorrow is six weeks behind, and six weeks is a rounding error against a requirement of several hundred independent days. It will still be a rounding error in a year. There is a sharper form: the denominator is unknowable in principle, not merely uncounted, because the choice of which hypotheses to register at all is a selection made by a designer whose intuitions are already a fitted model of decades of charts. Pre-registration protects against post-hoc selection within a claim; it does not protect against a search the market already ran.

The one number cited as evidence of edge is gross and produced by a fill model maximally favourable to that exact strategy. The zone grader contains no slippage, commission, spread or friction term anywhere, so the flagship result is gross of all costs. The paper engine fills a resting limit as soon as the last price crosses it, with no queue position, no volume check and no partial fills, which removes adverse selection from the order type most exposed to it, since in reality you fill when someone crosses to you and that happens disproportionately when the market is about to continue through you. And the friction that does exist is a constant basis-point figure independent of order size, so capacity is entirely unmodelled and a real edge cannot be distinguished from one that exists only below the size at which anyone would care. The reputational exposure is asymmetric: the first external technical reader will grep the grader for a cost term, find none, and conclude that the platform built on refusing to fabricate has been publishing a gross number as its flagship result. That conclusion would be unfair to the intent and entirely fair to the artefact.

The refusal statistic is uninterpretable without nulls. A gate refusing 20 of 21 buckets is impressive if the buckets were good candidates and the gate is discriminating, and unremarkable if the generator emits mostly noise, in which case any threshold refuses about that share of a null distribution. The two supporting numbers in the same document set point the wrong way: doctrine-conformant agreement at 3.25 percent, four events in 123, and the one bot with a real forward record at negative expectancy over 372 trades. That is a perfectly honest place to be six weeks in, and it means the refusal rate cannot be spent as proof of rigour. Running every family against matched random walks, block-bootstrapped bars and sign-flipped or time-reversed series, and reporting the null distribution beside every result, is described inside the programme's own review notes as a weekend of work and has not been done. Until it is, every number the platform publishes, including the refusals, is uninterpretable.

The position "willing to publish losses" is occupied, with depth. Numerai has run timestamped, encrypted, pre-submission predictions graded by a third party since December 2015, with public per-model performance, 30,000-plus participants and 1,200-plus staked models feeding a weekly meta model, and participants stake tokens that are burned on bad predictions. Staking is a strictly stronger commitment device than willingness, because publishing a loss costs an afternoon's discomfort and burning a stake costs money and is enforced by contract rather than by editorial character. Numerai engineered the willingness out of the trust equation entirely, which is the more durable solution. TipRanks makes the same point from distribution, having published per-analyst hit rates including the bad ones inside retail brokerage platforms for over a decade. So "we grade and they do not" is refuted by two live products. What is genuinely differentiated is provenance to the authority on the inputs, which Numerai deliberately does not have because its features are obfuscated, and honest absence as a served contract, which neither does.

Wing A is being built against a public good. The Open Source Asset Pricing dataset reproduces 212 published cross-sectional return predictors with full replication code, portfolio implementations and out-of-sample statistics, free, peer-reviewed, with a measured column spanning decades rather than six weeks. Wing A's first-pass scope is 15 to 20 entries. That does not make it worthless, because it is the vocabulary L3 needs and a codex tied to this codebase's actual implementations has real internal value, but it makes its external value roughly zero and the roadmap should not be defended on it. The second-order sting is sharper: that replication literature plus the publication-decay literature already answer the question the whole L1 and L2 programme is designed to ask. The signals are real, and publishing them destroys most of their value. The platform intends to publish, at enormous cost and tiny sample size, an experiment whose likely conclusion has been in the literature for a decade, and then to publish the result in a way that decays it.

Three of the four assets described as un-retrofittable are not. Point-in-time non-price data is a commercial product category, not an un-purchasable asset: vintage-aware fundamentals archives are sold, macro vintages are free from ALFRED and the Philadelphia Fed, DERA publishes as-filed statement data from 2009, and EDGAR acceptance timestamps give the knowledge time of every filing. A competitor can reconstruct what was knowable on a past date, which is the exact property claimed as unbuyable, and the platform is currently on the wrong side of it because of the FRED parameter leak. Anchored provenance is unrun in production, and even when run it proves that a claim existed at a time, not that it was the only claim; anchoring becomes an audit primitive only when the complete registry including the denominator is anchored, which is not built. And blind operator marks, the deepest claim, are probably retrofittable. The protocol requires that the marker cannot see the future of the window, not that the window's future has not yet happened in the world. A 2017 window, price-rescaled, ticker-stripped, date-stripped, shown to a trained marker today produces a mark made in genuine ignorance, and if that holds then the moat is a labelling budget: fifty labellers on de-identified historical windows produce in a month what one operator at a sustainable weekly quota produces in years, stratified deliberately rather than accumulated by accident, and with inter-rater reliability the current design can never have. This is marked as inference rather than measurement, and it is cheap to falsify: 200 blind historical marks against 200 live marks, one sprint, and the answer reorders the roadmap either way.

The two-application structure is a regulatory liability that nobody has priced. Smart Markets is impersonal, general, disclosure-shaped and identical for every caller, which makes it a strong publisher's-exclusion candidate on all three prongs of the governing case law. The cockpit is not: it holds the user's positions and orders, runs bots against them and surfaces claims into that context, which is advice adapted to a specific portfolio, and the moment it is charged for, the compensation element is satisfied too. The consequence is not merely that registration might be required; registration inverts the moat, because the entire graded record is paper output, which is model performance applied to periods in which the strategy was not actually traded, and that is hypothetical performance under the marketing rule. Hypothetical performance may not be handed to a mass-market audience and requires audience-appropriateness policies, so the customer-facing record becomes the most compliance-encumbered feature in the product. Eleven futures roots plus advice for compensation put commodity trading advisor registration in scope, where the publisher exclusion is narrower and the self-regulatory rulebook then dictates the presentation, including a prescribed disclaimer for hypothetical results and a bar on using them once three months of actual results exist. "We publish our losses in our own words" collides with a rulebook that specifies the words. This is a one-memo question and the answer determines the product architecture.

Redistribution is unlicensed. Naming the publisher of record is an epistemics doctrine about provenance and it says nothing about the right to retransmit. Serving bars to third-party callers through an MCP door is redistribution under any market data plan's ordinary meaning, and simultaneously non-display use because the consumer is an agent rather than a screen, and both are chargeable categories with vendor agreements behind them. The identity layer has the same hole one level down, where the free legs are FIGI, LEI and MIC and the licensed legs are ISIN, CUSIP and SEDOL. So the doctrine is one field short of correct across an entire dimension of the business, and this is the kind of exposure that surfaces as a letter after the product succeeds.

Finally, regime decay races accrual and the platform is on the wrong side. If a record is only valid inside the regime composition that produced it, then its useful life is the life of that regime, while the time to fill an envelope to significance is the time to accumulate several hundred independent days inside it. Volatility regimes turn over on a timescale of months to a couple of years. It is entirely possible, and nobody has checked, that the envelope expires before it fills, in which case the record never reaches significance in any envelope for which it is still valid, and the moat is a bucket with a hole in it. That also resolves the competitive question unfavourably: a competitor does not need to catch up on elapsed time, only to reach the same steady-state depth, which is bounded by the decay rate rather than by the start date. A moat capped by decay is a treadmill.

What would kill the thesis, stated as falsifiable propositions:

1. Blind historical marking is indistinguishable from live marking. If a trained marker shown a de-identified, rescaled, date-stripped historical window produces marks whose distribution and graded outcomes match contemporaneous marks, then the deepest claimed asset is a purchase order, and combined with purchasable point-in-time archives and reconstructible price-derived vocabulary, nothing irreproducible remains except a six-week ordering record. Test: 200 blind against 200 live, one sprint.
2. The validity envelope expires before it fills. If time-to-significance inside a fixed volatility regime exceeds that regime's empirical duration, no family can ever be promoted on a valid record and the path from L2 to L3 is arithmetically closed regardless of engineering quality. This is the most dangerous of the three because it is invisible until the last gate and it invalidates the plan rather than delaying it. Test: two days with the public predictor dataset, computing half-lives and implied time-to-significance for signals of matched construction.
3. Nobody pays for calibration. If the buyer wants answers rather than confidence intervals, and the retail trading market's revealed preference over thirty years says they do, then honest absence is an acquisition liability and the loop is a cost centre attached to a product competing on breadth. Test: sell it. One paying customer who bought because of an absence verdict and can say why settles it.

What would make the case for it: one number that has survived a null distribution, benchmark subtraction, realistic cost and fill modelling, cluster-robust days-not-rows accounting across both axes, and a symbol holdout declared and hashed before results existed, with the hypothesis ledger denominator printed beside it. One such number is worth more than the entire current record. Then a published reliability diagram, because calibration is estimable at the sample size that actually exists and edge is not, so "when we say 60 percent it is 60 percent" is a falsifiable claim available this quarter. Then a securities and commodities memo separating publication from advice across the two applications, dated before pricing. And three months of actual trading in a real account at any size, which converts the record out of hypothetical performance under both regimes and is the highest-leverage credibility act available: a ten thousand dollar live account started this quarter is worth more to the moat than a quarter of paper soak.

The honest reframe, if the arithmetic holds, is that the self-generated record is not a training corpus and calling it one invites a comparison the platform loses badly. Set 2,300 outcomes from one operator's detectors over one regime against a broker's fill archive, an exchange's order book, or ten years of out-of-sample predictions from 30,000 modellers. It is also self-selected, because outcomes exist only for events the platform's own detectors chose to emit, so the label distribution is a function of the generator's biases rather than of the market. The better claim is that it is a benchmark: a small, meticulously provenance-controlled, hindsight-free evaluation set with anchored ordering. Benchmarks are valuable precisely because they are small and clean, and a credible financial-claims benchmark is currently unserved because everyone who could build one has an incentive not to. That is a smaller business and a far more defensible one, it is compatible with everything already built, and it gives Smart Markets a second buyer: anyone who needs to prove their own model was not trained on hindsight.
