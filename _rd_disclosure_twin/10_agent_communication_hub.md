# The agent communication hub

> **Promoted 2026-08-16 to the durable stub thesis at `42b_stub_thesis_agent_communication_hub.md`**, which carries the honest unique-vs-not split, the focus ruling (frame, not build - current projects proceed unmodified), and the revisit protocol. This capture remains as the origin record; the thesis doc governs.

## The founding parable (why this concept exists)

Cotality is being extinguished across the portfolio - and the root cause was never the data. It was communication: an operator who could not communicate clearly with the vendor, a vendor that did not know how to work with AI, and an AI that needed information the vendor's humans were never able to provide. The relationship died of information asymmetry and channel failure. **In a world with an agent communication hub - shared data-room access, agents able to work together and communicate with each other - that relationship survives**: our agent asks their agent, their agent answers from authorized rooms with provenance, humans watch or join when judgment is needed. The migration cost, the weeks of failed emails, the guessing - none of it happens.

## The concept

Every organization exposes an **agent endpoint**, grounded in its data rooms and twins, authenticated per counterparty. Between organizations, the hub carries:

1. **Agent-to-agent communication**: my agent asks yours a question or requests a document/feed; yours answers from what my access passes authorize - with provenance on every answer and honest absence where access or knowledge ends.
2. **Data-room-grounded truth**: the conversation is never ungrounded chat between models; every substantive answer resolves against rooms (Smart Files) and twins. Communication with receipts.
3. **The human door, first-class**: Nick's named break - "for a human, I don't know how to interact with an MCP server." A person should be able to ask a counterparty a question in plain language and get the counterparty's agent's answer, in an interface a human actually uses. The two-door rule (agent door + human door over the same substrate) applies to communication itself.
4. **The auditable channel**: every exchange rides the event chain - who asked, who answered, from what source, under which pass. B2B communication that is itself a provenance-carrying record. Disputes, compliance, and "what did they tell us and when" become queries, not archaeology.
5. **Metering where it earns**: some answers are free relationship-maintenance; some are paid data delivery - the obligation/metering rail already runs this pattern.

## The two breaks it fixes

1. **Inter-organizational agent communication is unowned.** MCP standardized agent-to-tool. Nothing owns authenticated, room-grounded, auditable agent-to-agent communication BETWEEN companies - the B2B channel where vendor relationships, support, procurement, due diligence, and integrations actually live. (Protocol landscape - MCP-composed vs A2A-class protocols - is a research item for the landscape pass; posture likely: compose MCP, adopt what wins, own the grounded-and-audited layer either way.)
2. **The human-MCP break.** Agents get a clean machine door; humans get nothing legible. The hub's human surface - ask, see the answer with its receipts, escalate to a human on the other side - is as much the product as the protocol.

## How the in-flight work instantiates it

The research funnel (docs 05-09) is **instance one**: financial agents come into the MCP server and get full-depth company research - that is the hub in read-only, public-data form, with the claim motion adding the issuer's own agent-side later. The Bulls platform is **instance two in embryo**: team, league, vendors, and sponsors all needing exactly this channel (the financial umbrella's actor matrix is a hub roster). The Disclosure Twin's claimed-issuer tier is the moment a counterparty agent first ANSWERS through us rather than just being read - the pivot from library to hub.
