# SmartCity product positioning and collateral

> **Purpose.** One shared positioning + collateral pack for the SmartCity product line, drawn from by everyone who sells it to government: **Forrest** (city-domain SME and sales rep, onboarding per `80_meetings/transcripts/2026-05-forest_forrest_consulting_call_otter.txt`) and **Vertosoft** (the contract-vehicle distributor, [`_prospects/vertosoft/2026-06-07_vertosoft_channel_and_offer.md`](_prospects/vertosoft/2026-06-07_vertosoft_channel_and_offer.md)). Forrest also feeds product development from the city side. Grounded in the live Bastrop deployment.

## The buyer and the rule

Buyer: small-to-medium municipalities, the overlooked middle that Palantir-scale tools skip. The gov buyer cares about the problem solved, not the technology. **Sell use-case-first** and keep the substrate/blockchain/calibration plumbing out of the pitch (the same lesson from Bastrop: Sylvia could not act on "blockchain" until it was reframed as what it does for her). Vertosoft's own advice is identical.

## The one-liner

SmartCity OS: one pane of glass to run your city. See everything about any property in a single click, run plan review the way the state will, and get ahead of problems instead of reacting to them. Manage the city from your phone.

## The four products, use-case-first

1. **Dashboard (one pane of glass).** Problem: six departments, a dozen disconnected systems, no single view of the city. Solution: every system pulled into one purview; proactive instead of reactive; usable from a phone. Proof: Bastrop, Sylvia running the city day to day.
2. **Plan review (Codex).** Problem: review is slow, inconsistent, and resubmission-heavy. Solution: run the same review the authority will run, cited to the code, so submittals come back clean. Proof: Codex plan review at Bastrop.
3. **Parcel / property intelligence.** Problem: answering "what can happen on this parcel" or "what if it rains four inches in 24 hours" means digging across GIS, FEMA, zoning, and permit history. Solution: click a parcel, see everything through it, with a cited flood/hydrology answer. Proof: the four-inches-of-rain demo.
4. **Ambient capture extension (the new one).** Problem: the city's decisions and institutional knowledge are lost after the work is done. Solution: assist staff in the moment across the tools they already use, and capture each decision as the city's own intelligence so the platform gets sharper the more the team uses it. Positioned as "it learns your city," not as a calibration engine. This is the deposit layer that makes everything else compound; bundle it into SmartCity OS rather than price it separately.

## The grant angle

Cities will go through a procurement process for grant-funded programs (data redundancy, resilience, modernization), and the stack supports grant narratives. Position SmartCity as grant-supporting and fundable; it lowers the buyer's cost barrier and gives the rep a reason-to-act.

## Competitive frame

Closest analog is Palantir, but that is enterprise/big-government; SmartCity targets the small-to-medium municipalities Palantir overlooks. Not OpenGov (a point solution). Differentiator: one AI-native substrate, the city owns its data, and the platform compounds on the city's own outcomes.

## Collateral production (scope and sequence)

Production is a separate build, scoped here and held until fired. It mirrors the Mox collateral build (standalone branded HTML plus a walkthrough deck plus an executive summary), SmartCity/gov-branded and use-case-first. The Bastrop advantage over Mox is that the deployment is live, so the assets use real screenshots and real metrics, not placeholder mockups. Dispatch scaffolded at [`_dispatches/2026-06-07_smartcity_collateral_production_QUEUED.md`](_dispatches/2026-06-07_smartcity_collateral_production_QUEUED.md), status QUEUED.

Sequence, gated by what is operator-supplied:

1. **Pricing-independent assets first.** The overview deck (use-case-first, the `Mox_Intelligence_Overview` analog), the four per-product one-pagers (dashboard, plan review, parcel intelligence, ambient extension), and the Bastrop case-study one-pager (the lead asset, built from real Bastrop screenshots). None of these need the pricing decision. Bastrop case-study metrics must be real and source-attributed per the quality gate; do not invent numbers.
2. **Pricing slide and channel-partner pricing second.** The deck's pricing slide and the channel pack's pricing tiers are gated on the government pricing tiers / list price (operator decision in the Vertosoft offer doc). Hold these until the price is set.
3. **Channel-partner pack** for Forrest and Vertosoft: positioning, problem statements, contract-vehicle list, and the how-to-position cheat sheet. The non-pricing portions ship with step 1; the pricing tier table slots in at step 2.

Keep the substrate / calibration / revenue-share plumbing out of the gov pitch (use-case-first, per Vertosoft's own advice and I7). The pricing model is a Layer 3 integrated-product sale to cities, distinct from the Layer 1/2 substrate model.
