THE INVARIANT: every reader of cad_property filters to the county's single DECLARED vintage. No reader
mixes vintages within one county in one derivation; no reader falls back to another vintage silently
(a gap is an honest `vintage-gap` absence).

1. Registry fields: per-county `current_tax_year` + `current_tier` in
   _catalog/tx_cad_source_registry.json (doc_repo edit, uncommitted — planner commits). Flipped only
   at load completion, never mid-load. Seed current values from store truth (the 15 CAD counties are
   2025/cad-export or stratmap per their actual rows — derive by SQL, do not guess).
2. ONE blessed vintage-resolver helper (authored where cad-ingest lives: legacy-design-tools
   lib/cad-ingest) that resolves a county's declared vintage; every cad_property reader imports it.
3. Reader swaps: every query touching cad_property gains the vintage predicate via the helper —
   inventory readers at source across BOTH repos (ldt cad-ingest/scorers/facets AND the hauska-engine
   fact writers owner/land-use/cad-parcel-roll that join cad_property). Engine-side changes are a
   separate small PR; those writers are NOT in L16's file set, but do NOT touch
   special-district/rail/well/footprint writer files (L16's).
4. Fail-closed: declared vintage yielding zero rows for a prop that exists in another vintage emits
   `vintage-gap` absence basis — never a silent cross-vintage read. Failing-first test for exactly
   that case.
5. CI hook: `ci-vintage-predicate` grep — any cad_property query not going through the blessed helper
   fails CI (mirror the L5 grep-check pattern already live in ldt).
