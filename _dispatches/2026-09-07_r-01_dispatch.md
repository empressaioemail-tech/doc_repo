### Why this lane exists

Four report products (Site Plan, X-Ray, Feasibility, Flood and Drainage) each
have their own assembler, and they re-derive the same facts independently.
That is not a theory: on 2026-09-07 a parcel narrower than 16m produced no
site plan, no X-Ray and no Feasibility at all, because Flood and Drainage had
already learned to pad its DEM window and site-plan never inherited the
lesson. Two call sites, one lesson, one outage. Separately, X-Ray's facts come
from a completely different system (hauska-map computes them and the engine
prints them verbatim), so the two customer documents about one parcel can and
do disagree.

You are removing the second derivation, not reconciling it. Do not build a
bridge between models and do not build a parity checker that compares two
derivations. The operator's ruling on 2026-09-07 was explicit that the
program had drifted into exactly that kind of scaffolding.

### Scope

`packages/engine-core/src/site-plan/` and its tests. Plus the engine-api
route wiring in `services/engine-api/src/routes/parcel-terrain.ts` where a
product entry point changes.

R1. `composeParcelReport(parcelNodeId, options)`: ONE read of the parcel, ONE
geometry composition, ONE resolution of every fact family, returning a model
where every family carries its own present/absent state with provenance.
Today three of the four products already call `composeSitePlanModelForParcel`,
so most of this is promotion of existing structure, not new invention.

R2. Section-level failure isolation. A section that cannot compose renders as
a declared absence; it never fails the document. Today Feasibility fails
closed when the site plan fails, which is precisely how one small parcel took
down three products at once.

R3. Delete `floodStudyAvailable`. It is a caller-supplied boolean written
straight into the model as `studyAvailable`; Feasibility never reads a study,
never runs one, never verifies one exists. The "drainage study not on file"
line a customer sees means the caller did not pass a flag. Composition reads
the real persisted study, runs it when stale, and declares honest absence when
there is none. Removing the field from the type is the control; nothing else
is.

R4. A manifest type that names sections and CANNOT carry fact values. This is
the whole control for the disagreement class, and it is a type rather than a
check on purpose: a product physically cannot supply its own version of a
fact, so the failure mode is unreachable rather than policed. Verify it by
writing code that tries to supply a fact value and confirming the compiler
refuses.

R5. Feasibility renders from a manifest over the composed model, including the
real drainage study and the package layer (narrative, open items, verdict,
data quality) computed across all sections rather than one product's slice.
