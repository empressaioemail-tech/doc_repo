# Stub thesis: the agent communication hub

## The concept

Every organization is putting AI on its side of the table; between organizations, business still runs on email threads and PDF attachments. The hub is the missing layer: **authenticated agent-to-agent communication between companies, grounded in shared data-room access, with a first-class human door** - your agent asks their agent; theirs answers from what your access passes authorize; every answer carries provenance; honest absence states where access or knowledge ends; every exchange rides an event chain so "what did they tell us and when" is a query, not a dispute; humans ask in plain language and join when judgment is needed; metering prices the answers that are worth money.

## The founding parable

Cotality was extinguished across the portfolio not because its data was bad but because the channel was broken: an operator who could not communicate clearly with the vendor, a vendor that could not work with AI, an AI needing information the vendor's humans could never produce. The relationship died of information asymmetry. With a hub - shared rooms, agents conversing, humans escalating - it survives. This failure mode is about to be every B2B relationship in the world.

## What is unique, and what is not (the honest split)

**Not unique:** the channel. Agent-to-agent transport is being standardized by the platforms (MCP for agent-to-tool; A2A-class protocols for agent-to-agent; the collaboration incumbents will ship "your agent talks to theirs" natively). Competing on transport loses to distribution we do not have.

**Unique and ours:** the grounding and the record. Room-grounded answers with provenance, honest absence with stated scope, the conversation itself as an auditable provenance-carrying artifact, pass-governed access, metered answers. Transport is capital-solvable; verified grounding is time-solvable - the bottleneck asymmetry. **Position: protocol-agnostic; build the grounded, audited endpoint that speaks whatever channel wins.**

**The thesis symmetry:** the manifesto's three agent questions are who am I dealing with, what is this thing really, how do I pay. The referent layer answers question two. The hub is question one's infrastructure. The rails answer question three. The portfolio is quietly assembling all three.

## The focus ruling (Nick, 2026-08-16 - the operative decision)

**The hub is a frame, not a build.** The portfolio's current projects are already roughly hub-shaped and proceed exactly as planned, unmodified: the securities research funnel (an agent hub in read-only form; the claimed-issuer motion converts readers into answerers), the trading-application extension, the Bulls platform (team, league, vendors, sponsors - a captive network where both sides of the channel are controllable), Smart Files rooms and passes (the grounding substrate), the gate (auth, tenancy, metering). No staffing, no product line, no detour. The two-sided-network cold-start problem is not fought head-on; the hub emerges from running instances where we control both sides, or it does not emerge at all. The concept's best version: in time it is not a product that launched but the name for what customers discover they have already been using.

## Revisit protocol (why this document exists)

When Nick returns to this thought track, check, in order:

1. **Did the instances converge on the shape?** Are the funnel, the Bulls platform, and the trading extension growing counterparty-answering surfaces on their own demand (issuers claiming twins, vendors answering through the Bulls channel, counterparties querying the cockpit's data)? Convergence = the frame was right; promote deliberately. No convergence = record why and adjust the thesis.
2. **Did the protocol wars settle?** Which agent-to-agent transport won or consolidated; whether the winner shipped grounding/audit natively (if so, the unique layer thinned - reassess) or left it open (if so, the endpoint position strengthened).
3. **Did anyone else build the grounded/audited layer?** The competitive check on the only part that is ours.
4. **Did a customer ask for it by name?** A counterparty requesting "let our agent talk to yours" unprompted is the promotion trigger - a real buyer beats every plan.
5. Then either: promote to a build (own decision record, named queue casualties per the focus-queue rule), keep as frame, or retire with reasons - and update this doc's status accordingly.

## Reversal criteria

Retire this thesis if: the platforms ship grounded-and-audited inter-org agent communication natively (the unique layer evaporates), or two revisits pass with no instance convergence and no customer pull. Promote it if trigger 4 fires or three instances converge unprompted.
