Two open questions carried from v1. (1) Does an acquired-but-unatomizable rail count? Parcel geometry is verified in StratMap for 253 of 254 counties, but has no atom family, so this mockup shows it no-atom everywhere and grants it zero credit. Counting it instead would move the Texas number by roughly an order of magnitude. The mockup takes the strict reading: if the contract cannot hold the finding, the finding does not count. (2) PARTIAL is excluded from the score here. Per ruling 3 only SATISFIED counts, so Williamson zoning at 33.98 percent against a 95 percent threshold renders with a partial underline and coverage number but adds nothing to the county total.

Grid renders seeded counties of 254. The remaining exist in the rollup arithmetic only and are uniformly not-yet. Threshold values are the ruling's tuning start (95 percent spine / 90 percent derived), not measured.
