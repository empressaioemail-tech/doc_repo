## Vision 1: land administration system of record

A platform for the land department of an independent oil and gas operator. The pain it targets: land teams track millions of dollars in obligations (delay rentals, lease renewals, bonus payments) across spreadsheets and broken document links, and a single missed obligation can terminate a producing lease. The product centralizes agreements (right-of-way, surface and mineral leases, easements), tracks obligations on a deadline rail, and adds secure data rooms for third-party diligence. It positions as sitting on top of the industry system of record (Quorum Land Suite) and replacing the legacy document layer (OpenText), repairing the broken link between the record and the documents.

The essence that survives: an AI that returns structured, interactive output where every claim is provenance-backed and every real-world action requires explicit confirmation. That is our four structural commitments rendered as a product surface.

## Vision 2: asset marketplace

A two-sided marketplace for buying and selling oil and gas assets: mineral rights, working interests, royalty and override interests, and asset packages. Free listings for individual mineral owners, transaction fee at closing, subscription tiers for brokers and operators. Powered by an industry data integration (Enverus). Headline features: automated title run sheets (replacing 6 to 12 month courthouse title searches), AI deal scoring, virtual data rooms, and instant multi-party settlement.

The essence that survives: title chain as a reasoning product (not a data dump), deal scoring with confidence, and settlement that our payment substrate can now actually perform.

## Vision 3: Western Midstream as the named prospect

A specific midstream operator (gathering, processing, pipelines, produced water) that the land-administration and data-room angle was pitched to. The technical thread was replacing OpenText, integrating with the Quorum land suite, and standing up secure data rooms. Because WES is midstream, the marketplace (an upstream asset motion) is not the fit; the land, right-of-way, easement, and produced-water-permitting surface is.

## What is stale and should not be carried forward uncritically

- Specific tier prices, accuracy percentages, and market-size figures in the prior docs are unverified marketing numbers. Do not repeat as fact.
- The marketplace's blockchain-settlement and tokenization framing came before our payment substrate existed. Reconcile against the real Hauska SDK state rather than the 2025 framing.
- Product naming ("Empressa Land", "Empressa Marketplace") is pre-canon. See the naming flag in `00_oil_gas_index.md`.
- "Powered by Enverus" was the prior data dependency. Our spine sources differently (Cotality, county records, federal and state public data). Whether Enverus is a partner, a replaced dependency, or irrelevant is an open question, not a settled input.
